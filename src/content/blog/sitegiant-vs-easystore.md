## Verdict

Comparing these two website builders, you can see there are some areas where they offer similar features. Here’s what you need to know about each of them and which website builder comes out on top.

### Pricing: EasyStore

Currently, if you check SiteGiant pricing, the fees are on sale, and it can be as low as RM62 per month. This makes SiteGiant pretty affordable, had it not been for one major issue: they only offer yearly plans.

This means you can’t pay per month, you will have to pay RM744 straight off. This may be a problem for beginners who are uncertain if they will use their SiteGiant plan for a full year.

In comparison, EasyStore offers both monthly and annual payments. Even their lowest annual e-commerce payment plan is low at RM59 per month, making choosing it the more affordable option if you do choose to pay annually.

### 

### Free Trial: EasyStore

SiteGiant does not have any trial option that you can sign up for easily, but you can request a demo. With EasyStore, you can access a trial in just a few clicks and even gain access to a free online store and point of sale. You don’t even need to input your credit card details until you’re ready to upgrade your account.

### Payment & Logistics: EasyStore

![Malaysia E-Commerce Payment and Logistics | SiteGiant VS Easystore | One Search Pro Marketing](/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/word-image-41576-8.png)

While SiteGiant has the basic and important shipping and payment options, it’s quite limited and focuses a lot on the Malaysian market. EasyStore has more options in both payments and the other order fulfillment logistics, as they have offices in various other countries.

### 

### Support: SiteGiant

SiteGiant is well-known for its support and customer service, so should you ever have any issues, you can depend on them. There’s even an online chat support on their website where even as a casual viewer, you can ask questions.

While EasyStore does offer customer service, it’s not as easy to find. A quick check on their website also shows they’re not easy to contact unless you sign up.

### Transaction Fee: Tie

Both SiteGiant and EasyStore charge a zero transaction fee.

### Training & Learning: SiteGiant

EasyStore does offer weekly training, and they have an academy and blog section on their website to continue their training.

However, SiteGiant’s offerings are more in this regard, as they provide both online and offline training, webinars, and even workshops for their customers to participate in and provide a more hands-on approach, providing a more hands-on **[guide to starting a successful e-commerce business](https://onesearchpro.my/guide-on-how-to-start-a-successful-e-commerce-business/)**.

### 

### Marketplace Syncing: SiteGiant

![Marketplace Sync with Website Builder | SiteGiant VS Easystore | One Search Pro Marketing](/wp-content/uploads/2023/03/word-image-41576-9.png)

Currently, EasyStore can only do marketplace sync with three marketplaces; Lazada, Shopee, and Zalora. SiteGiant has more with six more marketplaces, including PGMall, YouBeli, SubPlace, Wow Shop, and more.

The only downside is that you will need to subscribe to the Webstore Pro plan and the ERP plan on SiteGiant to have access to more marketplace syncing.

### 

### Free Domain Name: Tie

Both SiteGiant and EasyStore offer free domain names, but each with specific requirements. For SiteGiant, it comes free with their web hosting plan. For EasyStore, you will need to subscribe to their annual plans.

## Which Platform Should You Go For?

### Pick Sitegiant if…

You’re looking to expand your current business further through a platform that helps integrate multiple marketplaces and everything together. You’re also looking for better support in this expanding goal, as SiteGiant’s customer service and training make this possible.

Also, if your customer base is mainly in Malaysia, SiteGiant’s payment gateways, shipping, and logistics make it ideal for reaching local customers better.

### 

### Pick EasyStore if…

You’re a beginner in e-commerce and looking to start out a website builder with less hassle and risk-free, as EasyStore makes it possible to try out the services without needing your credit card details. You’re also looking for a more affordable type of website builder in the market that lets you pay per month.

If you’re targeting various Asian countries, particularly Singapore, Taiwan, and Thailand, EasyStore offers payment gateways as well as shipping dedicated to these various countries. This makes it ideal if you want to expand your reach and sales.

In the end, it depends on which website builder features to suit your needs, especially as an online business. Understanding your target audience, your products, and how to market them are all key to knowing which website builder fits what you’re looking for in terms of features and budget.
