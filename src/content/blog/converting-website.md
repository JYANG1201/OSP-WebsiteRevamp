## What is Website Conversion?

The purpose of building a website is to serve an intended function. The functions can be to sign up, place orders, share content, convey data/ideas, etc.

Website conversions, in simple terms, are the actualization of these demands by the visitors who land on your website. It is when your website performs its desired action.

When a visitor browses your website, they perform actions such as signing up on a sign-up form, subscribing to your services, ordering products, and leaving comments.

These are examples of the goals set for different websites, better known as the desired action. Website conversion has two categories: **micro and macro-conversions.**

### Micro-Conversions

These are small actions in a website that lead to a macro-conversion. Micro does not affect/increase website conversion rate, but it gives valuable insights into the site.

It is an important element for businesses, and you can use them to tweak the site. Examples may include:

*   A user is browsing the internet on various brands.
*   Video watching and content sharing.
*   Feedback by users.
*   Content downloads like a newsletter, article, images, or blogs.
*   Email sign-ups plus an address.
*   Sign up form.

### Macro-Conversion

Macro-conversion describes the main desired action in a site, achieving the website’s primary goal. The best examples of macro goals in software are:

*   A new subscriber.
*   Purchased goods.
*   Service requests.
*   Contact form.
*   Submit sign-up forms.
*   Fill a form.

## How to Calculate Website Conversion Rate

Website conversion rate is the rate at which a website performs its desired actions. Before calculating, you have to review the number of visitors to your website and the number of website conversions.

### How do you calculate your business’s rate?

Conversion rate = number of conversions / total number of visitors × 100.

![](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/Learning-how-to-calculate-a-website-conversion-rate-is-simple..jpg)

Learning how to calculate a website conversion rate is simple. Source: [](https://sendpulse.com/blog/sales-conversion-rate)**[Send Pulse](https://sendpulse.com/blog/sales-conversion-rate)**

A great example of a case study: a website has 200000 visitors and 1000 conversions. So the conversion percentage is;

1000/200000 × 100 = 0.5%

## Reasons for Measuring Your Website Conversion Rate

Case studies show that a host knowing their website conversion rates help in optimizing businesses. [](https://www.optimizely.com/optimization-glossary/conversion-rate-optimization/)**[\[1\]](https://www.optimizely.com/optimization-glossary/conversion-rate-optimization/)** There are two significant reasons you should measure your website conversion rates: understanding your customer and understanding the user experience.

So how can you achieve these two? Simple, by getting conversant with the sales funnel in terms of marketing and conversion rate optimization.

### Sales Funnel

Sales funnels are the steps a customer takes for a web site conversion. Every website has its conversion funnel; [](https://sellingsignals.com/lead-generation-funnel/)**[\[2\]](https://sellingsignals.com/lead-generation-funnel/)** however, there are three imperative stages that customers undergo. They are: Awareness, Consideration, and Decision.

The processes start with Awareness: it entails prospects researching solutions to their situation. After getting an answer, they commit to solving it – this is the second stage, Consideration. Lastly, the final stage is called Decision. It is when visitors are ready to purchase products after thinking about the critical factor.

The stages are cyclical, meaning the customer may rethink their choice and start again. These three important stages considerably impact website conversion rates and revenue, hence the reasons for measuring the speed.

#### Understanding Your Customer

You and your co-founder need to understand the needs of your customer to offer an appropriate solution. According to what your company offers, you can know your customers’ situation.

For example, if you deal with furniture, you expect your client to want furniture for various spaces. Categorize this furniture according to pricing or rooms like living room, kitchen, bedroom, and so on.

Highlight these products with their descriptions so the client can find all the important information in one place. By doing this, you will be easing your customer’s work, influencing them to consider and finally decide on buying.

#### Understanding the User Experience

Website conversions help understand the user experience. If there is a fall in the conversion rate, the action is to identify and respond by changing anything wrong with the site.

Low conversion rates are caused by a few factors, from the site’s performance, industry to [](https://onesearchpro.my/mobile-seo/)**[mobile SEO](https://onesearchpro.my/mobile-seo/)**. You can test and audit your website regularly even when the site is doing well.

Furthermore, you can research and compare your website to a competitor’s. If it is a high converting website, watch and learn how their [**landing page**](https://www.andcards.com/blog/tips/optimize-contact-page/) are set to improve user experience. Also, improve your conversion funnel to encourage more website conversions.

### Aim To Be Mobile-Friendly

Is your website mobile-friendly? If not, then this is a wake-up call. A significant number of purchases are via mobile devices rather than computers.

A mobile-friendly page enables users to use any device when accessing your page. For impulsive buyers, they can order products wherever they are using their mobile devices.

If you can only access the page through a computer, the desire to purchase may not be there anymore by the time you get one.

Mobile-friendly pages have also shown a higher conversion rate. So how do you make your site mobile-friendly?

Below are some great pro tips:

*   Make the page responsive, meaning it will be accessible on a mobile device.
*   Use larger buttons since people use a thumb on mobile.
*   Allow a switch to desktop view to make reading the written points easy.
*   Use a larger font size in a newsletter for readers.
*   The site’s design should be straightforward and not difficult to scroll.
*   Constantly check how the site is doing on mobile to improve it, especially the subscribe button.

### Make It Easy to Convert

Increase your conversion rate by creating a simple conversion process. Making your website user-friendly by creating an easy converting process is the way to go. For instance, if the users are filling up a form, only ask relevant and brief questions.

Avoid many questions as it is among the reasons why many companies have low conversion rates. Also, reduce the steps taken for completing a conversion goal. Some users visit a page to convert but leave without any conversions due to a very long process.

**Pro Tips On Conversion Optimization:**

*   Ensure the steps are brief to avoid an error.
*   Only focus on the most important things.
*   Use a noticeable Call-To-Action, keywords, and Adwords. The prompts should not be hidden.
*   Make your web easy to navigate and understand, increasing more sales.
