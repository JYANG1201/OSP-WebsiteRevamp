## Ecommerce SEO: What is It?

Before talking about e-commerce SEO, let’s trace our steps all the way back to the basics: what is SEO and how it works?

[](/seo/)**[Search Engine Optimization](/seo/)** is a process to rank your website over search engines. The question is why is it important to rank your website?

It’s simple. Ranking can be regarded as a sure-shot way to reach your potential customer. When your website appears on the top page, it has a higher chance of getting your searcher’s attention compared to when your ecommerce site is buried deep among the search results.

**Ecommerce SEO** is like plain’o SEO where you’ll try to rank your e-commerce store. To put it in context, some examples include Myntra, Flipkart, etc. It can be used to get more traffic to your website and increase your expected sales conversion rate.

The thing with SEO and **ecommerce Search Engine Optimization** is that search engines like Google and Bing will only position your site on top if it has all the correct and relevant information.

The higher the rank, the better your visibility will be, and correspondingly, the more traffic. Apart from the obvious, you optimize your stores with product descriptions, metadata, headlines, etc.

Let’s see more points differentiating **SEO ecommerce** and SEO:

*   SEO is a process to rank your website’s pages for search engines like Google and Bing while **e-commerce SEO** deals with ranking your e-commerce store on search engines.
*   You optimize your pages mostly based on a single location in regular or local SEO. However, in **e-commerce SEO**, you need to look out for multiple locations all at once.
*   Under regular SEO, you don’t have to put any modifiers. In **e-commerce SEO,** you must include modifiers like deal, cheap, etc.
*   In SEO, keywords are used to optimize pages and rank your e-commerce store. Therefore, the content/ description section is kept short, unique and keyword optimized – the opposite of e-commerce SEO.

Let’s take the example of a coffee recipe.

When you search for instructions and recipes on _“how to make coffee”_, your search engine will fill your SERP with content that relates to your search term. The most relevant page with the proper keyword will usually rank on top above the others.

### Doing Ecommerce Keyword Research

It’s not possible to reach your target audience without making sure that your website has all the right keywords in it. Whenever potential buyers look for a product, they will search for it using a string of specific words or phrases.

This is the same even for [](/affiliate-marketing-malaysia/)**[affiliate marketing](/affiliate-marketing-malaysia/)** where you will be required to use a set of highly convertible keywords that would bring in customers.

For example, somebody wants to buy a pair of shoes. What could be the words they would use to find it? _“shoes”_ or, if we want to be more specific, _“red-colored shoes”._ These words could be the keywords that will help you appear on SERPs whenever your customer is looking for a product.

But the competition on these short and vague terms like _“shoes”_ is immeasurable. In such cases, you’ll be better off using long-tailed keywords like “lady shoes Selangor.” This way, you can target your audience for a specific product, service, or location.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Picture5-3.jpg)

An important e-commerce SEO tip for good keyword ranking is using relevant keywords.

When you are looking for the keywords, keep the following things in mind:

**Keyword Types:** To **improve SEO** of your website, try to use a mixture of different keyword types.

*   **Short-Tail Keywords:** Short-tail keywords consists of no more than three words with a relatively high search volume. They are also named after the main term keywords. These words tell you about the general products the e-commerce website is dealing with. Examples: “shoes”, “purses”, etc.
*   **Long-Tail Keywords:** Long-tail keywords are the phrases that are more specific and detailed than its counterpart. Examples: “best red-colored shoes online” or “top 10 restaurants near me”. The best part of using long-tail keywords is that there is less competition so it can be relatively easier to rank your website.
*   **Geo-Targeted Keywords:** Geo-targeted keywords are based on certain geographical areas. You can use these keywords to target a specific country or state. Suppose your searcher is looking for a pair of shoes in Malaysia.

One example can be: “good pair of shoes in Malaysia.” You’ll get a list of links that will help you find what you searched for in your specified location.

*   **Latent Semantic Keyword:** LSI keywords are similar to your main keyword. These keywords help the search engine understand what your content is all about. For example: using the term “footwear” instead of “shoes” or “automobile” instead of “cars.”

**Competition:** When it comes to keywords, competition is an important factor that one should look for. Check out your competitor’s page and inculcate keywords to help your website rank. Many **ecommerce SEO experts** recommend choosing a keyword that has a medium level of competition for an e-commerce business that is just starting.

**Cost Per Click (CPC):** For paid advertisements, pay attention to the Cost Per Click (CPC) metric. It tells you how much money you will be spending on each click to drive traffic to your website.

### Building Quality Backlinks

Many people confuse backlinks with links. Whereas backlinks are links that come from other websites, inbound links are in line with the information currently being displayed on the website.

The more inbound links you have, the more likely your ecommerce site will rank higher on search results pages.

Building quality backlinks for SEO is an ongoing process that can be achieved by using a few different techniques – some of which include:

1) **Guest blogging –** Guest blogging is a powerful way to get a link from a popular site with high domain authority which can improve your search engine rankings. It can help you establish yourself as an authority in your field.

2) **Outreach –** This strategy involves reaching out to other bloggers and websites that you want links from and asking them for a link exchange.

3) **Social media marketing –** Link building via social media is the act of asking other online businesses/brands to display a link on their website leading to yours. It’s especially important for companies because every like, click, or even share is considered an act of good faith.

![](/wp-content/uploads/2022/02/Picture16-1.jpg)

The key takeaway here is not to just dive in and build backlinks blindly. You’ll have to build quality links for SEO and website traffic – and this can be done with the application of different SEO link-building tips and strategies.

## Roles of CRO in Ecommerce SEO

No business can run without money. To make money, one needs to convert potential customers into actual customers.

In this case, executing some **ecommerce SEO best practices** can help you grab maximum organic traffic.

But what will you get from all the traffic if you cannot convert them?

Here is where CRO comes into the picture. Conversion rate optimization is a method to convert your traffic into consumers.

But how you ask? Let’s see how CRO matters in SEO.

1. **Understanding customer behavior:** One can bring in a pool of visitors to their e-commerce website after optimizing their sites with all the ecommerce **SEO strategies**, but will these visitors turn into customers? From adding things on your wishlist to looking for the other product, CRO administers all the activities on your website. Through this, you can figure out your potential customer and target them.

2. **Improves User’s Experience:** SEO is one of the methods for driving traffic on an e-commerce website. However, you need CRO to understand your user’s experience and improve your website. For example, when a visitor leaves your site without purchasing anything, CRO will help you figure out what went wrong and where improvement is required.

3. **Important part of Sales Funnel:** SEO is important to create traffic amongst the audience, which is a vital aspect of the sales funnel. CRO will help an **SEO business** turn that audience into consumers for reaching the end stage of the sales funnel, i.e., conversion.

Maximum [](/converting-website/)**[website conversion](/converting-website/)** takes place when both CRO and SEO work together. With the help of both, you will reach your goal and attain maximum profit in your online e-commerce business.
