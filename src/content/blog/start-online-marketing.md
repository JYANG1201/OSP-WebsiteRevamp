Businesses have traditionally relied on offline marketing to get their brands out there. They’ve relied on pamphlets, tele-marketing, billboards, mainstream media ads, and the like. However, these offline methods are increasingly being superseded by online marketing.

**Online marketing** isn’t new, as it has been around since the internet became accessible to the general public. However, since then, **digital marketing solutions** have developed to become much more effective and targeted. Digital marketing allows businesses to reach a much wider audience than ever before, and its more cost effective in many ways.

Nowadays, a company’s **digital marketing strategy** has to be influenced and shaped by things like big data, key performance indicators, and a sound digital marketing perspective. They have to explore an **online marketing plan** that suits the brand and target market. 

In short, when it comes to online marketing, there isn’t a ‘one size fits all’ strategy. Oftentimes, it will involve some level of trial and error. However, digital marketing is the future, and there’s really no turning back.

### **1\. Identify Your E-commerce Business Model**

There are four traditional business models in e-commerce, namely B2C, B2B, C2B and C2C. If these terms confuse, you, then let’s break it down to more understandable language:

B2C – The most conventional type of business model, where businesses(B) sell to customers( C). They may sell a product, or a service. B2C business models have the shortest decision making process, and therefore involve less marketing costs in general.

B2B – Whereby businesses sell to other businesses. The business that buys the service or product can consume it themselves and be the end consumer. They can also be resellers,that function like middlemen.

C2B – E-commerce platforms whereby consumers sell services or products to larger companies or businesses.. Examples of this are freelancing sites like Upwork, and Fiverr. Another example is whereby larger companies engage with influencers and Youtubers to feature their products for a fee.

C2C – Platforms that allow individual consumers to sell products and services to other consumers are called C2C platforms. These platforms allow individuals to become vendors, and sell products they’ve made themselves as well as second hand items. In Malaysia, one well known C2C platform is Mudah.my, especially in its early days.

Determining your business model helps you plan your marketing better.

Many companies and business owners will find that e-commerce platforms these days are a combination of several e-commerce business models. For example, Malaysian online marketplace Lazada is both a B2C and C2C platform. They allow individuals to sell home made snacks and crafts, as well as big brands to sell items like appliances and electronics.

Understanding your business model will determine how you plan and target your online marketing. 

### **2\. Embrace the Power of Social Media**

By  2020, an estimated 33.46 million Malaysians will be on social media, up from the 28.98 million users documented. These social media networks include Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, Twitter, TikTok and more. 

As business owners, your first step in online marketing should be to create a business profile for yourself on these platforms. Each social media platform caters to different demographics and age groups. 

Knowing your target market will help you decide which social media platforms to focus on. For example, Facebook has many users above the age of 30, whereas Instagram has many users below the age of 30. LinkedIn caters for the professional and business community, whereas TikTok caters more for fun-loving youths. If you’re looking to start a Facebook page, it is also important to have come up with a useful guide on how you can maximize your page’s marketing potential.

### **3\. Use Social Media Ads Strategically**

There are two main social media ads online today, Google Ads and Facebook Ads. Both of these can be strategically targeted towards your chosen demographic. To launch these ads, you’ll have to decide what age groups and geographical location you want to target, as well as the interests of your target market. 

Launching social media ads will also give you an insight into which target groups are most responsive. Therefore you can improve your **digital advertising in Malaysia** to focus on the people who matter most. It will also save your marketing costs in the long term. 

### **4\. Leverage on Influencer Marketing**

The concept of ‘influencers’ is very much a recent phenomenon. Basically, influencers are individuals on social media platforms who command a large following. They create content that attracts many people, and are able to monetize their following in different ways. 

Malaysia has its share of successful influencers, on platforms like Youtube and Instagram. More often than not, they create content that caters to a particular interest. These may include fashion, lifestyle, travel, food and many more. 

When an influencer endorses a product or service related to their niche, followers will naturally take an interest. This is why you should consider engaging an influencer for your brand when you start out.
