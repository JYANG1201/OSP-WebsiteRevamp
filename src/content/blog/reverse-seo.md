## So, What Is Reverse SEO And Why It Is So Important?

Reverse SEO stands for reverse search engine optimization. In short, it’s an online reputation management tactic that uses the same keywords to push down certain search rankings of a web page by intentionally optimizing other ones.

Usually, a reverse SEO marketing strategy takes place when you find a negative review or press release about your brand.

While you might think that a few bad criticisms won’t do your business any harm, a survey has shown that online customer reviews on the internet are very important.

According to it, 92% of consumers are less likely to use a business if they spot unfavorable rundowns. [\[1\]](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/)

![](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/word-image-23.png)

_Online customer reviews can make or break your brand’s popularity. Source:_ [**Medium**](https://medium.com/)

The most challenging part of getting a bad press release or a negative review is that they can’t be deleted. So you’ll have to work your way around them.

**They’re right there, in the open, for everyone to see.**

And this is exactly the reason why many leaders in the industry choose to boost their search engines’ reputation management. By having an impact on Google search results, reverse SEO can push down negative web pages and promote other ones that truly help your brand.

Are there any advantages to pushing down negative critiques?

After all, it’s not like you can delete those entries and people can still see them, right?

It’s a great approach to make those bad web pages rank lower by pushing them on page 2 of search results. It turns out that only 0.78% of Google searches click on something from the second page. [\[2\]](https://backlinko.com/google-ctr-stats)

So, if you suppress the damaging appraisals on the first page of Google Search, it’ll work great for your brand’s reputation!

## Is Reverse SEO the Same Thing as Negative SEO?

In short – absolutely not! Believe it or not, it is quite the opposite as reverse SEO is actually a white hat SEO technique.

And while we’re at it, let’s see the differences between negative SEO and reverse SEO.

First of all, the optimization techniques and the goals are totally different.

For instance, negative SEO focuses on lowering your competitor’s search rankings intentionally by producing a lot of low-quality backlinks to them. Basically, you’re sabotaging their search ranking in your favor.

Even if negative SEO is not illegal, it’s a malicious practice.

![](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/word-image.jpeg)

_Negative SEO is an unethical practice and will get you a Google penalty. Source:_ [**RedPoints**](https://www.redpoints.com/blog/negative-seo/)

However, reverse SEO aims to manage your brand’s damaged reputation. You don’t hurt other competitors on purpose and harm their search rankings.

Using the same keywords as the pages that portray you negatively, you create more positive or neutral pages that will rank better than negative criticisms.

With reverse SEO, the end goal is to create fresh and better pages that will outrank the negative ones because they’re simply optimized better. Surveys show that only 50% of consumers paid attention to reviews written in the past 2 weeks only. [\[3\]](https://www.brightlocal.com/research/local-consumer-review-survey/)

So even if you happen to discover bad reviews, creating newer and fresher pages will save your brand’s popularity.

Needless to say, negative SEO will get you **[Google Penalty](https://onesearchpro.my/google-penalty/)** since your main goal is to manipulate the search results on the Search Engine Results Page (SERPs) in your favor in an unethical way.

On the other hand, because reverse SEO is a white hat technique, you’ll be allowed to perform this strategy by search engines without being penalized.

### 1\. Optimizing And Deoptimizing for Keyword Cannibalization

![](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/word-image-1.jpeg)

_Keyword cannibalization is usually a misunderstood concept. Source:_ [**Forward Linking**](https://forward-linking.com/blog/keyword-cannibalization/)

Once you start optimizing your new content to bury the negative critiques, you’ll have to be extra careful with the keywords you choose. Otherwise, you can risk having a lot of pages competing for the same spot in search rankings.

This is why, the best thing when you’re rethinking your keyword campaigns, you’ll have to make sure that your pages don’t cannibalize themselves. What does that mean?

When you have several pages that compete for the same keywords or phrase in Google, it’s usually called keyword cannibalization. As Ahrefs likes to put it – keyword cannibalization is a hot topic in the SEO world, but it can be easily solved using few simple methods. [\[4\]](https://ahrefs.com/blog/keyword-cannibalization/)

Basically, when you’re optimizing new content, pay attention to the other pages on your website as well. If necessary, remove and replace any keywords that don’t help your site rank better.
