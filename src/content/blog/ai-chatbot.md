## FAQs

#### **What Does AI Chatbot Do?**

AI chatbots are computer programs that use AI algorithms to mimic human conversation and interact with users through text or voice.

They can be used for various purposes and integrated into different platforms, allowing businesses to automate communication and enhance customer experience.

AI conversation bots use NLP, machine learning, and other AI techniques to understand user intent, provide personalized responses, and improve their performance over time.

#### **How Do I Use AI Chatbot?**

The usage of chatbots depends on the specific chatbot you are interacting with and the platform it is integrated into. However, here are some general steps to use an AI chatbot:

*   **Find the Chatbot:** The chatbot may be available on a website, **AI chat app**, social media platform, or other digital channel.
*   **Initiate the Conversation:** Depending on the platform, you may need to click a button, type a message, or use a voice command to start the conversation with the chatbot.
*   **Ask a Question or Provide Input:** Once the conversation has started, you can ask the chatbot a question, provide input, or give a command. The chatbot will use AI algorithms to understand your intent and provide a response.
*   **Follow the Prompts:** The chatbot may ask you follow-up questions or provide prompts to guide you through the conversation. Follow these prompts to get the most out of the chatbot’s capabilities.
*   **Provide Feedback:** If the chatbot’s response is not helpful or accurate, you can provide feedback to help the chatbot learn and improve over time.
*   **End the Conversation:** Once you have received the information or completed the task, you can end the conversation with the chatbot by saying goodbye or closing the chat window.

Overall, using an AI chatbot is similar to having a conversation with a human, but with the added benefits of automation, efficiency, and personalization.

#### **What is the Difference Between AI and AI Chat Bot?**

Although chatbots fall under the umbrella of conversational AI, it’s important to note that not all chatbots utilize conversational AI technology.

Rule-based chatbots operate on a system of predetermined triggers and responses, such as keywords, without leveraging the power of true conversational AI.
