## **What are Google Shopping Actions?**

**Google Shopping Actions** is a program offered by Google that allows retailers to sell their products directly on Google’s platform. With this program, customers can purchase products from various retailers using a universal cart, which makes the shopping experience smoother and more streamlined.

![Google Shopping Actions: How to increase visibility with SEO | Google Merchant | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/word-image-44378-2.png)

Introduced in March 2018, Google Shopping Actions is an evolution of Purchases on Google. Unlike Google Shopping, which uses a pay-per-click model, Shopping Actions follows a commission-based model. This means that businesses only pay once a sale takes place.

Google Shopping Actions is designed to provide increased visibility for retailers. When you list items in Google Shopping Action, they gain higher visibility in Google Search results. This enables retailers to reach a vast online audience and increase their sales.

To participate in Google Shopping Actions, you need to have a **Google Merchant account**. This account allows you to manage your product listings, create shopping ads, and track your sales performance.

Overall, Google Shopping Actions is an excellent way for retailers to increase their visibility and sales on Google’s platform. By using SEO strategies, you can optimize your product listings and improve your rankings in Google Search results.

## **What is the difference with Google Shopping Ads?**

Google Shopping Ads and Google Shopping Actions are two distinct advertising models within the Google Merchant platform. While both of these models allow merchants to advertise their products on Google, there are some key differences between them.

### **Google Shopping Ads**

Google Shopping Ads is a model that allows merchants to advertise their products on Google search results pages. These ads appear at the top of the search results page, above the organic search results. The[](https://onesearchpro.my/google-display-ads/) [](https://onesearchpro.my/google-display-ads/)**[ads are displayed](https://onesearchpro.my/google-display-ads/)** in a carousel format and include an image of the product, its title, price, and the name of the merchant center.

![Google Shopping Ads allow merchants to advertise products in Google search results page | Google Merchant | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/google-shopping-ads-2.png)

Source: [](https://ads.google.com/intl/en_my/home/campaigns/shopping-ads/)[Google Shopping Ads](https://ads.google.com/intl/en_my/home/campaigns/shopping-ads/)

Merchants who wish to advertise their products on Google Shopping Ads must create a Google Merchant center account and upload their product feed to the Google Merchant Center. Google then uses this feed to display the products on Google Shopping Ads.

### **Google Shopping Actions**

Google Shopping Actions, on the other hand, is a model that allows merchants to sell their products directly on Google. This model allows customers to purchase products without ever leaving the Google platform.

Merchants who participate in Google Shopping Actions are required to provide Google with their product feed and inventory data.

The main difference between Google Shopping Ads and [](https://www.datafeedwatch.com/blog/google-shopping-actions)**[Google Shopping Actions](https://www.datafeedwatch.com/blog/google-shopping-actions)** is that the former is an advertising model, while the latter is a sales model.

Google Ads is designed to drive traffic to a merchant’s website, while Google Shopping Actions is designed to allow customers to purchase products directly on Google.

### **Differences between Google Shopping Ads and Google Shopping Actions**

Here are some of the key differences between Google Shopping Ads and Google Shopping Actions:

*   Google Shopping Ads is an advertising model, while Google Shopping Actions is a sales model.
*   Google Ads drives traffic to a merchant’s website, while Google Shopping Actions allows customers to purchase products directly on Google.
*   Merchants who participate in Google Shopping Ads must create a Google Merchant Center account and upload their product feed to the Google Merchant Center. Merchants who participate in Google Shopping Actions are required to provide Google with their product feed and inventory data.
*   Google Shopping Ads are displayed at the top of the search results page, above the organic search results. Google Shopping Actions are displayed in a separate section on the search results page.

While Google has introduced Google Shopping Actions as a new sales model, Google Shopping Ads still exist. The reason for keeping both shopping functions is that they serve different purposes.

By offering both models, Google can cater to the needs of different merchant centers and customers.

## **Getting Started with the New Google Shopping Platform**

If you’re looking to increase your visibility on **Google Shop**, you’ll need to get started with the new Google Shopping platform account. Here are the steps to follow:

1.  **Create a Google Account**: If you don’t already have a Google account, you’ll need to create one to access the Google Merchant Center.
2.  **Visit Google Merchant Center**: Once you have a Google account, you can visit the Google Merchant Center website to get started.

![Visit Google Merchant center website to start | Google Merchant | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/word-image-44378-6-1.png)

Source: [](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2019/09/18/google-merchant-center)[Wordstream](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/ws/2019/09/18/google-merchant-center)

1.  **Agree to the terms of service**: Before you can use the Google Merchant Center, you’ll need to agree to the terms of service.
2.  **Verify and Claim Your Website**: To use the Google Merchant Center, you’ll need to verify and claim your website. This involves adding a code to your website or verifying your domain ownership through Google Search Console.
3.  **Set up your shipping and tax information**: Once you’ve verified your website, you’ll need to set up your shipping and tax information in the Google Merchant Center.
4.  **Add your products**: After setting up your shipping and tax information, you can start adding your products to the Google Merchant Center. You can add your products manually or upload a product feed.
5.  **Comply with Google’s policies**: Google has a set of policies that all merchants must comply with to use the Google Merchant Center. Make sure you read and understand these policies before adding your products.
6.  **Submit your products for review**: Once you’ve added your products, you’ll need to submit them for review. Google will review your products to make sure they comply with their policies.
7.  **Monitor and optimize**: After your products have been approved, you can monitor and optimize your product listings to improve your visibility on Google Shopping. You can use SEO techniques to improve your product titles, descriptions, and images.

By following these steps, you can get started with the new Google Shopping platform and increase your visibility on Google Shopping.
