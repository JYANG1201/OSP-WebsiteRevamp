## **The Ever-Changing Facebook Algorithm**

Over the years, Facebook keeps changing its posting algorithm to better suit its users’ experience when browsing on the platform. Facebook algorithm manages how a user sees the order of posts being presented to them.

In the past, the algorithm used to be chronologically based, meaning, the freshest and newest posts get served to the users first.

Now, users see posts and ads in a different way, from the most relevant based on how frequent the user interacts with the person or page, to the least relevant.

While this change means nothing to users themselves, a new update to Facebook postings often cause marketers to scramble trying to research ways to adapt to the latest methodology. 

So what can we understand from this? 

It simply means Facebook’s posting algorithm is favouring non-commercial user posts over business pages’ content.

### **Using Facebook Insights to Find Your Best Time to Post on Facebook**

It can be rather overwhelming to try making sense of all the data in front of you, especially if you are unfamiliar with Facebook management.

Once you’ve landed on the overview of Facebook Insights for your page, these are the few key things to find out:

#### **1\. When Your Fans Are Online**

To know when your fans are online, click on “Posts” at the left menu. You will be presented with data that details the popular times and days your fans are active on Facebook (not your page). 

So how do you interpret your page’s data?

Look at the average number of fans online across the days and times first. Note down the trend, such as the times where your fans are most active as indicated by the large numbers. 

Compile at least a few weeks of data if your page has existed for some time. If your Facebook page is rather new, keep monitoring until you find a consistent pattern.

The times where your fans are most active on Facebook are your best bet as the most ideal times for posting content. 

![Sample Engagement Rate Analysis on Facebook | Best Time to Post on Facebook Malaysia | One Search Pro Digital Marketing](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/OXnYQ2gmYyD59efJyxKcnnOhkE5cqe_quNTjZW9sATiQRanjY5LRyUeHCHFyfE2V9ocgOt9DSLPLLJFCAxQ08rs7smegGGsYEoK_KZnMTLpVatOjOwgT6HTmO3rEmucNu5bwKofX)

Of course, the times when a majority of your fans are online do not mean you are guaranteed of a high engagement, but you are increasing the likelihood of your posts being seen by them.

You will need to experiment on a few posting time variations though — it may be a long process but it’s worth the results.

#### **2\. Posting Times of Successful Posts**

Another means of identifying the best times to post will be analysing the posting times of your most successful posts. 

To do this, after clicking on Facebook Insights, click on “Posts” and scroll to the bottom. You’ll find a list of your published posts under “All Posts Published” section. Sieve through your well-to-do posts, and compile their posting times.

![Back-End Analysis of All Posts' Performance | Best Time to Post on Facebook Malaysia | One Search Pro Digital Marketing](https://lh5.googleusercontent.com/KeTNGv7p3Ou44PnONVoaW5mnQq_9QcXh4C59hB6rUP8gndzuunQlYIpX1CR0Sr_YEHmf_29EkcTbqQf-Zb9sxW-gNDAeTy0WvEF2CVOjcpG4kF547IrSWbLFIK9crzDDau2Sdaav)
