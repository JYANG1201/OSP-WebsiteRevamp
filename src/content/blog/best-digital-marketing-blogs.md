## **How Many Types of Digital Marketing Niches Exist at the Moment?**

1.  **Content marketing** is a strategy that’s focused on creating and sharing content constantly to attract a relevant audience and drive sales.  
      
    
2.  **Backlinks marketing** refers strictly to campaigns that specialize in finding high-authority websites that are willing to mention you in their blog posts.  
      
    
3.  **White hat link building** is an approach that ensures there is nothing shady about the way you get mentioned by other websites so that you rank higher.  
      
    
4.  **Affiliate marketing** – because everyone needs a good little incentive. This tactic focuses on different marketing methods that earn commissions by promoting other businesses.  
      
    
5.  **Email marketing** refers strictly to the emails you send to your customers or potential leads that may include business requests, ads, or important news.  
      
    
6.  **Inbound marketing** focuses on giving practical and useful solutions to your customers rather than spamming them with the information they don’t need.  
      
    
7.  **Marketing automation** is a great way to save time while planning different outreach campaigns. It’s easy, efficient, and it increases your revenue quicker than doing things manually.
