## Tips on Choosing the Best Website Builder For Website Design and SEO Services

The good news is that there are numerous website builder options and each of them caters to a particular set of needs.

The challenge, however, lies in picking the right site builder from this sea of options.

To help you cross that bridge, here are some tell-tale features that could help you identify and get your website builder that works best according to different needs.

### Ease of Use

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/word-image-49.png)

_Code-based website building requires refined tech skills. Source:_ [](https://www.interaction-design.org/literature/article/an-introduction-to-usability)**[Interaction Design Foundation](https://www.interaction-design.org/literature/article/an-introduction-to-usability)**

Ease of use varies from person to person. For example, if you are tech-savvy, you may enjoy the creative freedom offered by a code-based platform for SEO.

In contrast, if you are not adept at coding and other design tasks, website builders like Wix and Shopify may be more ideal. Suffice to say, it is best to choose an option that is simple for you to use based on your needs and capabilities.

### Scalability

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/word-image-50.png)

_Aim for an SEO platform that can accommodate your needs when you scale up. Source:_ [](https://www.educba.com/scalability-testing/)**[eduCBA](https://www.educba.com/scalability-testing/)**

Do you intend to grow your online business to a bigger scale in the future? If you employ a [](/customer-driven-marketing-strategy/)[**customer-driven marketing strategy**](/customer-driven-marketing-strategy/) or run a content-centric website, do you anticipate some growth in your subscriber base?

If your answers are in the affirmative then it is advisable that you aim for a website builder that can meet your scaling requirements.

This is especially crucial if the website builder does not allow site migration because you would need to start afresh if you have to move to another platform to scale up.

### Design

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/word-image-51.png)

_Themes and templates make designing easy. Source:_ [](https://www.ovtt.org/en/resources/design-thinking-agile-methods/)**[OVTT](https://www.ovtt.org/en/resources/design-thinking-agile-methods/)**

A site builder that offers quality templates, themes, and layout options can make it easy for you to build a website that’s appealing.

Alternatively, if you prefer a highly customized web design, aim for a platform that offers more creative flexibility through coding or third-party apps.

### Support

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/word-image-52.png)

_Reliable customer support is essential for troubleshooting. Source:_ [](https://martech.zone/how-to-choose-the-best-support-channels/)**[Martech Zone](https://martech.zone/how-to-choose-the-best-support-channels/)**

Reliable customer support makes building your website less stressful. It also comes in handy when you face operational challenges like downtime or compromised security.

Subsequently, it would be ideal to choose a website builder that responds promptly to troubleshooting requests and offers support through convenient channels.

### Pricing

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/word-image-53.png)

_Aim for a website builder within your budget. Source:_ [](https://www.business2community.com/consumer-marketing/six-stages-consumer-buying-process-market-0811565)**[Business2Community](https://www.business2community.com/consumer-marketing/six-stages-consumer-buying-process-market-0811565)**

Contrary to popular opinion, the priciest website builder is not always the best SEO solution.

A better approach would be to find a web builder that is within your budget but also offers value for money in terms of advanced features and functionality.

### Security

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/word-image-54.png)

_Website insecurity negatively impacts SEO rankings. Source:_ [](https://www.velocityconsultancy.com/importance-of-website-security-for-your-business/)**[Velocity Consultancy](https://www.velocityconsultancy.com/importance-of-website-security-for-your-business/)**

Site security should be a priority for you regardless of whether you are creating an e-commerce platform or a content-driven website.

Search engines such as Google can detect insecure websites and this could negatively impact your SEO efforts and rankings. [](https://www.awebco.com/blog/site-not-secure/)[\[2\]](https://www.awebco.com/blog/site-not-secure/)

## Wrapping Up

All factors and traits considered, we would recommend:

*   **Wix-** if you prefer an easy-to-use website builder with SEO provisions. Weebly can be an alternative to this but its features are not as robust as those on Wix.

*   **Shopify**– for appealing and effective-commerce websites

*   **Web.com-** for entry-level site-building and SEO features on a budget

*   **GoDaddy-** if you need help or would prefer to entirely outsource SEO

*   **Squarespace-** for broader creative freedom with an option to export your content

*   **WordPress-** for a robust content management system and superior SEO hosting

A website builder can best be described as a set of design tools. Subsequently, the site builder that you choose will determine whether you have the right instruments to create the type of website you want and achieve your SEO goals.

It would be helpful to keep this in mind as you make your choice.
