## **What is Mobile SEO?**

In simple terms, **mobile SEO** is the practice of optimizing websites for mobile devices (smartphone and tablet) users, and this includes making the website accessible to search engine crawlers.

Before smartphones proliferated in society, websites prioritized personal computers, but this limited their reach because not many people had access to computers.

As smartphones started to take over, the need to make these websites fit into smaller smartphone screens gave birth to **mobile SEO strategy**.

*   The more the mobile users, the more time people spend and the higher their chances of conducting business with you, which helps you grow.

## **How to Check if You Have a Mobile-Friendly** **Site**

As a business, you can do everything right, from running effective ad campaigns to making your website the best looking one around, trying all the known _online marketing tricks_, but disregarding mobile SEO optimization will be your biggest undoing.

So how then do you ascertain that your website is mobile-friendly?

Consider the following tips to check if your website is user and mobile-friendly:

### **1\. Mobile-Friendly Tests**

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/word-image-1.png)

An SEO mobile optimized web page should be able to fit on a smartphone screen without distortions. Source: [Mobile-Friendly Test](https://search.google.com/test/mobile-friendly)

This is the easiest way to check if your website is optimized for mobile phones and tablets. You simply open the [](https://search.google.com/test/mobile-friendly)**[mobile-friendly test page](https://search.google.com/test/mobile-friendly)**, enter your URL, and hit the test button.

The tool provides you with all the information in regards to your website’s mobile optimization. You even get a screenshot of how it looks on the mobile and tablet screens.

### **2\. Use Your Browser**

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/word-image-2.png)

The contents of the webpage should change to fit the size of the new window. Source: One Search Pro

Another quick way of telling if your website pages are optimized for mobile devices is by using your regular PC browser, be it Google Chrome or Firefox. Use the following steps to get around this within minutes.

*   Open your website on your PC browser, then minimize the window to a small size.
*   With your mouse, grab the left side of the minimized window and shrink it as much as you can to the right.
*   Check how everything fits into the minimized window. A mobile-friendly website will have all the contents within the screen restructured without anything being cut off.

### **3\. Use Your Mobile Phone**

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/word-image-5.jpeg)

A mobile-optimized page will automatically rescale when opened on a mobile phone or tablet. Source: One Search Pro

Trying to open your webpage on your mobile phone or tablet is another sure way of telling if a _mobile SEO platform_ works.

All you need is to open the page on the phone and to check if any of the content is being cut from any side or if the mobile browser **automatically redirects you to the desktop version** of the webpage no matter what you do.

If you have to manually scroll from left to right and back to view the entire page, that is a clear indication that what you are running is not mobile-friendly.

### **4\. Chrome DevTools**

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/word-image-3.png)

Using Chrome DevTools will give you all the information you need to know. Source: One Search Pro

Google Chrome is one of the most widely used browsers for both mobile and PCs. **Chrome DevTools** is a set of web-based and in-built developer tools that allow you to check how mobile-friendly a website is. This can be achieved through the following quick steps.

*   Open your chrome browser and the website you would wish to test.
*   Right-click any point on the website page and choose the “_Inspect”_ option.
*   Click the “_Toggle Device Toolbar”_ next.
*   Select the “_Responsive”_ option. If the selected website shrinks with everything being resized to fit within the margins, then that’s an indication that your website is optimized for portable devices like mobiles and tablets.
*   You can repeat the same steps on other mobile devices like phones and tablets for confirmation.

### **5\. Check PageSpeed Insights on Google**

![](/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/word-image-4.png)

Google Page Speed Insights will show you how well your website page is mobile-friendly. Source: One Search Pro

The final option you can use to check if your website is optimized for smaller smart gadgets is to use the PageInsights tool present in Google to check on your page speed. The tool will provide you with a wide range of data. To access the information, try the following steps.

*   Visit [](https://developers.google.com/speed/pagespeed/insights/)[**Google PageSpeed Insights**](https://developers.google.com/speed/pagespeed/insights/) on your PC.
*   Enter your website’s URL in the space provided and click “_Analyz_e”.
*   A screenshot will be rendered on the screen, and you will see all the data relating to your website. If any of the mobile content is cut off, then your website is not mobile-friendly.

## **What are Some Mobile SEO Best Practices?** **How Can You Improve Mobile SEO?**

The good news for websites that are not optimized for mobiles and tablets is that all is not lost. Knowing how to optimize websites for mobile is not that hard.

Now that we’ve understood **why is mobile SEO important**, there are ways you can further improve the user experience after optimizing the website for smart gadgets. Some of these mobile **SEO best practices** include the following.

### **1.** **Improve Website Speed**

Before venturing into the how-to improve mobile SEO, understand that the **loading speed** of your website will determine its success or failure. No user will waste any more time once they run into loading issues.

_Google mobile optimizer_ also takes into account how fast the site loads in determining its ranking. In a nutshell, getting those speeds up is the first thing you need to do. Make it a **responsive site**!

### **2\. Don’t Block JavaScript, CSS, and Image Files**

Blocking or removing JavaScript, CSS, HTML, or the ability to load image files will not end well for your website. This limits the way Google has access to your webpage, and every time people search for something related to it, it will not show up due to bad **Search Engine Optimization mobile keyword rankings**.

**You may be interested in: [CSS Margin VS Padding for Maximized User Experience](https://onesearchpro.my/margin-vs-padding/)**

### **3\. Avoid Popups**

You will incur a Google penalty if you allow extensive popups for ads, newsletters, or sign-up forms as they get in the way of the mobile users getting Google mobile search results that are qualitative. If you have to resort to pop-ups to get ad revenues, then ensure you follow the guidelines laid down by Google.

### **4\. Integrate Responsive Web Design**

A dead site will impact your rankings badly. A **responsive web design** is highly advocated for by Google as it makes the site understandable for the search engines to allow for easy mobile-first index.

You can verify the extent of your responsive web design by using the _mobile SEO tools_ we mentioned in the last section.

Always keep in mind that Google prefers responsive design!

**Related: [How to Revamp Website](https://onesearchpro.my/how-to-revamp-website/)**

### **5\. Create Mobile-Targeted Content**

Mobile devices like smartphones and tablets are not simply smaller versions of desktop websites; thinking along that line will cause many problems when you try to optimize the website.

Think of mobile content that will work best with smaller gadgets rather than resizing everything down and forcing them to fit into smaller screens. This includes making clickable buttons that are big enough for mobile devices.

### **6\. Optimize Meta Titles and Descriptions**

Precision is everything when dealing with smaller screens; therefore, you have to use optimized [](https://onesearchpro.my/meta-title-description/)**[meta titles and meta descriptions](https://onesearchpro.my/meta-title-description/)** without compromising the quality of the information. Meta descriptions and tags, in general, are one of the many ranking factors that Google takes into consideration.

### **7\. Use Structured Data**

**Structured data** also has to do with the limited space switching from the PC to any mobile devices.

The smaller screen means that any mobile search optimization result that is laden with rich snippets (thanks to structured data) on search results will stand out more when compared to what is displayed on a PC.

You can read more on how [](https://moz.com/learn/seo/schema-structured-data)[**structured data**](https://moz.com/learn/seo/schema-structured-data) can improve the user experience of mobile searchers accessing your website via mobile devices.

### **8\. Build Mobile Sitemap by Creating a Separate Mobile URL**

Creating a second **parallel site** (or even multiple separate URLs) for mobile users independent of the desktop one is another way of improving SEO for mobile. This gives you more control as you can develop all kinds of customizations without worrying about the desktop site affecting your desktop users and desktop searches.

However, care must be taken with site redirects to avoid sending visitors to dead pages or the desktop instead of mobile version of your website.

### **9\. Optimize for Local Mobile Searches**

Consider your target market in terms of **geographical placement** when optimizing for mobile local search results.

If the mobile SEO company you are running has a [](https://onesearchpro.my/local-seo/)[**local SEO**](https://onesearchpro.my/local-seo/) element to it, then localize it to the best of your abilities to become more familiar to the local mobile users.

This ranges from standardizing the address, the name, phone numbers, the details of the city you are based in in the site’s metadata. Simply put, you want organic search results appearing on mobile devices to be within the vicinity!

### **10\. Mobile Site Configuration**

You have to decide whether you will be going with a dynamic, responsive, or separate site configuration. Each of these has its strengths and weaknesses, and Google supports all of them. You just have to compare all of them and go with what you believe to be the most suitable for your SEO mobile optimization plans.
