## **Why You Should Choose WordPress SEO Plugins**

It is not by accident that WordPress SEO plugins are in heavy rotation in most of the websites around today.

It has taken WordPress years of patience, dedication, and research in developing these plugins to turn them into indispensable tools.

The following are the [**reasons to choose WordPress**](https://www.hostnamaste.com/blog/why-to-choose-wordpress-for-better-seo/) and why you are better off with WordPress SEO plugins or your marketing needs.

*   WordPress pays close attention to the user experience to make the websites fun, attractive, and easy to use. This, in turn, makes visitors spend more time on the website, reducing the bounce rate and increasing the website’s ranking.

*   WordPress optimizes images for SEO in a simple way by providing options for adding alternative text while giving you access to tools you can use to alter the images to your preferences for better impact.

*   WordPress is optimized for mobile use. What you do on your leading site will automatically be resized to fit any device your visitors are using. There’s no need to do anything extra to make the website accessible to other devices.

*   The number of SEO plugins that work with WordPress is unlimited, both free and premium. You just have to find the one that suits your needs best and add your tweaks to it to your satisfaction.
