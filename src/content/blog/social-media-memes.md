## **What Exactly are Memes and Where did They Come From?**

One of the first places that mentions the word meme was in the book [](https://www.nature.com/articles/529462a)**[_‘The Selfish Gene’_](https://www.nature.com/articles/529462a)** by biologist Richard Dawkins. However, the word meme in this book was used to describe behaviors or trends that were copied and remixed from person to person.

The existence of Internet memes is as old as the Internet itself.

**_The word meme itself comes from an older English word, ‘mimeme’ which means to copy._**

This came about because memes were copied, personalized and then spread all over the Internet.

No one knows for sure where memes came from, but it’s more likely that it was a natural cultural practice that evolved from Internet use and netizens came up with a term for it later on.

It’s not unusual for the public to copy and share jokes, images or quotes that they liked in the past. The same practice can be applied to a social media meme.

These days, memes are used to subtly convey ideas, opinions and culture in a funny and lighthearted manner.

## **Why are Memes Suitable for Social Media Marketing?**

If you are a marketer with a business page, you can use memes as a way to create a connection with your visitors and followers.

Business memes may not sound like something that’s useful for your brand, but it has plenty of benefits for your sites in the long run. Here are some ways it can help to viralize your content media marketing.

**_\# Benefit 1_**

### **It’s Cost Effective to Produce**

Brand memes are usually not fully original content. Instead, you find and personalize meme templates that others have created. These templates are usually free of any copyright and you can obtain them easily via Google search.

This saves plenty of time and effort, as well as other resources. All you have to do is come up with your own personalized meme ideas to make the meme convey the message you want.

As memes are not copyrighted, you’ll be able to use them and edit them pretty much freely. You are also free to produce your own unique [](https://onesearchpro.my/attractive-niche-content/)[**content marketing**](https://onesearchpro.my/attractive-niche-content/) meme.

**_\# Benefit 2_**

### **It’s Easy to Relate to**

Memes often take on the trendiest current issues and present them in a funny and interesting way. Netizens would have seen this meme somewhere else before, and understand the message being conveyed.

This makes your brand more human and relatable, rather than stiff and distant. Making your brand more relatable [](https://www.msureporter.com/2017/06/08/memes-bring-people-closer-together/)[**brings you closer**](https://www.msureporter.com/2017/06/08/memes-bring-people-closer-together/) to your visitor, and increases their engagement of your brand too since they will perceive your brand as being on the same level as them, rather than talking down to them.

Memes have a way of making your followers and visitors feel relaxed enough to be able to engage with you. These engagements can take the form of likes, follows, subscriptions, reactions, shares and comments.

No matter what type of engagement your content receives, it is a good sign. It means that people are generally interested in your posts.

If you observe memes on social media, you’ll find that they attract some of the highest interactions from visitors. Therefore, memes are an easy way to attract more traffic and increase conversion.

## **How to Post the Best Memes for Your Business**: 5 Tips on How To Use Memes For Your Content Marketing

Here are some of the best practices that you can carry out when creating quality content memes for your social media pages.

There are many ways to design commercial memes, and not all of them have to hard sell your products and services. Sometimes, it’s about attracting the right amount of traffic for your page.

### **1\. Optimize Targeting for Your Meme**

Knowing your audience is the most important aspect of meme creation. Some types of businesses, especially those that target younger consumers, have a better chance of attracting target audiences via memes.

As such, as a meme creator, you should also know what issues resonate with your [](https://onesearchpro.my/social-media-target-audience/)[**target audience**](https://onesearchpro.my/social-media-target-audience/) and the current trends are.

Linking your meme with an issue that’s currently _‘hot’_ among viewers is the best way to optimize your meme for increased traffic.

### **2\. Determine Your Brand Voice**

As a business, it helps if you’re consistent with your brand voice. This means the tone you adopt and the type of content you put up has to be consistent.

The use of memes isn’t necessarily suitable for every type of business. If you have a brand voice that is lighthearted, casual or caters to a younger audience, then memes are for you.

Examples of businesses that usually feature memes as part of their content include entertainment companies, TV channels, trendy food outlets and travel companies.

For these types of businesses, a positive and bright brand voice works to their advantage.

Another type of business that will benefit include startups and micro-enterprises. Small businesses using memes are a great way to be noticed by netizens.

### **3\. Keep Up with Current Issues and Staying Relevant**

It’s easy to keep up with what’s happening around you, especially with the Internet. If you’re on Facebook, you can follow several legitimate local news outlets and this will give you a clearer picture.

It helps to also see what your friends and others are sharing on social media so as to know what is going viral among Malaysians at any given time.

Whenever you see others sharing memes, knowing current issues will allow you to understand the context behind those memes.

It will also allow you to understand the situation enough to be able to create your own memes.

### **4\. Don’t Overdo It**

The usage of memes should not be forced. For example, creating too many memes advertising your products and services may backfire.

Instead of attracting visitors to your account, these advertisement memes may be annoyed and feel pressured that you’re constantly pushing your brand.

Memes should come naturally and it should focus on delivering fun messages.

Seeing how a meme is being used elsewhere is the best way you can learn. They should be subtle and shouldn’t involve over-thinking, just like you’re coming up with a funny joke.

### **5\. Risk Assessment is Necessary**

As a business, it’s advisable for you not to create offensive or controversial content in any way, as this will hurt your company in the long run.

Therefore, before you post any memes, you have to ask yourself whether this content is able to hurt anyone’s feelings.

It is also a good idea to understand the community standards set by all the social platforms today, which prohibit content with violence, racism, sexism, hate speech and the like.

**DO’S**

**DONT’S**

Be funny and relevant to your audience

Make controversial memes that go against community standards

Keep up with the latest pop culture

Use memes you don’t fully understand

Keep track of your meme’s performance
