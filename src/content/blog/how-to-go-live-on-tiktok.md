Going LIVE on TikTok lets you talk to your audience in real time, answer questions and sell products – which is why many Malaysian brands now use TikTok LIVE alongside short videos.

![A TikTok LIVE appearing in the For You feed](/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/2.jpg)

## What are the requirements to go live on TikTok?

**To go LIVE on TikTok you must be at least 18 years old, and most accounts need at least 1,000 followers.** Your account must also follow TikTok's Community Guidelines. TikTok raised the minimum hosting age from 16 to 18 in November 2022.

| Requirement | Detail |
|---|---|
| Minimum age to host a LIVE | 18 years old (based on your account birthday) |
| Followers | Usually at least 1,000 – can vary by region |
| Minimum age to receive Gifts | 18 years old |
| Account standing | No recent Community Guidelines violations |
| App | Latest version of TikTok |

Requirements change from time to time, so the simplest check is to tap **+** in the app – if you see **LIVE**, your account is eligible.

During a LIVE, TikTok's Community Guidelines still apply: no hateful, explicit or extremist content, and creators shouldn't pressure viewers to send Gifts.

## How do you go live on TikTok?

**Tap the + button, swipe to LIVE, add a title and cover image, choose your settings, then tap Go LIVE.**

1. **Tap +** at the bottom of the screen.
2. **Swipe to LIVE** next to the record button. If you only see photo and video options, your account isn't eligible yet.
3. **Add a title and cover.** A clear title (and relevant [TikTok hashtags](/guide-to-hashtags-tiktok/)) helps TikTok show your LIVE to people outside your followers.
4. **Choose settings** such as comment filters, effects and who can join.
5. **Tap Go LIVE.** Viewers, comments and Gifts appear on screen as notifications.
6. **End the LIVE** by tapping the end/power button and confirming.

![The + button at the bottom of the TikTok app](/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/3.jpg)

## How do you schedule a TikTok LIVE?

**Create a LIVE Event from your profile so followers can subscribe and get a reminder before you go live.**

1. Open your profile and look for the **LIVE Events** option (in the LIVE Center or menu, depending on your app version).
2. Tap **Create an event** and enter the name, date, time and description.
3. Submit the event and promote it with a short video before the session.

![Scheduling a TikTok LIVE event](/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/4-2.png)

## How do you go live on TikTok with a guest or co-host?

**While you're LIVE, tap the co-host or guest icon, pick a creator or viewer to invite, and they'll appear on a split screen once they accept.**

1. Start your LIVE.
2. Tap the **co-host / guest** icon on the LIVE screen.
3. Choose a creator who is also LIVE, or accept a viewer's request to join.
4. To end the session, tap the same icon and remove the guest.

![The co-host icon on a TikTok LIVE screen](/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/6.jpg)

## How do you join someone's TikTok LIVE as a guest?

**Open the creator's LIVE and tap the request-to-join option; if the host accepts, you'll appear on screen with them.** You can only join if the host allows guests, and the host can remove you at any time.

## Can you go live on TikTok from a PC?

**Yes – accounts with LIVE access can stream from a computer using TikTok LIVE Studio, TikTok's desktop streaming app.** It's useful for gaming streams, presentations and higher-quality cameras and microphones.

## Why can't I go live on TikTok?

**Usually because of age, follower count, a guidelines violation, an outdated app, or LIVE not being available for your account yet.**

| Reason | What to do |
|---|---|
| Under 18 | You can't host until you're 18 |
| Fewer than the required followers | Keep posting until you reach the threshold (usually 1,000) |
| Community Guidelines violation | Check your account status in Settings; wait for restrictions to end |
| Old app version | Update TikTok and restart |
| LIVE not available in your region/account | Check again later; requirements can change |

## How do you make your TikTok LIVE stand out?

**Plan your LIVE like a show: promote it in advance, go live when followers are active, use good light and sound, and keep talking to viewers.**

- **Go live when followers are active.** Check Follower activity in TikTok Analytics – see our guide to the [best time to post on TikTok in Malaysia](/best-time-to-post-tik-tok-malaysia/).
- **Promote it first.** Post a short video announcing when you'll go live.
- **Use a stable setup.** A phone stand or tripod, a ring light or window light, and a quiet room.
- **Check your internet.** A steady connection prevents lag and dropped streams.
- **Talk to viewers.** Greet new viewers by name and answer comments.
- **Have a rough script.** List the topics or products you'll cover.
- **Moderate comments.** Ask a teammate to filter, mute or block trolls.
