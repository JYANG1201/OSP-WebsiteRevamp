## [](https://www.tiktok.com/@ceddyornot)The Basic Requirements for Going Live on TikTok

According to TikTok’s community guidelines, not everyone can go **live on TikTok.** The **requirements to go Live on TikTok** are twofold.

The **_first_** criteria to access TikTok Live is that you must be at least 16 years old. This is calculated based on the age given at the time you registered your account.

Additionally, even if you’re 16 years old, you are still not eligible to receive Live gifts during your TikTok Live stream, as only those 18 years old and above can do that.

Tiktok Live gifts are a system where users can donate money to a content creator during their Live. The users must first purchase coins from TikTok and then use these coins to offer gifts in the form of cute stickers to the content creator. Each sticker has a different coin value.

The **_second requirement_** to access TikTok Live is that you have at least 1000 followers on TikTok before you’re allowed to go Live.

The requirement on **how many followers on TikTok to go Live** is necessary to ensure that only legitimate content creators can go Live.

In line with the **community guidelines on TikTok**, content creators also need to be aware of the boundaries set for them during the Live. This includes not being allowed to actively ask for Live gifts and other rewards.

The exception to this is if you as the content creator are on a mission to raise money or funds for a cause.

The community guidelines are also strict in forbidding any hateful, explicit, and extremist content during a **TikTok Live.**

## Step by Step Guide on How to Go Live on TikTok

Once you know the basic conditions for a Tik Tok Live, the next step is to start your first Live session. Knowing **how to go Live on TikTok** is actually pretty straightforward.

Here’s a guide to go Live on TikTok and how you can get started to stream directly:

### Find and Press The ‘+’

The moment you open the TikTok app, you’ll be able to see a white square with a ‘+’ sign at the bottom of the screen. If you haven’t signed in to the app, you’ll have to first complete this step before you’re allowed to do any **Live TikTok** session.  

Once you’ve clicked the plus sign, you’ll see several types of content to create, i.e. photo, video or Live.

If you’re not eligible to start a Live on TikTok, you’ll only see the photo and TikTok videos option. Click on the ‘Live’ which is near the recording button.

### Fill in The Live Details

Before you actually go Live, you’ll be asked to fill in some of the Live details. This includes selecting a thumbnail and a title.

Both of these are important in how to make Live on TikTok, because it will determine who your Live broadcast is recommended to. In addition to how many followers you have, your Live will also appear in the ‘For You Page’ or FYP of other TikTok users.

Therefore, the title and subsequent [](https://onesearchpro.my/guide-to-hashtags-tiktok/)**[TikTok hashtags](https://onesearchpro.my/guide-to-hashtags-tiktok/)** you use in the title will help publicize your Live session to users outside your **real time followers** by appearing on their TikTok feed.

Apart from the two details mentioned above, you’ll also be able to control whether viewers can **leave comments**, whether you want **filters or effects**, whether you want to **support a charity**, or share your upcoming live stream on your other social media platforms.

### Click the ‘Live’ Button Once You’re Ready

Once you’re ready, you can now tap the ‘Go Live’ button to start your Live stream directly. You’ll be able to see who is watching your Live as they will appear as **notifications.** This goes the same for any new comments and Live gifts coming in from your viewers.

You will still have control over several aspects of your live stream once you start. You can filter comments, flip your camera angle, and add effects to make the overall experience more pleasant for both you and your viewers.

Once you complete your Live, just tap the power button on the bottom of your launch Live screen and it will immediately end.

## Scheduling a Live Session on TikTok

When you want to make your Live a special event, you can schedule it using the following steps:

*   Go to your TikTok profile page and select the calendar icon in the upper right-hand corner.
*   Click on ‘Create an Event’.
*   Fill in all the relevant event details like event name, date, time, event duration, and description.
*   Click the ‘Create’ icon and wait for the event to be reviewed and approved.
*   Once approved, you will receive a notification and the event will appear on your profile.
*   You can now create a Live video promoting your event or send invites to your friends or followers.

Keep in mind that you can change the description but not the time and name of your event.

## How to Go Live on TikTok with a Co-host or Guest

TikTok has recently allowed **dual Lives**, where two content creators can both be hosting the same Live video. To do this, both creators have to be Live at the same time.

One of the co-hosts will have to carry out the following steps.

Firstly, you will have to know **how to Live on TikTok** as described above in the previous section.

*   Look for a special icon resembling two circles linking each other on the bottom left of your Live screen. Click on it.
*   A list of all available friends and suggested hosts who are Live will appear.
*   Click on your co-host’s ‘Invite’ button.
*   Once your co-host accepts your invite, they will appear on your Live feature in a split-screen.
*   To end the co-hosting session, just click the two circles link icon again and click ‘Confirm’.
