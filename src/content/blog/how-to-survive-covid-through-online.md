However, There’s a way to survive the CMCO restrictions – Taking your business online.

### **4\. Broadcasting Facebook Live Sessions**

In 2018, about 43% of businesses broadcasted at least one live video for their followers. Facebook Live is one of the strongest engagement tools that can take your business to unprecedented heights. 

With the help of Facebook Live, you can directly talk to your business page’s followers and tell them about your products and services. 

Giving a human face to your business can increase brand loyalty, and you can form an emotional connection with your followers. 

You can go live from your Facebook business page every once in a while and just talk to the people. You can host a Q&A session and answer their questions. 

You can even crack jokes and make them laugh. More people will connect to your brand if you connect with them through live video sessions. 

If your video gets a high engagement, it can even get listed under the popular video category. If that happens, even those people can watch your video who have not liked or followed your business page. 

Just like how you endorse your products using images and videos in the Instagram business, Facebook life can do the same for you.
