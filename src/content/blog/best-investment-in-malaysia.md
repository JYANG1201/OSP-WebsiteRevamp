## 13 Best Investment in Malaysia To Grow Your Wealth

If you’re wondering **where to invest in Malaysia**, there are all sorts of investments you can choose from.

These range from **low risk investment in Malaysia** to the **most profitable investment in Malaysia**, though it comes with high risk.

Whatever it is, you can be sure each investment will cater for specific types of [](https://onesearchpro.my/social-media-target-audience/)[**target audience**](https://onesearchpro.my/social-media-target-audience/). Here are 13 of which you can look out for:

### Amanah Saham Bumiputera (ASB) and Amanah Saham Malaysia (ASM)

![Amanah Saham Nasional Berhad | Best Investment in Malaysia | One Search Pro Marketing
](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/word-image-42344-2-1.jpeg)

ASB and ASM are both popular investment options for **low risk investment Malaysia** with attractive dividends. Managed by its main company Amanah Saham Nasional Berhad (ASNB), ASB is meant for bumiputeras while ASM is meant for all Malaysians.

ASB and ASM can be accessed through various platforms such as Maybank and CIMB, making it convenient for investors. Plus, there are no sales or redemption charges and the dividends are distributed annually.

These are subject to changes based on the fund’s performance, and even though it has been on the decline in recent years, you can be sure your returns will always never be negative.

**Risk:** Low

**Returns:** 4.25% and above per annum

### Tabung Haji

![Tabung Haji | Best Investment in Malaysia | One Search Pro Marketing
](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/word-image-42344-3-1.png)

Tabung Haji, also known as the Pilgrims’ Fund Board, is a unique investment opportunity in Malaysia specifically tailored for Muslims who intend to perform Hajj (pilgrimage to Mecca) or Umrah.

It operates as a savings institution and investment fund, aiming to help Muslims save and invest for their pilgrimage expenses. Tabung Haji offers a Shariah-compliant platform, adhering to Islamic principles in its investment activities.

This is truly one of the many low risk investments you can consider as not only are you guaranteed returns by the government, this investment is exempted from taxes and has no fixed deposit amount limit.

**Risk:** Low

**Returns:** Currently around 3% – 4%

### Employees Provident Fund (EPF)

![Employees Provident Fund (EPF) | Best Investment in Malaysia | One Search Pro Marketing
](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/word-image-42344-4-1.png)

EPF is a government-backed retirement savings scheme in Malaysia that offers attractive dividends and allows individuals to save for their retirement.

Through this, both employees and employers contribute a portion of their salaries to the EPF, with the minimum contribution being at 11% of the employee’s monthly salary.

However, you have the option to increase this by either making an additional self-contribution to your EPF account for further investment or raising the mandatory 11% contribution rate, though you will need to contact your company’s human resources department for details.

Thanks to this, EPF offers low risk investments that’s also reliable in the long term due to its steady returns. However, the funds are locked until retirement, limiting liquidity.

You can, however, take out a certain amount to help make major payments such as a house or medical treatments.

**Risk:** Low

**Returns:** 2.5% (for non-conventional accounts) and above per annum

### Private Retirement Schemes (PRS)

![Private Retirement Schemes (PRS) | Best Investment in Malaysia | One Search Pro Marketing
](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/word-image-42344-5-1.jpeg)

The PRS is designed to supplement your EPF or savings account, offering you more flexibility in retirement planning. It is managed by a private fund manager and provides a range of investment options.

The investment amount varies depending on the fund selected, with some offering a minimum investment of RM100. PRS offers potential returns through various investment strategies, including equity and fixed income.

However, it’s important to assess the risk profile of each PRS fund before investing, as your investment returns aren’t always guaranteed.

Take note that just like EPF, you will not be able to take out the investment money you earned easily, and will have to pay an 8% tax penalty should you decide to do so.

On the bright side, you can save money in other areas such as income tax as you can claim tax relief of up to RM3000 when you invest in PRS!

**Risk:** Low to Medium

**Returns:** As high as 4.3% per annum in the year 2023 though it depends on the PRS Fund

### Unit Trust Funds

![Unit Trust Funds | Best Investment in Malaysia | One Search Pro Marketing
](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/word-image-42344-6-1.png)

Unit Trust Funds are a type of collective investment that pool the funds of many different participants to invest in a variety of assets and portfolios, including stocks, bonds, as well as commodities.

Unit trust funds provide an opportunity for investors to invest their money under the guidance of professional fund managers. These managers make informed investment decisions and help create maximum returns with minimum risk.

The minimum investment amount varies depending on the fund and can range from RM100 to a few thousand ringgit, which makes it highly accessible if you can only start small. Plus, Unit Trust Funds offers you the opportunity to access various markets and sectors.

### Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs)

![Exchange Traded Funds (ETFs) | Best Investment in Malaysia | One Search Pro Marketing
](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/word-image-42344-7-1.png)

ETFs are similar to unit trust funds, as ETFs are investment funds traded on the stock exchange that aim to track the performance of an underlying index, commodity, or sector.

ETFs offer diversification and flexibility to investors, as they can be purchased in the same way as stocks, with a minimum investment depending on the market price of the ETF.

Dividends are typically distributed according to the underlying assets of the **ETF Malaysia**. ETFs provide a cost-effective way to gain exposure to different markets and sectors.

There’s no fund managers, which makes it safe and ideal for beginners. However, you will need to consider the liquidity, expense ratios, and tracking accuracy of the ETFs before investing.

**Risk:** Anywhere from low to high due to its diverse portfolio

**Returns:** Highly dependent on your portfolio and funds

### Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs)

![Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) | Best Investment in Malaysia | One Search Pro Marketing
](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/word-image-42344-8-1.png)

REITs are the best type of investments if you want to invest in real estate without directly owning the property. These can include income-generating properties such as commercial buildings, shopping malls, and hotels.

You can purchase REIT units on the stock exchange, which means they’re highly accessible, though the minimum investment depends on the market price of the REIT unit. REITs generate income through rental returns and capital appreciation.

Dividends can be pretty high through REITs and are distributed regularly, typically on a quarterly or semi-annual basis.

While REITs offer the potential for steady income and diversification, it’s crucial to consider the underlying properties and management expertise before investing, as this is key to ensuring high returns in the long run.

**Risk:** Medium

**Returns:** As high as 4% or more per share in annum for the high performing REITs

### Blue Chip Stocks

![Blue Chip Stocks | Best Investment in Malaysia | One Search Pro Marketing
](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/word-image-42344-9-1.png)

Blue chip stocks refer to shares of large, well-established companies with a track record of stable performance. These stocks are often considered less volatile and provide consistent dividends.

As an investor, you can buy blue chip stocks through brokerage accounts, with the minimum investment depending on the stock price. Dividends are usually paid on a regular basis, depending on the company’s dividend policy.

When it comes to investing in blue chip stocks, you can be sure they offer the potential for capital appreciation and long-term returns.

However, it’s important to conduct thorough research and consider the company’s financial health, industry trends, and market conditions before investing.

Plus, there’s high investment costs due to transaction fees and even brokerage fees, which means you’ll lose out more especially if you’re aiming for small, short-term transactions.

**Risk:** Medium to High

**Returns:** Dependent on the current market and the company

### P2P Lending/Debt-Based Crowdfunding

![P2P Lending/ Debt-Based Crowdfunding | Best Investment in Malaysia | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/word-image-42344-12-1.png)

P2P is a form of crowdfunding that uses lending platforms like Fundaztic and Funding Societies to give you the opportunity to lend money directly to borrowers and earn interest. As long as the loan is in effect, you’ll gain interest.

The minimum investment for some of these can start as low as RM50, which makes it highly accessible especially for beginners.

P2P lending offers potential returns through interest payments, some as high as around 10% per annum, and you can always diversify your investments across multiple borrowers to manage risk.

However, default risk is a concern, and investors should thoroughly assess the creditworthiness of borrowers and understand the platform’s risk mitigation measures before participating.

**Risk:** High

**Returns:** Depends on the P2P Platform and whether the borrowers default

### Bonds

![Bonds | Best Investment in Malaysia | One Search Pro Marketing
](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/06/word-image-42344-14-1.png)

Bonds are a form of fixed-income securities that represent a loan made by an investor to the issuer, typically provided by governments or corporations.

Bonds offer regular interest payments and the return of principal upon maturity. The minimum investment amount varies depending on the bond, and they can be purchased through brokers or financial institutions.

Bonds are considered less risky compared to stocks but offer lower potential returns.

For example, the Malaysia 10 Years Government Bond yielded 3.9% – which is why before investing, it’s crucial to evaluate the creditworthiness of the issuer, the bond’s duration, and prevailing interest rates.

**Risk:** Low

**Returns:** Commonly below 4% if provided by government

## Factors to Consider Before Investing

Before diving into any investment, it’s important to take note of several key factors that can significantly impact your investment journey.

To make the **best investment plan in Malaysia**, you need to consider these factors as it will help you make informed decisions and align your investment with your financial goals.

### Risk Tolerance

Risk tolerance and knowing how much you’re willing to tolerate is very important as different investments carry varying degrees of risk. It’s crucial to evaluate your comfort level with market fluctuations and potential losses.

If you’re a conservative investor who doesn’t want to risk high losses, you may opt for lower-risk investments with more stable returns. If you’re an aggressive investor, it means you may be willing to take on higher risk for the potential of greater rewards.

### Investment Horizon

The amount of time you expect to hold your investments before you need to withdraw the money is referred to as your investment horizon.

It can vary based on your financial goals, such as short-term goals (less than five years), medium-term goals (five to ten years), or long-term goals (over ten years).

Short-term goals may prioritise liquidity and stability, while long-term goals can accommodate more volatile investments that offer higher potential returns.

### Financial Goals

Clearly defining your [](https://credit.org/blog/financial-goals-examples/)**[financial goals](https://credit.org/blog/financial-goals-examples/)** is important if you’re aiming for effective investment planning.

Whether you aim to save for retirement, purchase a home, fund your children’s education, or achieve financial independence, your investment objectives will influence your investment strategy.

Each goal may have a different time frame and the risk you’re willing to take, requiring a tailored approach.

### Market Conditions

Market conditions should also be considered as it plays a significant role in investment performance. Economic indicators such as geopolitical events and industry trends can impact the performance of various investment options.

To check on this, conduct research, follow financial news, and consult with financial advisors to gain insights into market trends.

While it’s impossible to predict market movements with certainty, awareness of market conditions can help you make more informed investment decisions and adjust your portfolio accordingly.

## FAQs on How To Invest in Malaysia

#### **Where Should I Invest My Money to Get Good Returns in Malaysia?**

There are several investment options in Malaysia that can potentially provide good returns. Consider diversifying your investments across different asset classes such as stocks, bonds, mutual funds, and real estate.

Blue chip stocks and diversified unit trust funds are popular choices for long-term growth. Real estate investment trusts (REITs) can offer both income and capital appreciation.

It’s essential to conduct thorough research, assess your risk tolerance, and consider professional advice to make an informed and **good investment in Malaysia**.

#### **Which Investment Has the Highest Return in Malaysia?**

The most profitable investments in Malaysia often come with high risks. Generally, investments like equities (stocks) and certain high-growth sectors or individual stocks can offer the potential for higher returns over the long term.

However, it’s important to note that past performance is not indicative of future results. It’s best to carefully evaluate the risk associated with each investment option and diversify your portfolio to manage risk while seeking higher returns.

#### **What is the Best Short Term Investment in Malaysia?**

For **short term investment Malaysia**, there are options such as fixed deposits, money market funds, or short term bond funds which can provide relatively stable returns over a shorter duration.

These investments typically offer lower risk and quick access to funds when needed. However, the returns are lower compared to longer-term investments.

#### **What to Invest With RM100k in Malaysia?**

It depends on your investment objectives. With RM100,000, the best suggestion is to diversify your portfolio. You could allocate a portion of the funds to blue chip stocks, diversified unit trust funds, or exchange-traded funds (ETFs) if you aim to potentially achieve long-term growth.

Additionally, investing in a mix of fixed income securities such as bonds or bond funds can provide stable income. In the end, the allocation money market fund should be based on your risk tolerance, investment horizon, and financial goals.

#### **Which Bank is Best for Investment in Malaysia?**

Several banks in Malaysia offer investment services, and the “best” bank for investment depends on your specific requirements and preferences.

Some renowned banks in Malaysia with investment offerings include Maybank, CIMB, Public Bank, and RHB Bank. To choose, consider factors such as the range of investment products and services, fees and charges, customer service, and convenience of online platforms.
