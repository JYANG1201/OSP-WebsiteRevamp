## What Does an **SEO Expert** Do? 

Let’s start with the basics. 

**What is an SEO expert** and why is their job so important?  

Put simply, SEO stands for **S**earch **E**ngine **O**ptimization. Thus, those who know how to improve your business’s visibility on the Internet using special keywords (from extensive keyword research) relevant to your niche are search engine specialists. 

This takes us to the next point – SEO is fundamental if you want to boost your brand awareness. The better you optimize your website and your social media for search engines, the higher chances to attract more potential customers. 

As an SEO specialist, your job is to make businesses more visible on the Internet. To do that, you need to come up with different marketing strategies, relevant keywords with the correct user intent, and interesting website elements for their brand. 

If you want to become the **best SEO expert**, there are some standards you must meet: 

*   High flexibility;   
*   Constant desire for improvement; 
*   Originality; 
*   A true passion for growing others’ business; 
*   Being tech-savvy.

![Google Search Engine | How to Become an SEO Expert | One Search Pro](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/JUiDX4gi7ECQ-8bXDZggK6wTMvGgaEcIa_7L7HQP35eqkC23tcgRx4EqACzYC3sacxWKQ1-ac18_9n-iTQG_WcJDIwK8KtcA7YUnzh_Vh8gYjhrw9KBC8pP5B5Qr9_s3zO7r9-c)

To make your websites rank, you’ll need to understand on-page SEO, technical SEO, and how search engines crawl your web pages. 

Google analyzes your website’s images, YouTube videos, and content to see what they’re about. After that, the information is stored in Google Index which is a huge database. 

Meanwhile, every time a user searches for something on the Internet, Google tries to provide only the best results for them. Practically, what you do as an SEO specialist is to make sure that Google shows your website to relevant users every time they search for something similar to your business on the Internet. 

If you want to learn **how to become a SEO specialist,** you’ll need to invest your time reading about this topic. The more thorough your research is, the better you’ll understand Google crawlers and optimize your websites.

Why do you have to look at the SERPs? 

The higher your website will rank, the more people will click on your website. Plus, the meta title and description, the name’s of the company, and the link to your website all appear in the SERPs. That’s what people call Google snippets. 

So, every time you’re optimizing web content using technical SEO, remember to work on these elements as well.
