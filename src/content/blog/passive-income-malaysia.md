## What Is Passive Income?

Passive income is a type of financial stream that requires minimal effort to maintain once set in motion, allowing you to make money while not actively involved in the day-to-day operations.

Unlike active income, which demands continuous time and effort, passive income can lead to financial freedom, enabling individuals to break free from the limitations of trading time for money.

Passive income sources can take various forms, from **[making money online](https://onesearchpro.my/make-money-online-malaysia/)** to investments, royalties, real estate, and automated businesses.

Here’s a comparative overview of active income vs passive income:

**Criteria**

**Active Income**

**Passive Income**

**Involvement**

Constant effort and time required

Initial effort, followed by minimal maintenance

**Time Dependency**

Directly linked to hours worked

Decoupled from time; can generate income 24/7

**Flexibility**

Limited flexibility; tied to work hours

High flexibility; income generation is autonomous

**Risk**

Generally stable but subject to job security

Diverse risks depending on the passive source

**Scalability**

Limited scalability; often capped income potential

Higher scalability; potential for exponential growth

**Example**

Monthly salary from employment

Dividend income, rental payments, royalties

### 12\. Open a High-Interest Savings Account

Optimize your savings by opening a high-interest or high yield savings account.

While the returns might not be as substantial as some other investments, this low-risk option provides a safe and passive way to grow your money over time, thanks to compounded interest.

#### How to Get Started:

*   **Research Financial Institutions:** Compare high-interest savings accounts from different banks or financial institutions. Consider factors like interest rates, fees, and account features.
*   **Account Setup**: Open a high-interest savings account with the chosen financial institution.
*   **Automate Savings:** Set up automated transfers to your high-interest savings account and take advantage of compound interest.
*   **Regularly Review Rates:** Keep track of interest rates and be aware of any changes in your account terms. Consider switching to a higher-yielding account if better options become available.

### 13\. Lend Money to Peers

Explore peer-to-peer lending platforms that connect borrowers with individual lenders.

By lending money to peers, you earn interest on the loan, transforming your capital into a passive income source.

#### How to Get Started:

*   **Choose a Peer-to-Peer Platform:** Select a reputable peer-to-peer lending platform that aligns with your lending goals. Research platform fees, borrower profiles, and past performance.
*   **Diversify Your Loans:** Spread your lending across multiple borrowers to minimize risk. Choose loans based on borrower profiles, loan terms, and risk assessment.
*   **Monitor Loan Performance:** Regularly check the performance of your loans through the platform. Be proactive in addressing any late payments or potential defaults.

### 17\. Invest in REITs

Real Estate Investment Trusts (REITs) offer an opportunity to invest in real estate without the hassle of property management.

By purchasing shares in REITs, you become a partial owner of income-generating properties, receiving dividends as your share of the rental income.

#### How to Get Started:

*   **Educate Yourself:** Understand the basics of REITs, including how they operate, the types available (equity, mortgage, hybrid), and their potential returns and risks.
*   **Determine Investment Goals:** Clarify your investment goals, whether they are income generation through dividends or [**capital appreciation**](https://smartasset.com/financial-advisor/capital-appreciation).
*   **Choose a Brokerage:** Open a brokerage account that allows you to invest in REITs.
*   **Diversify Your REIT Portfolio:** Spread your investments across different types of REITs and geographical regions to mitigate risks associated with specific real estate sectors.
