## The Differences Between DA Scores Across SEO Tools

How is domain authority calculated? Because there are many DA checker tools on the market, some of them have a slightly different approach when they calculate your site’s domain authority.

One of the most important differences you should learn about is the variation between linking root domains authority and domain rating.

To make this explanation easier to understand, we’ll discuss Moz and Ahrefs site authority tools.

### **Moz Page Authority Explained**

![](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/MozBar-shows-your-domain-score-and-gives-you-great-insights-on-how-to-improve-it.-1024x546.jpg)

_MozBar shows your domain score and gives you great insights on how to improve it. Source:_ [](https://seopressor.com/blog/how-to-increase-domain-authority/)**[SeoPressor](https://seopressor.com/blog/how-to-increase-domain-authority/)**

To calculate your website domain authority by evaluating multiple factors, including linking root domains and the number of total links, Moz includes:

*   Total number of links
*   The quality of your backlinks
*   The quality of the websites you’re linking to

After [](https://moz.com/products/pro/seo-toolbar)**[MozBar](https://moz.com/products/pro/seo-toolbar)** compares all these values, your website will be assigned a specific value that will be your domain authority score.

However, don’t forget that the values can fluctuate over time.

For example, let’s say that you have a high-quality backlink from a good, medium-sized business. You can’t tell what will happen to that website over time. If they grow their audience and produce high quality content, their authority domain will increase as well. Thus, it will automatically improve your scores too.

On the other hand, if that business sells their website to someone that will turn it into something spammy or shady, it will affect your website’s domain authority values as well.

### **Ahrefs Domain Rating**

![](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Ahrefs-is-one-of-the-most-important-and-popular-SEO-tools-you-can-use-to-check-domain-authority.-1024x659.jpg)

_Ahrefs is one of the most important and popular SEO tools you can use to check domain authority. Source:_ [](https://ahrefs.com/blog/how-to-increase-website-authority/)**[Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/blog/how-to-increase-website-authority/)**

[](https://ahrefs.com/)**[Ahrefs](https://ahrefs.com/)** has developed another domain authority metric that shows you the strength of a certain website called Domain Rating. This metric checks the quality of your website’s backlink profile so it can calculate the rating of your domain. The good news is that these values are similar to Moz’s – the scale goes from 0 to 100 and the higher the score you get, the better for your website.

To calculate your unique rating, Ahrefs thoroughly checks the quality of your website’s backlink profile.

However, Ahrefs considers other 3 important elements when they measure a website DA:

*   The quality of the linking domains on your website
*   The number of the unique domains that have a minimum of 1 do-follow link to your website
*   The number of the unique domains those websites link to

Hence, you’ll have to build high-quality backlinks and referring domains with websites that don’t link to spammy or low-quality websites or inbound links linking back to you. You attract real links only!

### **2\. On-page SEO Optimization**

![](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2021/11/Googles-search-portals-crawl-your-website-faster-if-you-have-excellent-on-page-SEO-optimization.-1024x638.jpg)

Google’s search engine needs a lot of information so they can crawl and rank your website accordingly.

To make sure they rank your website correctly, you need to offer as much information as possible, so make sure you optimize your web page and its elements!

#### **Titles And Descriptions**

Make sure that you create relevant [](https://onesearchpro.my/meta-title-description/)**[meta titles and descriptions](https://onesearchpro.my/meta-title-description/)** for every page on your website.

Include relevant keywords so your visitors and search engines will know exactly what the website or your blog posts are about.

#### **Craft Proper And Relevant Headings**

Headings make your content more scannable because they guide the reader through the main point of the article. Use H2, H3, bulleted list, or any other helpful organizational system.

#### **Use Accurate URL Structure/ Permalinks**

Search engines crawl your website quicker if you use adequate permalinks or URL structures. Besides, your website visitors use URLs structures to navigate quicker on your website, so come up with an intuitive internal link structure that is easy to follow.

Whenever you post a video or an image, optimize those elements too by adding alt and title attributes.

#### **Include Main Keywords’ Variations**

Keyword stuffing is when you use the same keywords over and over again in an attempt to rank higher in SERPs. It looks unprofessional, spammy, and it gives an unnatural flow to your content.

This is a black hat SEO technique that should be avoided because Google and other search engines will think you abuse their algorithms to rank high and they will penalize you.

### **4\. Optimize Your Technical SEO Elements As Well**

Technical SEO is all about those tedious, hard-to-remember metrics that you’d wish you wouldn’t have to handle.

It is related to website and server optimization and it’s crucial because you do it solely for search engines crawlers. One example of this is none other than the broken link building technique.

So what can you do to improve your technical SEO? Here’s a few tips!

#### **Register With Google Search Console**

Google Search Console gives you constant reports about your performances. Based on them, you can optimize any error that might appear on your website.

#### **Create and Submit an XML Sitemap**

An XML sitemap is a file that has all your website’s important pages. By submitting this sitemap, Google can crawl your website easily and efficiently.

In this file, you should include elements like website pages, non-canonical pages, share via email URLs, or reply to comment URLs.

#### **Fix Crawling Issues**

These issues appear when search engines can’t crawl your website pages properly. Just a few examples of issues you might experience are 404 Not Found errors, duplicate pages, using HTTP instead of HTTPS, or robots.txt failure.

#### **Check The Robots.txt File**

Even though Google announced that GoogleBot will no longer obey a robots.txt directive, you might still want to check this file’s setting and make sure it’s working properly. To help you out with it, Google made a [](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/6062598?hl=en)**[quick guide](https://support.google.com/webmasters/answer/6062598?hl=en)** on how you can test your robots.txt file.

The difference between HTTP and HTTPS is the little S that’s very important. Using HTTPS boosts your brand’s credibility because it secures your data-in-transit, and protects your site from data breaches or phishing.

## Frequently Asked Questions on Website Domain Authority

### 1\. What is Domain Authority?

Domain authority is a tool created by Moz that predicts how high your website might rank on Google. The higher the score, the higher chances are for authoritative sites to rank well in Google search results.

### **2\. Is Domain Authority A Ranking Factor?**

No, Google doesn’t consider domain authority a ranking component. However, it’s a good method to see how good your on-site SEO is and how well you can network with other relevant businesses in your niche.

### **3\. What Is A Good Domain Rating Score?**

A good domain authority score or rating is somewhere between 50-60, and anything that’s above 60 is considered an excellent DA value.

However, keep an eye on your competitors and always aim to have a better domain authority score than them. For example, if their score is 70, try to improve your website’s DA to 80.

### **4\. How Can I Raise My DA?**

A simple answer to “how to increase my domain authority” – is to try to connect as much as possible with other influential people in your business.

Cold outreach strategies are a great way to find blogs to pitch for guest posting. Building link-worthy and high-quality DA backlinks to your website will raise your brand authority.

### **5\. How Often Should I Check My DA?**

Moz updates its domain authority metrics monthly, and the good news is that website owners can check your DA scores for free using the MOZ Domain Authority Checker.

### **6\. Domain Authority Vs Page Authority**

When we’re talking about page authority, it means that we’re strictly referring to the strength of that specific page (About Us page, a certain blog post, the home page, and so on). However, domain authority includes all the pages and subdomains available on your website.

### **7\. Why Is Domain Authority Important?**

Domain authority speaks volumes about your potential as a business. Many potential buyers will pay attention to your score because it’s living proof of how trustworthy your business is.
