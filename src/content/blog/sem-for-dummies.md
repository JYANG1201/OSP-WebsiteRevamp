## What is SEM for Dummies? Here’s An Overview

As mentioned previously, SEM stands for [**Search Engine Marketing**](https://onesearchpro.my/sem/). SEM basics involves using a search engine like Google to drive traffic to your business website or social media pages.

An SEM campaign involves paid advertising in order to increase your company’s visibility online.

There are two basic strategies involved in SEM tactics, namely pay per click (PPC) and search engine optimization (SEO).

If you’re a beginner in Google SEM, these are the two strategies that you will have to familiarize yourself with. Apart from this, there’s also pay per impression (PPI). [\[1\]](https://smallbusiness.chron.com/ppi-marketing-47185.html)

### Pay Per Click (PPC)

This is a type of advertising where a business owner pays a specific fee for advertisements for their website link to appear at the top of the web search results list. The fee is based on the number of clicks the link is to receive. [\[2\]](https://digitalmarketinginstitute.com/blog/2-key-formulas-for-understanding-your-ppc-data)

For example, a company pays _RM300_ for an ad that will stay up on Google for 500 clicks.

After the ad receives 500 clicks from curious users, the created ad will finish its run and be removed.

This is the basics of PPC for dummies. The same can be done for Facebook ad campaigns.

The usage of SEO is pretty straightforward. It means incorporating highly searched for keywords into the content of your site.

These highly relevant keywords will help your site or social media post appear higher and more prominently in a search list.

## Understanding The Technical Terms in Search Engine Marketing: SEM Glossary That You Should Know

SEM is a concept that involves several terminologies that you may be unfamiliar with. In order to understand what is SEM better, it’s also good to know what these terms mean.

### SEO Keywords

SEO Keywords are search terms related to your products or services that have a high search rate. It is usually incorporated into your content in order to increase its visibility in a search results list and boost your post’s rankings.

### Advertising Network

A series of websites on which your advertisements will appear. On Google Adwords this is called a Display Ad network. You can choose to customize your advertising network by choosing the types of websites your ads appear on.

### Impression Rates

The number of impressions in the number of times your ads appear on a search engine results when a related term is typed in. [\[3\]](https://www.investopedia.com/terms/i/impression.asp) This is no guarantee that users see your created ads.

### Click-Throughs Rate

The click-through rates of your search engine ads is the number of clicks your ads gets from web users. The higher the better.

### Ad Campaign

A search engine advertising campaign is also called an ad campaign. It involves all the ads you will place on a search engine that has the same theme and message.

### Performance Score

Usually, when you start an account on a search engine, the search engine will give you back end data on how your ads are performing, based on things like click-through rates, landing page traffic, keyword quality and others.

### SEM Strategy

A series of methods you can employ to achieve your SEM objectives. These objectives are to increase your brand’s visibility on search engine results and increase traffic to your business websites.

## SEO vs SEM: What’s The Difference?

Both SEO and SEM are strategies for web-based marketing. The most important thing to keep in mind is that SEO is a subset of SEM.

SEM is a wide term that covers several strategies to market your brand on a particular search engine platform, and SEO is one of these strategies.

Ultimately, both of these terms will lead to a single objective, which is to increase your brand’s visibility in a search results list. This list is the one that emerges after a user types in a related search term.

### The Main Difference

Although some people use the terms interchangeably, both SEO and SEM could stand for different things. Here are the differences between SEO vs SEM:

****SEO –** [](https://onesearchpro.my/seo/)[Search Engine Optimization](https://onesearchpro.my/seo/),** which is the concept of inserting highly searchable keywords into a website’s content to make it appear more prominently in a search engine results list.

This is an organic marketing strategy, and does not involve any type of paid advertising to push the website to a higher position.

The traffic that SEO generates is therefore organic traffic and generated without any advertisement. Instead, it is the clever use of related search terms to your products, locality or service that makes it more visible.

**SEM –** Search Engine Marketing, which includes the concept of paying for your site to appear higher in the list and more visibly on a search engine.

This is done through a system called pay per click, where business owners pay to have their websites promoted for a specific number of clicks from users.

SEM is therefore considered inorganic marketing, which means it is pushed higher in visibility by being made into an advertisement.

### SEO vs SEM – Which Is Better?

The answer to this is actually, _why not both_.

SEO and SEM practices should be carried out hand in hand while trying to make your websites stand out and lead more users to discovering your business.

Having a website and multiple social media accounts is not enough, as you must be able to push your created content to the masses, both organically and non-organically.

### 1\. Keyword Identification

As mentioned previously, keyword identification is the crux of SEM. This step involves identification of various important phrases and words associated with your business.

In order to understand which words are the most sought after, you can refer to tools provided by the search engine itself. For Google, there’s the free to use [](https://trends.google.com/trends/?geo=MY)[**Google Trends**](https://trends.google.com/trends/?geo=MY) of the subscription based [](https://ads.google.com/)[**Google Ads**](https://ads.google.com/).

The more highly they trend, the more you should use them in your web copy or content.

### 2\. Determining Solid SEM Goals

Starting an SEM campaign without solid objectives is like going on a plane without a destination in mind.

Creating goals allows you to better strategize your SEM campaign, and determine the details that will go into the campaign.

_Goals should be set in a stepwise manner_ – this means that you should set small and reasonable goals first before setting larger ones.

For example, if you’re a small business selling homemade food, you should aim for targeting about 50 ad clicks for the first few months, rather than 1000 or more.

### 5\. Landing Page Optimization

A good website is one where the landing pages are easy and quick to load. This means a minimal amount of pop-ups, videos, animation and other things that take time to load.

Information should be presented with as little text as possible, and links should lead to other pages containing information.

You want a landing page that’s very direct to the point, clean and loads fast.

This is because the loading time of pages will determine important metrics of user engagement, like bounce rate, time spent on pages and number of unique visitors.

If your landing page is cluttered and is difficult to load, this will discourage visitors from staying long.

Less traffic to your pages could also mean a lower conversion to sales rate from your site.
