“It’s possible to commit no mistakes and still lose.” These words by Picard from Star Trek hold true even in the realms of SEO. You might’ve everything properly optimized, following every rule in the book and yet you’ll be bewildered by negative results for the site you’re trying to rank. 

Well, don’t beat yourself up because:-

1.  This might not be your fault
2.  This could be the works of negative SEO 

Indeed, negative SEO exists – this black hat SEO approach basically refers to a strategy to bring down another website’s presence on the internet, be it in terms of keyword rankings or brand visibility. 

This is done with malicious intent usually towards direct competitors, so that at the end of the day, the perpetrator’s website is the one ranking on the first page of Google while the other websites get beaten down or kicked out of the SERP into oblivion.

Sounds serious? No doubt, it could pose a big threat but understanding negative SEO in detail will help you prevent or navigate out of the circumstance. Here’s what you’ll learn from the article: 

*   The different types of negative SEO methods like malicious link building, fake reviews, and content scraping. We’ll also be covering how these methods are carried out as well and how they can cripple your website’s ranking on SERP
*   The telltale signs of your website being attacked by negative SEO approaches. We’ll be including symptoms like sudden rankings drop and unusual backlink activity
*   The preventive and precautionary measures you should take like site auditing and backlink monitoring. 
*   Real-life cases of negative SEO happening from Kinsta.com and Jellyfish. We’ll also discuss the remedy options and the long-term effects.

## **What is Negative SEO?** 

Recognized by some as “Google Bowling,” or adverse SEO represents a darker side of search engine optimization where instead of bolstering one’s online presence, the aim is to harm a competitor’s. Despite advancements in Google’s algorithm and its efforts to devalue irrelevant and low-quality links, the threat of negative SEO still lurks.

## **The Types of Negative SEO Tactics and Techniques**

Negative SEO is not a one-trick pony. The darker side of SEO encompasses a set of underhanded methods aimed at damaging a competitor’s search engine rankings. Common negative SEO techniques include the creation of toxic backlinks and various forms of website sabotage.

Here are some examples of tactics used:

### Malicious Link Building

Malicious link-building is an underhanded tactic intended to undermine your website’s search engine rankings. This form of negative SEO involves the creation of numerous spammy and low-quality links pointing to your site.

Looking into spammy links, attackers tend to build a large number of artificial links using irrelevant or over-optimized anchor text making them irrelevant. The brothers and sisters to this link-spamming include linking your site to and from networks known for distributing low-quality links and distributing these links to random sites and comment sections.

Fortunately, it’s actually not too difficult to notice these malicious link-building behavior. The key characteristics of these links are their anchor texts are keyword-rich; unnatural and overused. They also have questionable domains where they are placed on irrelevant sites with poor reputations.

Ultimately, the perpetrator’s objective is to convince the search engines to penalize your site, consequently making it drop in ranking and visibility.  Engage with **[Google’s disavow tool](https://onesearchpro.my/google-disavow-links/)** to communicate the illegitimacy of these links to search engines, safeguarding your site’s standing.

### Content Scraping

Content scraping is a technique where someone copies material from your website and publishes it elsewhere without authorization. The process typically involves the use of bots that crawl your site, identify content, and replicate it across various domains.

Content scraping occurs through various methods. Firstly, automated bots are programmed to search the internet and gather data, which is then duplicated on other websites. Secondly, individuals can manually copy content and republish it without consent. Lastly, specialized software tools are utilized to automate the scraping process.

As for its impacts, content scraping, imagine this: when Google’s algorithms are programmed to only show one version of the content in search results, which can cause some trouble. 

First off, search engines might have a hard time figuring out where the original content came from, so your site’s authority could get a little diluted. Plus, if search engines think your site is a copycat, you might face some SEO penalties. To top it off, you could lose some precious web traffic as users get redirected to the site with the copied content.

To prevent this from happening, it’s important to take proactive measures. Start by monitoring your content regularly using plagiarism detection tools to identify unauthorized copies. 

Legal action is also an option against those who scrape your content without permission. Additionally, implement technical solutions on your website, such as CAPTCHAs and IP blocking, to deter scraping activities.

### Fake Reviews

Fake reviews are intentionally false or misleading assessments posted on the internet. They can be detrimental to a business by distorting the public perception and influencing search engine rankings. Typically, these insincere evaluations fall into two categories; positive and negative fake reviews.

![Spotting fake reviews | Negative SEO | One Search Pro Marketing](https://lh7-us.googleusercontent.com/KpymzSlujHw-h8aRkGcRpPj133gkM_L_xu-OT5qyWSolBaq1plwnTYBymHnD6ivZlymmmZ1Q8inybbXzKnk_ddMXg8OSJ4CQ1IOBvsvLbIuAgET_XwAiOIqgiU50kboTymQV-MU7fc1oF7bw4AVa2ak)

_Fake reviews tend to be posted by dubious profiles and are ambiguous in their identity. Image source:_ **_ReviewTrackers_**

The positive fake reviews are intended to boost a company’s reputation artificially while the negative ones are aimed at harming competitors or retaliating. Some decent ways to spot these spoof reviews are pattern observation and reviewer verification.

Through pattern observation, you would want to look for reviews that are repetitious, use similar language, or are posted in a short time frame. As for reviewer verification, you would want to investigate the reviewers’ profiles for authenticity, including their history and the range of their reviews.

### Unwarranted Hotlinking

The art of unwanted hotlinking is quite subtle in the sense that it uses technical sophistication to make your website’s rankings tank. It often goes unnoticed and if left unattended can lead to slow loading time and unresponsiveness of the said site.

Hotlinking commonly occurs when you link directly to media on someone else’s server, such as images, from your website without their permission. This practice uses the host’s bandwidth every time your page is loaded. If your site attracts significant traffic, this can strain the host server, potentially increasing their operating costs and slowing down their website.

When the effects of hotlinking take effect, bandwidth theft can occur when it utilizes the host site’s allocated bandwidth each time an image is loaded on your site. This can result in increased costs for websites paying for bandwidth, leading to higher hosting bills.

Hence, if you wish to safeguard your website against hotlinking, consider implementing protective measures. One approach is to implement a Content Delivery Network (CDN) with built-in hotlink protection. Additionally, you can modify your server’s .htaccess file by accessing it via FTP to prevent unauthorized access.

## **Preventive Measures**

Now that we’ve ascertained that negative SEO is bad for your website, the million-dollar question is—how do you prevent it from happening?

To safeguard your website from negative SEO and harmful backlinks, it’s essential to be proactive. Start by setting up alerts in Google Webmaster Tools to notify you of potential issues. Here are key strategies to protect your website:

### Enhancing Security and Regular Audits

Now that we’ve established the dangers of negative SEO, it is imperative to safeguard your website from potential hacking attempts. Keep your software up to date, use strong passwords, and consider implementing security plugins to fortify your website’s defenses against negative SEO attacks.

### Creating Quality Content and Be Aware of Content Theft

A proactive measure against negative SEO to ensure your content remains high quality and unique is super important. With the correct precautions, your valuable content will become less susceptible to negative SEO tactics, consequently helping establish your site’s credibility.

Preventing and being aware of content theft is also vital to protect your website from negative SEO. Duplicate content can harm your search engine ranking, so it’s important to set up alerts to detect content scraping and take necessary steps to have plagiarized content removed, thereby safeguarding your website from potential negative SEO tactics.

## **Signs and Symptoms of Negative SEO**

Nipping it in the bud before it blooms is arguably the best defense you can have in preventing negative SEO from harming your site. Recognizing the occurrence of a negative SEO attack can be critical for the health of your website. As the owner of a website, here are the signs:

### Sudden Rankings Drop

When your website experiences a sudden drop in search engine rankings, it’s often a sign of negative SEO at play or other issues that warrant your immediate attention. This involves malicious actions taken by others to harm your website’s organic search performance.

Usually, a sudden drop in rankings is attributed to a number of reasons. For one, Google might penalize your site if your site has violated Google’s Webmaster Guidelines, such as cloaking techniques or user-generated spam. Link spamming is also a tripwire you should be wary about because spammy backlinks birthed from negative SEO attacks can also cause a drop.

![Checking a sudden drop in performance using Ahrefs Site Explorer function | Negative SEO | One Search Pro Marketing](https://lh7-us.googleusercontent.com/hzD3LKEzs8MLMX7YOAItdIKVRQUNF82exwQPN946bYKDvosrbd2B1jl9uPKTFXPCCyrARhFTeb5rKIpykkBskMFVhxOCpkeUZS05dtnb_zeLCzXbyl9N7RBzxq62TJ7Rjl2EJxdW6vZP-M5-pn5oTZ0)

_Ahrefs showing a drastic drop in traffic using its Site Explorer function. Image source:_ **_Ahrefs.com_**

If you have encountered such situations, do not be alarmed, there is still a chance to rectify them. Foremost, fire up your Google Search Console and review any messages regarding manual penalties. Another remedy process also includes a backlink audit where you examine your link profile for unusual activity and disavow spammy or low-quality links.

### Unusual Backlink Activity

When monitoring your website’s backlink profile, be vigilant about negative SEO backlinks. The common flags that website owners should be aware of are the sudden influx of backlinks and the quality of backlinks.

Tools like Semrush easily let you check the quality of your backlinks:

![Checking toxicity score using Semrush | Negative SEO | One Search Pro Marketing](https://lh7-us.googleusercontent.com/kEqLZDZyHAZ2Aa-JXg2_PRK3H1BmL4edQ6x3E2R_U9WQHu_5AXktGpao2qQt8pxuhGssPYF72Vj7FxwxjirKGbVDXQEOZH-iB8vpOloZjxnZm_2LG88QiD8mUKzrg54-OejNwcdXcfM3b4jLPUJ9KsU)

It’s really unusual for a large number of new backlinks to appear over a short period of time. If that’s the case, it should be a cause of concern, especially if they’re from irrelevant or unrelated sites. 

The credibility of your link domains is also important in assessing quality. A surge of low-quality and spammy links is a sign of an attack to undermine your SEO efforts.
