#### **3\. UI/ UX**

User Experience (UX) emphasizes a deep understanding of users in terms of what they need, what they are looking for, their abilities, limitations, and how they’ll respond to a design.

It’s a process of creating a site that brings enjoyment to use and at ease to navigate.

UX focuses on understanding a person’s behavior and experience while engaging and guiding them through the site.

If your website chooses to use large images and flashy videos that slow down loading times, visitors will get frustrated and leave the site, and that’s the last thing you want. 

![](https://lh4.googleusercontent.com/kmgBs4YTg1bBLL0a10w21li5L4q31EpR_DiwBe_4Zx5IJjwna_1-yfL9o1Rxtg3eParyz2F3OA609SgjaZQbAn2g2ZH4xFc_aeGkNwMSxd9gliW7F3GBmoRv0UsE7n9A3oK0HKQ)

_One of web designer duties is to create designs with great user experience with good UX web design. Source_ [_Trone Brand Energy_](https://www.tronebrandenergy.com/blog/10-examples-good-user-experience-ux)

**Best UX practice includes:**

*   Understand your audience. Once you’ve got a clear idea of your site’s audience, you can find out their needs and wants and create the perfect design that will fulfill their desires. 
*   Be trustworthy, approachable, and transparent with human interaction preferred over machine interaction.
*   Creating a scannable site page will appeal to your audience.
*   Understand that the user wants clarity and simplicity. Don’t make it hard to find action buttons. 

While UX focuses on the comprehensive features of web page design, UI manages the specific elements of how users interact with the site. UI is often considered as the menus and buttons that make up all the navigation on the site. These come together to create a unified experience and an effortless web interface design. 

**Best UI involves various aspects of usability that include:**

*   The simple and straightforward navigation interface
*   Call to action button that helps people to do what you want them to do in a brief amount of time
*   Intuitive and repeatable steps and actions that your audience can do and understand when they use your design

#### **4\. Responsive Design**

Responsive web design is a way to arrange and organize a website to automatically scale its elements and contents to complement the screen size on which it’s viewed.

It maintains images from being too large than the screen width and prevents the site’s visitors on mobile devices to easily navigate the site to read the content. Responsive design is an essential element of web development.

The directions for responsive design ensure that HTML, CSS, and JavaScript components like text buttons, search bars are easy to use and accessible.

#### **9\. Content Management System – CMS**

Just as the name indicates, a content management system, also known as CMS, is a software application or set of intertwined programs that let you manage and create your website’s digital content.

CMS gives you the ability to upload, delete and edit content from the website without understanding CSS, HTML, or other coding languages. 

Knowing how to use CMS correctly helps you organize content smoothly.

For content that requires regular updates like recipes, blog posts, or events, a CMS will help streamline the process. 

Thus, to be a good web designer, you need to familiarize yourself with CMS’s basic features. A CMS also makes custom web design, embedding graphics, video-audio files, and inserting programming codes easy.

### ****_Soft Skills_****

#### **15\. Communication Skills**

Good communication skills are the key to success in work, relationships, and life. Without effective communication, a message can be misunderstood, leading to errors in judgement.

Or even the worst disasters if misinterpreted or poorly delivered. 

A good web designer understands that good communication skills are crucial.

It’s vital to get your point across and stay in touch all the time of the project.

Nobody likes to be left hanging without knowing what the other parties are thinking or planning on the other side.

You need to regularly update your client on the project’s progress and explain to them briefly your ideas and creations so they comprehend your plan and can provide you with feedback. 

#### **16\. Time Management**

Everyone gets the same 24 hours in a single day.

As a web designer, there are tons of moving parts to your responsibility like planning, coding, designing, strategizing, and the list goes on. While you can’t possibly add more time back into the day, you can find ways to maximize your workflow to get some time back. In business, time is money, and as a web designer, every single minute is valuable. 

While your schedule can be quite hectic, it’s vital to take care of your health and wellness. When you give your body the best environment to flourish, you’ll see it work more in your favor. 

Another key to time management is having a clear and defined list of tasks each day to keep you organized.

Make sure your goals are measurable, doable, realistic, and timely.

This will set the expectation bar for you and the client, so your plans are transparent.

#### **18\. Discipline**

Discipline is a must when you want to ensure productivity and meeting deadlines.

Make every minute of your working time useful and avoid distractions at all times.

Don’t check on social media when you’re working.

One key to self-discipline is to follow a strict daily schedule.

Set a time to accomplish a task, and when the time is up, move on. If it’s not the time to check on your email, don’t do it.

Interpersonal skills are characteristics you depend on when you interact with other people.

It covers some scenarios where communication and cooperation are essential.

These skills involve the potential to communicate and build relationships with others.

Often known as people skills, it tends to incorporate your natural personality characteristics and how you have learned to handle certain situations. Good interpersonal skills can help you score more projects and positively impact your career development.

#### **20\. Self-Awareness**

Recognizing your limitations does pay off.

Never promise the client something that you can’t deliver.

Please don’t accept the project and hope to learn how to do it along the way as it will only affect your reputation and credibility. 

Receive a job when you are confident that you can deliver and are knowledgeable to do it.

Accepting a job merely to create a name is not worth it.

It’s okay to decline an offer for the reason of lack of knowledge in a specific area.

You’ll do the client a favor by not wasting their time and money.

When you’re ready, you can contact the client again, and they’ll be sure happy to work with you as you’re honest about yourself.

In actuality, feedback is around us all the time.

By asking for feedback, it can help you stay motivated to perform your task better.

And feedback from clients can be used to help build better working relations.
