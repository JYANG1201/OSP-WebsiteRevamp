This is because many Malaysians are active on the internet and especially on socmed Malaysia, and many are looking for great content, which influencers can provide.

This is even more so if the influencer is presenting content in specific languages. This means a Malay Influencer in Malaysia can easily target the Malay market if that is your target market, or if you are targeting the Malaysian Chinese, then you should look for a Malaysian Chinese influencer.

According to Malaysian Digest Malaysian social media influencers earn anywhere between RM 9 million to RM 24 million per year, but this is only for Instagram pictures. This amount also depends on their level of influence and the campaigns they have run.
