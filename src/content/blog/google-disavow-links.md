If you’re looking to improve your website’s search engine ranking, you’ll need to focus on building high-quality backlinks. These links are essential for SEO because they signal to Google that other websites consider your content valuable and worth linking to.

However, not all the backlinks are created equal. Some links can actually hurt your site’s ranking, which is where the **Google Disavow** tool comes in.

The **Disavow** tool allows you to tell Google which backlinks to ignore when evaluating your site’s ranking. This can be useful if you’ve acquired spammy or low-quality links in the past, or if you’re concerned that a competitor is trying to harm your site’s ranking by building bad links to your site. By disavowing these links, you can help ensure that your site isn’t penalized by Google for having bad links.

In this article, we’ll take a closer look at the Google Disavow tool and when it makes sense to use it. We’ll also cover some best practices for using the tool effectively and avoiding common mistakes.

Whether you’re a seasoned [](/seo/)**[SEO](/seo/)** pro or just getting started with link building, you’ll find plenty of useful information in this guide.

## **Why to Disavow Links?**

The action of disavowing backlinks is an important process that can help improve your website’s ranking on Google. There are several reasons why you might want to **disavow links:**

1.  **Low-quality links:** If your website has links from low-quality or spammy websites, it can negatively impact your website’s ranking. Disavowing these links can help prevent this from happening.
2.  **Penalties:** If your website has received a penalty from Google for having unnatural or spammy links, disavowing those links can help you recover from the penalty.

![Penalties by Google shows in Google Search Console | Google Disavow Links | One Search Pro Marketing
](/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/word-image-44304-3-1.png)

Check frequently your Google Search Console to ensure your site wasn’t affected by the penalty. Source: Ahrefs

1.  **Negative SEO:** Your competitors may try to harm your website’s ranking by creating low-quality or spammy links to your website. Disavowing these links can help prevent this from happening.  
    
2.  **Link Networks:** If you have been involved in a link network or have paid for links, it is important to disavow those links to avoid any penalties.

To know if you need to disavow links, you can use tools like Google Search Console or Ahrefs to identify any low-quality or spammy links pointing to your website. Once you have identified these links, you can create a disavow file and submit it to Google.

It is important to note that disavowing links should not be your first option. You should always try to remove the links manually before disavowing them. Disavowing links should only be done as a last resort.

### **What is a Bad Backlink?**

In simple terms, bad backlinks are links that harm your website’s reputation, either in the eyes of users or as determined by Google itself. These links can potentially interfere with the calculation of your website’s authority and jeopardize your ranking strategy.

Bad backlinks are usually generated by **spammy websites** or low-quality sites that violate search engine guidelines. These links can harm your website’s rankings, and in severe cases, result in a manual penalty from search engines.

When you have too many bad backlinks pointing to your website, Google may consider your site to be spammy or low-quality, and this can lead to a drop in your website’s ranking.

It’s important to note that you have only limited control over which links point to your site. If you notice any harmful links pointing to your website, it’s crucial to take action to remove or disavow them. This is where Google’s Disavow Links Tool comes in handy.

In summary, bad backlinks are links from low-quality websites or sites that violate search engine guidelines. These links can harm your website’s rankings and reputation, and it’s important to take action to remove or disavow them to protect **[your website’s SEO](/how-to-become-an-seo-expert/)**.

## **What you can do before you disavow the link**

Disavowing links should be the last resort because it can bring damage to your current SEO. Before you **disavow backlinks**, you should try some ways to remove the spam backlinks. This is because the disavow link action is irreversible, and it can take months for Google to process the disavow request.

Here are some steps you can take before disavowing links:

*   **Analyze the backlinks:** Before disavowing links, you should analyze the backlinks to determine which ones are harmful and separate out the good backlinks. You can use tools such as Ahrefs, SEMrush, or Google Search Console to analyze your backlinks. Look for spammy websites that have low domain authority, irrelevant content, or unnatural anchor text.

*   **Contact the website owner:** If you find a harmful backlink, you can contact the website owner and ask them to remove the link. You can use webmaster tools such as Hunter.io or VoilaNorbert to find the email address of the website owner. Send them a polite email requesting them to remove the link and explain why the link is harmful.  
    
*   **Document your efforts:** Before disavowing links, you should document your efforts to remove the harmful backlinks. Keep a record of the emails you sent to the website owners, the responses you received, and the dates of your attempts to remove the links. This documentation can be useful in case you need to prove to Google that you made an effort to remove the harmful backlinks.

## **What Happens When You Disavow Links?**

When you disavow a link, you are essentially telling Google that you do not want that link to be associated with your website. This means that Google will no longer consider that link when determining your website’s ranking in search results.

Disavowing links can be a useful tool for website owners who have received backlinks from spammy websites or have engaged in link-building practices that violate Google’s guidelines. By disavowing these links, you can prevent them from negatively impacting your [](/how-to-drive-traffic-to-your-website/)**[website’s search engine ranking](/how-to-drive-traffic-to-your-website/)**.

Disavowing links should not be your first course of action when dealing with problematic backlinks. Before disavowing a link, you should first attempt to have it removed manually by contacting the website owner or webmaster. Only after these efforts have failed should you consider using the disavow tool.

When using the disavow tool, it is important to be very careful about which links you choose to disavow. Disavowing too many links, or disavowing links that are beneficial to your website, can harm your search engine ranking. Therefore, it is important to only disavow links that are spammy or harmful to your website’s reputation.

Overall, the disavow tool can be a useful tool for website owners looking to improve their search engine ranking. However, it should be used with caution and only after all other options have been exhausted.

### **Is it possible to undo a link disavow?**

Once you disavow a link, it is not permanent. You can undo it by removing the link from your disavow file and re-uploading it to Google Search Console. Google will then process the changes, and the previously disavowed links will be reconsidered.

However, it is important to note that undoing a disavow is not always advisable. When you disavow a link, you are telling Google that you do not want it to consider that link when evaluating your website’s authority and relevance. If you undo a disavow, then that link will once again be considered by Google, and it may harm your SEO results.

![Can you undo your disavowed link | Google Disavow Links | One Search Pro Marketing
](/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/word-image-44304-6.png)

Therefore, before you decide to undo a disavow, you should carefully evaluate the situation. If the link is from a **spammy website** or a low-quality source, then it is probably best to leave it disavowed.

On the other hand, if the link is from a reputable website and it provides value to your website, then you may want to consider undoing the disavow.

It is also important to note that even if you delete the disavow file, there may still be an effect on your SEO results. Google may take some time to recrawl the disavowed links and adjust its evaluation of your website’s backlink profile.

Therefore, it is important to make the disavow decision carefully and only disavow links that are truly harmful to your website’s ranking.

## **How to disavow links in Google Search Console?**

If you have identified spam unwanted [](/seo-backlinks/)**[backlinks](/seo-backlinks/)** to your website that are hurting your search engine rankings, you can use Google’s Disavow Tool to disavow the link and tell Google to ignore those links. Here is how to disavow links in Google Search Console:

![Google Disavow links tool | Google Disavow Tools | One Search Pro Marketing](/wp-content/uploads/2024/02/word-image-44304-7.png)

Disavow unwanted links to your site. Source: Google Search console

1.  Create a list of URLs or domains that you want to disavow. You can use a plain text editor like Notepad or TextEdit to create this list.  
    
2.  Log in to your Google Search Console account and select the website that you want to disavow links for.  
    
3.  Navigate to the “Disavow Links” tool.  
    
4.  Click on the “Disavow Links” button to upload the file containing the list of URLs or domains that you want to disavow.  
    
5.  Review the list of URLs or domains that you have uploaded and make sure that you have not accidentally included any legitimate links.  
    
6.  Click on the “Submit” button to disavow the selected links.  
    

Remember that you should only use the Google Disavow Links tool as a last option after you have tried to remove the spammy links manually. The tool is not a quick fix for bad unwanted backlinks and should be used with caution.

Keep in mind that disavowing links does not guarantee that your website’s search engine rankings will improve. However, it can help to remove the negative impact of spammy links on your website’s search engine rankings.
