## What You Need to Know Before Choosing a Marketplace: 5 Factors You Should Consider For Seller

### 1\. Dashboard for Sellers

A seller dashboard provides an overview of inventory and sales progress. The dashboard should give you a snapshot of all the data you need to run the business, making it an indispensable tool for managing your account.

### 2\. Store Set Up Costs

Some of the most common fees associated with online marketplace transactions are commission fees, listing fees, admin fees, and shipping costs. Each marketplace will have its different fee.

### 3\. Commission Rate

An online marketplace can be an excellent way to sell your used items, but you should understand the rules of the applied commission rate before listing anything. It might be wise to not sell an item for more than what it would cost because the difference in commission will lead to a lower profit margin. Below is the commission rate for each platform:

*   **Shopee –**
    *   Preferred and Prefered Plus sellers: 2%,
    *   Non preferred sellers who managed to sell less than 100: 1%
    *   Sellers with more than 100 completed orders: 0%
    *   \*Shopee Mall seller fee is between 3-5% and 5-6%for overseas sellers.
*   **Lazada –** 1-4 %
*   **Mudah –** 0-10%
*   **Lelong –** 0-10%
*   **Zalora –** 10- 30%
*   **Go Shop –** N/A
*   **PGmall –** 5%
*   **Carousell –** 2.5%
*   **eBay Malaysia** – 1.5% to 15%.
*   **PrestoMall –** Rate varies based on product category
*   **Hermo –** N/A
*   **Qoo10 –** 9-12%
*   **Ezbuy –** N/A

### 4\. Payment Gateway Fee

You should also consider the payment gateway fee charged against the seller for each sale. This is typically a small percentage of the product cost, but it varies from one marketplace to another.

### 5\. Settlement Day

A settlement day describes the days when funds are transferred from buyers to the seller and seller to the platform.
