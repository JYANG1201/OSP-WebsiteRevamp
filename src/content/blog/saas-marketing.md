But first, let’s start with the basics!

SaaS is an acronym that stands for **_Software as a Service_** and it refers to products like apps, online tools, and cloud-computing software. Some examples may include: Netflix, Dropbox, Google Apps, Slack, or DocuSign – they are some of the most popular SaaS companies.

As a rule of thumb, a SaaS product is any cloud-based app that you access via an internet browser instead of downloading it on your device (PC, phone, or console).

## Why Is SaaS Marketing Different?

Knowing the difference between [](https://onesearchpro.my/traditional-vs-online-marketing/)**[traditional vs online marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/traditional-vs-online-marketing/)** will help you a lot, but there’s more to an SaaS marketing proposition than this.

### 1\. Product

SaaS products are difficult to sell because they have no physical presence. And, in many cases, they don’t even make sense to a lot of people.

Sure, everyone understands how Netflix works. But using Adobe Creative Cloud requires a lot more training. You’ll have to present an Adobe certification if you want customers to trust your designer skills.

### 2\. Customer

Buying a pair of shoes or a comfy blanket are things that people want anyway. Thus, creating a digital marketing strategy for tangible products will be easy. But convincing people that they need something they can’t touch is challenging.

Even more, persuading B2B companies that need your product will be ambitious. In general, B2B companies are harder to please because they are more demanding than a regular customer. For instance, you’ll have to implement:

*   a few extra layers of protection against cyber-attacks;
*   a more complex design that satisfies the needs of a company;
*   constant updates and features that are more useful than your competitors.

### 3\. Customer Journey

The hardest part of having an SaaS product is to convince your potential customers (individuals, companies, or both) that your software plays an important part in their daily life.

Persuading people to buy a shirt is easy – all you need to do is to come up with an attractive design and put a fair price on it.

However, if you’re running a **[B2B business](https://wellyx.com/)**, your customers will have to go through multiple stages until you can call your marketing strategy successful. SaaS marketers call these stages the B2B sales funnel, and it looks like this:

*   Build brand awareness (cold outreach, [](https://onesearchpro.my/affiliate-marketing-malaysia/)**[affiliate marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/affiliate-marketing-malaysia/)**, [](https://onesearchpro.my/sem/)**[Search Engine Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/sem/)**)
*   Earn the trust of your potential loyal customers (testimonials, [](https://onesearchpro.my/social-media-content/)**[social media content](https://onesearchpro.my/social-media-content/)**)
*   Engage with your clients (webinars, analytics, tutorials, demos)
*   Sale the service (clear CTAs, create special events, implement surveys, reviews)
*   Retain loyalty (discounts, special offers, flash sales, better updates)

### 4\. Competition

SaaS is a very competitive field. In the last year alone, this segment has grown significantly by 30%.

To stay ahead of the game, you’ll have to research what other services are available on the market and use this information to gain the trust of your possible customers.

For this, you’ll have to focus on both attracting new prospects and improving customer retention. If one of these 2 tactics doesn’t yield effective results, then your software won’t last long on the market.

### 5\. Pricing

Because you can’t make your clients pay a large amount of money right from the start, the ideal SaaS customers will subscribe to a monthly or yearly plan to use your app regularly.

For this reason, the SaaS business has a short sales cycle – under 12 months. [](https://neilpatel.com/blog/how-saas-marketing-is-different/)**[\[1\]](https://neilpatel.com/blog/how-saas-marketing-is-different/)**

When you’re setting the price of your service, you have to segment your customers. The most common segments for SaaS pricing are:

*   Small businesses
*   Medium businesses or agencies
*   Enterprises

Each segment will have a different price and value, so customers will have access to different features. As the price goes higher, they can benefit from more user accounts, better analytics, or more storage.

## How Can You Measure the Success of a SaaS Marketing Strategy?

Because your ideal customers are B2B companies, the SaaS marketing metrics are a bit different than the traditional ones.

Here are 4 crucial marketing KPIs you should track:

### 1\. Customer Churn Rate

According to specialists, here’s the traditional formula you can use to determine your churn rate: [](https://skale.so/saas-marketing/metrics/)**[\[5\]](https://skale.so/saas-marketing/metrics/)**

*   Custom churn rate = \[the existing number of customers at the beginning of a period – the existing number of customers at the end of the period\] / Existing customers at the start of that period.

If you happen to get a churn rate you don’t like, all the answers you need to know are in the marketing strategy:

*   Maybe you need to come up with a better UX
*   The pricing might be too high or too low
*   The prospects don’t enjoy the product too much

After you get your answers, you can create a better SaaS marketing strategy that improves your customer churn rate.

### 2\. Customer Lifetime Value (CLV)

Because one main goal of an SaaS company is to increase customer retention, you need to focus on a metric that shows you how your relationship with the customers is evolving over the years.

To make it easier to understand, let’s take Netflix as an example. CLV will measure:

*   How often do their customers use their membership
*   When they stopped renewing their subscription: after 1 year, 3 years, and so on.
*   What made them come back: better pricing, new TV series.

Once you have a better understanding of how customers use your product and for how long they do so, you can improve your SaaS marketing strategies.

![](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/One-of-the-most-important-marketing-strategies-is-to-lower-SaaS-customer-churn.-1024x631.jpg)

_One of the most important marketing strategies is to lower SaaS customer churn. Source:_ [](https://clevertap.com/blog/customer-lifetime-value/)**[Clever Tap](https://clevertap.com/blog/customer-lifetime-value/)**

### 3\. Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC)

These metrics refer to the cost related to gaining a new client whether we’re talking about the costs of the resources used to attract a new customer.

The formula you can use to determine CAC is as follows:

*   CAC = Marketing costs and Sales / Number of new customers

However, keep in mind the marketing costs includes every expense for a certain period such as:

*   Marketing strategies costs
*   Advertising
*   Bonuses
*   Managers and marketers salaries

### 4\. CAC-to-LTV Ratio

The ideal CAC-to-LTV should be 3:1, meaning that the value of a customer should be 3x times more than the cost of gaining them.

If the ratio is too high, let’s say 6:1, that means you’re spending too little and you should improve your marketing strategies. Otherwise, your business will fail. If your metrics are close to a 1:1 ratio, it means that you’re spending too much.
