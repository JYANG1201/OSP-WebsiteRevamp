## What is Technical SEO Audit?

Technical SEO refers to the process of optimizing your website for the crawling and indexing phase, with the goal of improving organic rankings.

It is a critical component of the larger SEO strategy, focusing on enhancing the infrastructure of your website to facilitate search engine spiders. Understanding “**what is technical SEO**” is essential for achieving and maintaining high search engine rankings.

The technical SEO basics involve improving website speed, ensuring mobile-friendliness, creating an XML sitemap, and implementing structured data.

![Mobile Usability Pages on Google Search Console | Technical SEO | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/word-image-43787-2.png)

These fundamental elements contribute to a website’s overall health, ensuring it is easily navigable and understandable for both users and search engines.

In essence, technical SEO is about creating a solid foundation for your website, ensuring freedom for search engine bots to crawl and index your pages without barriers.

This, in turn, allows your audience the same liberty to access and navigate your website seamlessly, ultimately enhancing their experience and your website’s performance.

## Technical SEO: How Do I Get Started?

### 1\. Set Up a Google Search Console Account

Creating a **Google Search Console** account is an essential step in optimizing your site, as it provides valuable insights into your website’s performance and visibility on Google’s search engine.

![Setting Up a Google Search Console Account | Technical SEO | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/word-image-43787-3.png)

If you crave the freedom to understand and enhance your site’s SEO indexing, the Google Search Console tool is pivotal.

Through Google Search Console, you can:

*   Monitor your site’s technical SEO performance
*   Get alerts for SEO technical issues
*   View and analyze your website’s SEO indexing status
*   Utilize data to improve your technical SEO optimization

### 2\. Create a Robots.txt File

A robots.txt file serves as a guide for search browsers, directing them on which web pages or sections of your website should not be processed or scanned.

As an integral part of technical optimization, creating a well-structured robots.txt file is one of the technical SEO best practices that can significantly enhance your website’s search engine visibility.

It provides you with the freedom to control how search browsers interact with your site. By excluding certain pages from being indexed, you can focus the attention of search engines on the most important content, improving your website’s ranking potential.

![Creating a Robots.txt File | Technical SEO | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/word-image-43787-4.png)

It’s essential to be meticulous and analytical when creating your robots.txt file, as incorrect directives can lead to significant indexing issues. Thus, a well-executed robots.txt file is a critical aspect of technical SEO optimization.

### 3\. Build an XML and HTML Sitemap

In the realm of website development, building both XML and HTML sitemaps significantly enhances the navigability of a site, subsequently improving its visibility to search browsers.

These sitemaps, serving as a roadmap, guide search engine bots to index the vital pages of the website, thereby boosting its SEO ranking.

*   **_XML sitemaps_**_:_ Primarily for search engines, these sitemaps list the URLs for a site and include additional metadata about each URL.
*   **_HTML sitemaps_**_:_ Designed for users, these sitemaps assist with site navigation, particularly in large or complex websites.
*   **_Sitemaps and SEO_**_:_ Sitemaps enhance SEO by making it easier for search engines to find all pages on your site.
*   **_Creating sitemaps_**_:_ Various online **technical SEO tools** are available to generate sitemaps, ensuring a user-friendly experience.

### 4\. Use HTTPS

Transitioning to HTTPS from HTTP is a crucial step in enhancing website security, as it ensures encrypted communication and secure identity of your web servers.

HTTPS, known as Hyper Text Transfer Protocol Secure, implements an SSL/TLS protocol to provide a secure connection, protecting data integrity and confidentiality.

It’s a key component in technical SEO, boosting website ranking as search engines prioritize secure sites.

### 5\. Establish an SEO-Friendly Navigation

Establishing an SEO-friendly navigation structure is paramount for ensuring that search engines can crawl and index your website effectively, thereby increasing its visibility and usability for users.

A well-structured, intuitive navigation system not only delights your visitors but also helps search browsers understand your site’s hierarchy.

Use concise, descriptive navigation labels that reflect popular keyword searches. Incorporate breadcrumb navigation as it helps users trace their path from the page they’re currently viewing to the home page.

Keep your navigation structure shallow to ensure that all pages are easily accessible. Avoid using JavaScript or Flash for critical elements of your navigation.

### 6\. Follow an SEO-friendly URL Structure

Following an SEO-friendly URL structure is another crucial aspect of enhancing a website’s visibility and user experience.

It allows for easier navigation and comprehension for both users and search engines. By ensuring that each URL is concise, relevant, and logically organized, your site becomes more accessible and understandable.

Keywords play a significant role in this process. They signal the content’s theme to search engines and users alike.

### 7\. Create a Mobile-Friendly Website

After optimizing your website’s URLs, it’s vital to focus on making your site mobile-friendly.

In our increasingly digital world, mobile internet usage has overtaken desktop usage. Google’s mobile-first indexing is evidence of the shift towards mobile usability.

![Google Mobile-Friendly Test | Technical SEO | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/word-image-43787-6.png)

A mobile-friendly website not only enhances user experience but also boosts SEO performance.

To develop a mobile-friendly website, consider the following:

*   **_Design for mobile-first:_** Prioritize mobile design in your web development strategy.
*   **_Speed up your site:_** Mobile users expect quick load times.
*   **_Make it touch-friendly:_** Ensure buttons and links are large enough to touch on small screens.
*   **_Use responsive design:_** Your site should adapt to any screen size.

Strive to provide a seamless and enjoyable mobile experience for your users, granting them the freedom to browse at their convenience.

### 8\. Optimize Page Speed

In the realm of mobile-first design, it is essential to address the page speed as it significantly impacts the user’s ability to browse freely and enjoyably.

A slow-loading site not only frustrates users but also hampers the site’s ranking on search browsers like Google. Page speed optimization, therefore, becomes a crucial aspect of technical SEO.

To ensure smooth browsing, webmasters should employ tactics such as compressing images, leveraging browser caching, and reducing server response time. It is also beneficial to minimize HTTP requests and eliminate render-blocking JavaScript.

![Google Page Speed Insights | Technical SEO | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/word-image-43787-7.png)

Implementing these strategies can help improve the user experience, increase engagement, and boost search rankings.

Ultimately, an optimized page speed facilitates a freedom-oriented browsing experience that users yearn for.

### 9\. Improve Internal Linking

Having optimized your page speed, let’s now turn our attention to another critical aspect of technical SEO: improving internal linking.

Internal links are hyperlinks that point to different pages on the same domain.

![Internal Linking Between Pages on a Website | Technical SEO | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/word-image-43787-8.jpeg)

They are essential for a website’s SEO performance as they help in distributing page authority and ranking power among pages on your site.

Here are four key strategies to improve your internal linking structure:

*   **_Cultivating a rich network of links_:** This involves linking relevant pages together to increase the value of each page.
*   **_Strategic placement of links_:** Positioning links in high-visibility areas can enhance user engagement.
*   **_Using descriptive anchor text_:** Anchor text should provide clear context about the linked page.
*   **_Balancing link distribution_:** Ensuring that no single page hoards all the internal links.

### 10\. Add Structured Data to Relevant Pages

Structured data, another critical element to consider, can be added to relevant pages to enhance their visibility on search results pages and improve click-through rates.

This type of data communicates specific information about a webpage to the search engine, allowing it to understand the page’s content. It can include details such as product pricing, reviews, or business hours.

By integrating structured data, website owners gain more control over how their content is presented in search results, potentially leading to higher visibility. Analyzing and optimizing this feature is a crucial part of technical SEO.

In the pursuit of online freedom, structured data provides a way for entrepreneurs and businesses to stand out in the crowded digital landscape.
