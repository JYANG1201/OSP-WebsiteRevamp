## What is Tactical Planning? Tactical Planning Definition Explained

Tactical planning is an essential part of any business plan. It’s all about creating and implementing short-term and long-term strategies that will help achieve objectives.

This will help the business make strategic decisions based on the current situation, and to adjust plans as necessary. Tactical planning also helps companies get a better sense of where they are currently situated, and what needs to be done in order to reach their business goals.

Tactical planning can also be used when a team or corporation needs to react quickly to certain issues or circumstances. For instance, a business needs to adapt to fulfill a specific request from a client who wants to start a TikTok platform.

Using **tactical management**, the business can look into a number of small steps, such as creating an account and looking into [](https://onesearchpro.my/tiktok-small-businesses-ideas/)**[tiktok small business ideas](https://onesearchpro.my/tiktok-small-businesses-ideas/)** to help fulfill this request.

This makes it different from strategic planning vs operational planning, which both function differently. Here’s how they differ from each other:

### Strategic VS Tactical Planning

The difference between **tactical VS strategic** planning is based on the type of goals and how long it takes to achieve it.

Strategic planning focuses on the long term, with overarching objectives that a company or person wishes to accomplish. Tactical planning helps support this strategic plan by specifying the immediate tasks and actions that need to be taken.

### Tactical VS Operational Planning

Operational plans focus on the organization itself and how it will typically carry out actions in order to achieve the organization’s objective. On the other hand, **tactical plans** ask specific questions about what has to happen in order to achieve a strategic goal.

The goal of technical planning is to ensure that current and future needs are met while minimizing risks.

This is why the process of technical planning includes understanding the environment and situation the business is in, gathering information of available tools and resources, as well as understanding the requirements of the goal.

With a clearly defined strategy and a sound technical plan, you can expect the plan to be successfully implemented.

## Elements Involved In a Tactical Plan

There are a few elements involved in the creation of your tactical plan.

It’s important to note that each element of your tactical planning process should be analyzed using SWOT (Strengths, Weaknesses, Opportunities, Threats) and SMART (Specificity, Measurability, Averaged Resilience to Trends, Time-frame).

This will help you understand both the advantages and disadvantages of each factor while enabling you to make informed decisions about how best to address them.

With this in mind, here are five elements you should look into for your tactical plan:

### Goals

The entire purpose of using tactical planning is to achieve goals. Using the SMART analysis, you can make your goals more achievable as it becomes easier to define.

For example, rather than stating your goal as getting more organic visits through content as it’s too vague, you can instead state your goal as publishing 5 articles each week that generate 500+ social shares and 2,000+ organic visits.

### Tactics

Tactics are methods that can be applied in tactical planning to make achieving corporate objectives possible. There’s no defined deadline for completion when it comes to tactics, but they do help move things along in the direction of the goal.

Using the example of publishing 5 articles a week, the tactics involved would include:

*   Research the latest trends in online marketing to find inspiration for topic articles and ranking keywords
*   Write targeted, relevant topics using the right keywords
*   Use engaging writing style

### Actions

Taking action to realize each short term goal set is next. This is where specific steps are outlined to help you achieve the end result.

Continuing on with the article example, this means listing out the popular topics and best keywords you can use to achieve the goal. This could also include hiring competent writers who are capable of [](https://onesearchpro.my/copywriting-malaysia/)**[copywriting](https://onesearchpro.my/copywriting-malaysia/)** in a more engaged style.

### Resources

Achieving goals not only requires planning and action, but resources as well. Materials, labor force, equipment, finances, outsourcing products, and other resources may be required for a successful tactical plan.

This also includes using available systems online to find relevant keywords, or even using Google Drive to back up written content.

### Timeline

Timing is crucial for tactical planning as the goal is to achieve it as soon as possible. Everything needs to be completed in the correct order, so it’s good to state the actionable steps needed to get there. Without this, a significant amount of time and additional money will be lost in the process.

So if your strategic planning strategy for your content is that it must be achieved in the following year, the tactical plan needs to achieve this in a shorter time.

In the case of our example, you may want to aim to achieve this in the next 6 months instead. Setting a timetable or deadline will help you move forward and achieve your objectives or get a positive result.

### 2\. Clearly Define the Goal

Don’t be vague with the goals of your tactical plan. Make sure the goals are clear so that all teams involved know what they need to do.

When identifying strategies needed, it’s also important for managers to consider the output and effort of the team members to avoid setting up unrealistic expectations.

### 3\. Assign The Action Accordingly

All team members involved should be assigned the actions accordingly so they know what needs to be done to achieve the stated objectives. This makes using the resources available more effective, while preventing any possible confusion and distraction that could lead to further delays.

### 4\. Ensure Actions Are Combined With Objectives

Assigning the action needs to also be tied to goals, as this will make it more likely for team members to achieve them. ### 6\. Stay Flexible

Tactical planning is all about being flexible. This can be reassuring for teams as the stress and intense pressure to perform actions and hit objectives can hurt the company’s overall performance.

It’s important to remember that every business is different, and things can change in the long run. So having a flexible tactical plan keeps you open to alternative options.

### 7\. Prepare a Plan B

Because of its flexibility, this means having a Plan B is essential. Nothing ever goes as planned after all, and a Plan B helps you avoid plenty of dangers and uncertainty, keeping your team from feeling demoralized should things turn for the worse.

Additionally, this minimizes the negative outcome and keeps the business on course, which is why it’s good to have a plan for emergencies to help cope with the potential loss.

### 8\. Determine Who and What Are Involved

Always get the people involved in the tactical plan. This not only helps you in identifying the SWOT analysis but also in designing the technique in accordance with the department’s needs.

This is why it’s important to delegate the tasks to those who are best suited to complete them.

Access to the proper training, production platforms, and tools and resources is also helpful so get those involved as well. This can help produce the required outcomes, potentially offering excellent financial returns for the firm.
