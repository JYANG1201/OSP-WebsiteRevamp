Local search rankings occur when a user includes a local keyword phrase in their search engine and concludes a location from the user’s device.

An example of a local search phrase would be “car wash services in Ampang, Kuala Lumpur.” These can be business to consumer type with a physical location, or service area businesses that run throughout a specific geographic area.

As a whole, Search Engine Optimization for local businesses helps improve online visibility as many local customers use **local search engines** and directories to search for a local firm.

And 50 percent of searchers visit businesses within 24-hours of doing a local search.
