## **Top 20 Tik Tok Songs That is Most Well Known!**

### **1\. Laxed (Siren Beat) by Jawsh 685**

Laxed was originally a tune created by Jawsh 685, a teen producer from New Zealand.

This short yet catchy tune was accompanied by a simple dance that everyone could do, and soon everyone wanted to dance to it too. 

Later on, Jawsh 685’s was contacted by American singer Jason Derulo, who created lyrics for the tune and released it as ‘Savage Love’. 

In total, this song has been featured by 54.7 million videos. 

Source: [Viral Crunch](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_cKQIxT9pqA)

### **2\. What You Know Bout Love – Pop Smoke**

Deceased rapper Pop Smoke’s song ‘What You Know Bout Love’ is also a dance challenge, It has been featured by about 11 million videos on TikTok songs compilation.

The dance allows room for TikTok users to make the moves their own and express themselves, thereby making it even more popular as a challenge.

The song has famously been danced to by TikTok influencers like Addison Rae, Noah Beck and Dixie D’Amelio.

UK rapper Millie B’s record M to the B may not have made it to public knowledge, if it wasn’t for TikTok.

Currently, M to the B is used in about 7.5 million videos.
