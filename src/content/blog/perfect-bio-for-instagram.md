Your Instagram bio is the first thing people read on your profile. In 150 characters it has to explain who you are and why someone should follow you.

Below are **60 Instagram bio ideas you can copy**, grouped by type, followed by a business bio template and 8 tips to write your own.

## What makes a good Instagram bio?

**A good Instagram bio clearly says who you are, who it's for, adds a bit of personality, and ends with one call to action – all within 150 characters.**

- **Clear identity:** what you do or what you post about.
- **Audience:** who it's for (e.g. "for busy mums", "in Klang Valley").
- **Personality:** one emoji or phrase that sounds like your brand.
- **Proof:** an award, a number of clients or a well-known partner, if you have one.
- **Call to action:** what to do next – "Shop ↓", "DM to order", "Book a free audit ↓".
- **Keyword in the Name field:** the Name field (not the bio text) is searchable on Instagram.

## What is a good Instagram bio template for businesses?

**Use this formula: what you do + who it's for + proof + call to action.**

| Part | What to write | Example |
|---|---|---|
| What you do | Your product or service in plain words | Digital marketing |
| Who it's for | Your audience or location | for Malaysian SMEs |
| Proof | Clients, years, awards | 10+ years · 200+ clients |
| Call to action | One next step with an arrow to your link | Get a free proposal ↓ |

Fill-in-the-blanks templates:

- [What you do] for [who] 📍[City] | [Proof] | [CTA] ↓
- [Product] that [benefit] ✨ [Delivery area] | Shop ↓
- [Topic] tips every [day] 💡 | Free guide ↓

## How do you write the perfect Instagram bio? 8 tips

**Write for the person landing on your profile for the first time: tell them what you offer, why it matters and what to do next, in short lines with a clear call to action.**

### 1. Add a branded hashtag

A branded hashtag in your bio encourages followers to tag their own posts. London's tourism account, for example, puts #thisislondon in its bio.

![London's Instagram bio with a branded hashtag](/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/word-image-53.jpeg)

### 2. Use line breaks and emojis to make it easy to read

Short lines and relevant emojis make a bio easier to scan. Only use emojis that relate to your brand – a bakery can use 🥐, but random emojis look messy.

### 3. Include one clear call to action

Tell people exactly what to do: shop, book, DM or download. One call to action works better than three.

![An Instagram bio focused on an online store link](/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/word-image-54.jpeg)

### 4. Use your bio links

Instagram lets you add **up to five links** in your bio (Edit profile → Links). Link-in-bio tools like [Linktree](https://linktr.ee/) are still useful if you need more links or a branded landing page.

### 5. Add key business details

Add your location, opening hours if relevant, and enable contact buttons (call, email, directions) on a business or creator account so they don't use bio space.

### 6. Show a current offer

Promote a current offer – "15% off this week" or "Raya hampers now open" – and remove it as soon as it ends. Shops can also enable an Instagram shop so a **View shop** button appears on the profile.

![Zoo Negara's Instagram bio with a promotional link](/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/word-image-56.jpeg)

### 7. Add a tagline

A short tagline or mission statement shows your personality and helps you stand out from competitors.

![Starbucks' Instagram bio with a tagline](/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/word-image-57.jpeg)

### 8. Mention well-known clients or achievements

A short line of social proof – "200+ clients" or "as seen on Astro" – builds trust quickly.

## What should an Instagram business profile include?

**Besides the bio, a strong business profile has a searchable name, a clear profile photo, the right category, links, action buttons and contact details.**

| Element | Why it matters | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Username and Name field | Helps people find and remember you | Put your main keyword in the Name field |
| Profile photo | Recognisable at a glance | Use your logo; upload at least 320 × 320 px |
| Category | Shows what you do under your name | Choose the closest match |
| Bio links | Sends traffic to your site or shop | Up to five links |
| Action buttons | Lets people book, order or call | Enable on a business account |
| Contact options | Makes it easy to reach you | Add email, phone and address |
