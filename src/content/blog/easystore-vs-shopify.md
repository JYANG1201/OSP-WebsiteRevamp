### Security Features

There’s a lot of client data involved in online retail shopping. Clients may have to log in using emails and passwords, and when it comes to payments, they may have to provide their card details.

This is sensitive information and has to be protected from scammers, hackers, and phishers. EasyStore and Shopify have varying security features as detailed below.

#### Shopify

Shopify has a robust security framework that ensures any online store set up on this platform is assured of data protection. It takes PCI compliance seriously.

You get SSL certificates that increase trust in your store by boosting security, and that’s very important.

Clients are very particular with their information, and any sign that your business is compromised will have them running in the opposite direction.

#### EasyStore

EasyStore has serious data protection protocols, and it has SSL certificates that link the browser and the server, making it hard for hackers to intercept and hijack sensitive information anytime payments or EasyStore login attempts are made.

Features like Let’s Encrypt ensure your site is trusted enough for clients to use their bank information to make purchases.

##### Verdict

Both platforms are secured as far as the basic needs of an e-commerce website are concerned, but Shopify has stronger security features and support, which may be because it’s bigger and is used on a bigger scale globally.

## Final Verdict

So who takes the crown in the EasyStore vs. Shopify debate?

The answer is not clear-cut because each platform is designed for specific niches, and they excel in their own way.

Go with **Shopify marketplace** if:

1.  You plan to sell internationally and would like to get global courier companies that operate in most countries on board.
2.  You want the flexibility and convenience of using limitless themes and apps for customizing your store.
3.  You have a preference for Oberlo in case you need dropshipping services for clients outside Malaysia.
4.  You want access to vast libraries of learning materials that allow you to get more out of your online store.

Go with **Easy Store Malaysia** if:

1.  You aim to act as a seller to local customers in Malaysia and Southeast Asia.
2.  You are on a budget and want to start small at first without breaking the bank.
3.  You want locally accepted payment gateways that are a little more native.
4.  You are looking for local support that you can turn to every time you have a problem.

It is clear that EasyStore vs Shopify depends on various factors but you should be able to make the right decision now based on the information we have provided.
