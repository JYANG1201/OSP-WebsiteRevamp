When we talk about websites, there’s a lot of terms regarding them that many may not take note of. One of these terms is none other than **URL**.

URL stands for _‘Uniform Resource Locator’_, and just like a home address, it functions as the official address of your website.

Have you ever wondered _“How can I make people remember my website address better”_ or _“How can I make them click my links”_?

The thing with these website addresses is that if your URL is too long or complicated, it won’t leave much of an impression.

That’s why **vanity URLs** exist.

**What is a vanity URL**? This is the question we will be answering in this post. Read on as we cover all related aspects pertaining to vanity links, and more in the coming sections!

## What is a Vanity URL?

Very simply, a **vanity URL** is a shorter, more catchy web address.

It is often a shorter and more concise version of the original URL for your page. It offers many benefits in terms of branding and marketing efforts and also tracking.

Related: [](/branding-vs-marketing/)**[Differences Between Branding VS Marketing](/branding-vs-marketing/)**

With vanity links, you have the freedom to choose and customize your own web addresses according to your creativity and preference.

To put it in context, let’s check out some **vanity URL examples** as follows.

### Vanity URL VS Vanity Domain Name

A vanity URL is different from something called a **vanity domain** name. Previously, we looked at a **vanity URL meaning.**

Unlike URLs, a domain name is the main website’s address, which is just the front of the URL.

It can too, be customized and is usually named after a certain individual – usually the owner of the business website.

Vanity domains are usually adopted by solopreneurs rather than companies. These individuals use their own names as a brand, and those who use them include lawyers, motivational coaches, politicians, social media influencers, consultants, and the like.

## Why Should You Use a Vanity URL?

When you pick a URL, you want to make things easier for the public in more ways than one. It goes without saying that this is for users to easily look up your business when they want to.

Related: [](/search-google-or-type-a-url/)**[Should You Search Google or Type a URL?](/search-google-or-type-a-url/)**

Vanity URLs are a type of **link customization** that can benefit both you and the users that will interact with your online presence. Here are some of those benefits:

### More Memorable

When someone wants to search for your brand using the search bar, a **vanity website** address is much easier to remember than long-winded ones.

The main branding objective you want to achieve is to let people remember you better, so keeping a **shorten URL** will make your brand name ‘stick’ in people’s minds a lot easier.

Many businesses with a good **vanity link** will also insert one or two words describing what their company sells or provides. So instead of just saying Spades.com, you might want to add SpadesBurgers.com, so people know you’re a gourmet burger shop.

![Shortened Link Used by Spade's Burger on Instagram | Vanity URL | One Search Pro Digital Marketing](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/6.png)

_Source: Spade’s Burger_

### Easier Sharing and Usage

One of the main things you want users to do that can really help with your digital marketing is that you want them to share your URL link with others.

This helps to grow your brand organically, especially among your [](/social-media-target-audience/)**[target audience](/social-media-target-audience/)** and niche market.

Users can share your links better if you provide them with a vanity URL that is short and sweet.

Imagine if they’re sharing a link that’s a wall of text on their chat apps like WhatsApp. The individuals they’re communicating to may lose interest in it completely thinking it’s some sort of spam link.

Some users might also be interested to embed your URL in their posts on social media. Having a vanity link will allow the post to look better and much nicer.

### Better for Offline Marketing

Using a **link shortener** will also be useful if your URL is being featured in offline ads and print materials such as newspaper ads, brochures, or leaflets. This way, no one has to type in complicated symbols, numbers, or a long list of alphabets.

With offline marketing, there is no way for anyone to copy and paste your link – neither can they click it easily. Therefore, you have to provide a link that can be typed fast and efficiently. Apart from printed materials, a vanity URL is also very useful for audio and visual marketing such as TV and radio ads. They can be mentioned by someone easily and be heard by consumers who can then replicate what they hear in the browser search engine bar.

## How Do I Make a Vanity URL?

How to create a vanity URL? There are basically two main approaches on **how to create a vanity URL**. The first is on a CMS (Content Management System) plugin or control panel and the second is via a URL shortener.

### Step 1: Designing a Vanity URL

Before you install your vanity URL, you have to first design that URL in the most optimal way possible.

There are 3 parts to a good and concise URL: the domain, TLD, and URL slug.

Take for example the address _“OneSearchPro.my/blog”_. Let’s break this down to see what each portion means and how you can pick the best ones.

![The Three Parts of a Good Vanity URL | Vanity URL | One Search Pro Digital Marketing](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/8.jpg)

_Source: Rebrandly_

**Domain (OneSearchPro)**: This is your brand name or company name. It is important to tie it back to your brand as your domain will set the tone and standard for the entire website, as well as be the basis for all your social media URLs.  
  
**TLD (.my):** The TLD stands for _Top Level Domain_ and is basically the domain endings. Some of the most common TLDs you can choose include .com, .my, .live and .news, and so on. If you operate in a certain country, it helps to have a TLD that represents the country’s name in order to build trust.

**URL slug(/blog) :** The URL slug represents portions of your site, and it often includes keywords that will increase its chances of appearing in search engine results. You can use various [](/keyword-research-tools-seo/)**[keyword research tools](/keyword-research-tools-seo/)** to help you determine the most searched terms that you can incorporate into your vanity URLs.

### Step 2: Publishing Your Vanity URL

This has to be done from the backend of your website’s programming. There are two main methods we can go about doing this depending on what platform your website is hosted.

#### CMS Plugin or Control Panel

If you build your website on a CMS like WordPress, Joomla, Drupal, or Magento, there will be plugins where you can customize the URL of each page that you’re publishing.

On CMS platforms like WordPress, you have plug-ins like [](https://wordpress.org/plugins/redirection/)[**Redirection**](https://wordpress.org/plugins/redirection/), whereby you can enter a source URL and then enter a new vanity URL in the _Target URL_ section.

Another plugin that’s useful is [](https://wordpress.org/plugins/custom-permalinks/)[**Custom Permalinks**](https://wordpress.org/plugins/custom-permalinks/), which allows you to customize the URLs for posts, pages, tags, and categories on your website.

## How Do Vanity URLs Affect SEO?

Vanity URLs are actually an integral part of the entire [](/seo/)**[Search Engine Optimization](/seo/)** (SEO) strategy for your site.

That said, in order to help the overall concept, they have to be utilized in the correct manner.

### Varying Vanity URLs to Avoid Competition

In SEO lingo, competition between two closely related or similar keywords is known as keyword cannibalization.

Self-cannibalization is whereby having the same keywords for different pages on your website cause them to compete with each other for a position on SERPs (Search Engine Results Page).

As your page is already competing with other websites, it helps to minimize any competition with itself. Therefore, each part of your site should have clearly distinct URLs.

### Don’t Underestimate the Importance of TLDs

When choosing a TLD, make sure that it is relevant to your brand and nature of business.

If you’re functioning mainly in one country, you can consider a geo-targeted TLD. For example, [](/)[**One Search Pro Digital Marketing**](/) provides services mainly to the Malaysian market; therefore, it would be a wise choice to adopt the local TLD of .my.

### Ensure All your URLs Are Functioning

Shorter branded links are always preferable to longer branded links because they also ensure that there are fewer broken links around.

Make sure that you’re using the correct 301 redirects when making multiple vanity URLs for the same page as this redirect is the one that is most suitable and efficient for search engine tracking. It’s also important so that your link juice isn’t spread too much or interrupted.

## Basic But Best Practices When Designing a Vanity URL

It’s not enough just knowing **what’s a URL** that is unique. When considering **what does a URL look like**, there are some things you can do to make your vanity URL really stand out.

Here are some of the things to keep in mind when you’re picking that custom URL.

*   **Make It Short and Catchy –** Your vanity URL should not be too long as some platforms may reject it. Instead, focus on making it creative and catchy. For example, you may use puns or an alternative spelling of the word.
*   **Avoid Special Symbols –** While having hyphens and the occasional underscore is acceptable, you should try your best to avoid too many other symbols that make your vanity URL hard to read. These include question marks, long series of numbers, dollar signs, and the like.
*   **Capitalize Each Word –** Capitalization makes words easier to read at a glance, and you can use all caps if you want to highlight certain words.
*   **Know When Not to Use Vanity URLs –** There are certain cases when vanity URLs aren’t needed. This includes cases when you already have a branded link that’s short and understandable enough. Also, you should know that some mailing services will send your email to spam if they detect a redirect URL in its content.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**1\. What Are Vanity Redirects?**

Vanity redirects are shorter and more memorable versions of an original website URL. Both send visitors to the same page.

**2\. What Makes A Good Vanity URL?**

A good vanity URL should be clear and concise with no special characters or long numbers.

**3\. How Long Should A Vanity URL Be?**

There’s no hard and fast rule, but keeping it as short as possible is definitely the end goal. Ideally, stick to a two to three-word phrase.

**4\. Can You Redirect A Vanity URL?**

Yes, you can definitely do that either manually or using plug-ins provided for your CMS platform.

**5\. When Not To Use A Vanity URL?**

A vanity URL should not be used with difficult abbreviations, added dashes, or strange abbreviations. You should also avoid adding the words ‘www’ or ‘dotcom’ to the vanity link’s name.
