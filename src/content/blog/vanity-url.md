Long web addresses full of numbers and symbols are hard to remember and look untrustworthy. A vanity URL fixes that. This guide explains what a vanity URL is, with examples, how to create one and how it affects SEO.

## What is a vanity URL?

**A vanity URL is a short, branded web address that's easy to read and remember – for example yourbrand.com/raya instead of a long page address – and it usually redirects to a longer page.** Marketers use vanity URLs in ads, print, social media bios and anywhere people need to type or remember a link.

For example, a long page address like this:

![A long, complicated web page address](/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/Screenshot-2023-10-02-095545.jpg)

can become a short, branded vanity URL such as **onesearchpro.my/blog**. The vanity URL sends visitors to the original page using a *redirect*.

## What is the difference between a vanity URL and a vanity domain?

**A vanity URL is a short, memorable link (often a path on your domain or a branded short link), while a vanity domain is an entire domain name built around a personal or brand name.**

| | Vanity URL | Vanity domain |
|---|---|---|
| What it is | A short, branded link to one page | A whole domain name |
| Example | yourbrand.com/sale, go.yourbrand.com/raya | imkarenkho.com |
| Typical users | Brands running campaigns | Influencers, coaches, consultants, solopreneurs |
| How it works | Redirects to a longer page | Hosts the full website |

## What are some vanity URL examples?

**Vanity URLs appear on websites and on social media profiles, where you can claim a custom username so your profile link is short and branded.**

| Platform | Vanity URL format |
|---|---|
| Website | yourbrand.com/promo |
| Branded short link | go.yourbrand.com/raya |
| Facebook | facebook.com/yourbrand |
| Instagram | instagram.com/yourbrand |
| X (Twitter) | x.com/yourbrand |
| LinkedIn | linkedin.com/company/yourbrand |
| TikTok | tiktok.com/@yourbrand |

Claim your social media vanity URLs early – popular names get taken quickly.

## Why should you use a vanity URL?

**Vanity URLs are easier to remember, easier to share, better for offline ads, easier to track and look more trustworthy than long links.**

- **More memorable:** people can recall yourbrand.com/sale after seeing it once. Adding a descriptive word helps – *SpadesBurgers* tells people you sell burgers.
- **Easier to share:** short links look cleaner in WhatsApp, social posts and emails, and less like spam.
- **Better for offline marketing:** billboards, flyers, radio and TV need links people can type or remember quickly.
- **Easier to track:** use a different vanity URL for each campaign or channel and compare clicks in your analytics.
- **More trust:** a link with your brand name looks safer than a random string of characters.

## How do you create a vanity URL?

**Choose a short, branded address, then point it to the target page with a 301 redirect – either on your own website or through a branded link shortener.**

1. **Design the URL.** Use your brand name, a relevant domain ending (TLD) and a short slug, e.g. *onesearchpro.my/blog*.
2. **Pick a method:** a redirect on your own website, or a branded short-link service.
3. **Set up a 301 redirect** from the vanity URL to the target page.
4. **Test it** on mobile and desktop.
5. **Track it** with UTM parameters or your shortener's click stats.

| Part | Example | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Domain | OneSearchPro | Use your brand or company name |
| TLD | .my | A country TLD like .my builds local trust |
| Slug | /blog | Short, readable, one to three words |

![The three parts of a vanity URL: domain, TLD and slug](/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/8.jpg)

### Option 1: Redirects on your own website

On WordPress, plugins such as [Redirection](https://wordpress.org/plugins/redirection/) let you enter the vanity URL as the source and the long page as the target. Other platforms (Shopify, Wix, or a hosting control panel) have their own redirect settings.

### Option 2: A branded link shortener

Services like [Bitly](https://bitly.com/), [Rebrandly](https://www.rebrandly.com/) and [TinyURL](https://tinyurl.com/app) create short links. Their paid plans let you use your own domain (e.g. *go.yourbrand.com*), which makes the link a true vanity URL.

## How do vanity URLs affect SEO?

**Vanity URLs don't hurt SEO when they use a single 301 redirect to one relevant page; problems come from redirect chains, broken links and several URLs competing for the same keyword.**

- **Use 301 redirects** so search engines pass link value to the target page.
- **Avoid redirect chains** – point the vanity URL straight to the final page.
- **Keep URLs distinct** so your own pages don't compete for the same keyword (keyword cannibalisation).
- **Choose a relevant TLD** – a .my domain signals a Malaysian business.
- **Check links regularly** so old vanity URLs don't break.

## What are the best practices for vanity URLs?

- **Keep it short and catchy** – brand plus one to three words.
- **Avoid special characters** – no question marks, dollar signs or long numbers; a hyphen is fine.
- **Capitalise only the domain in print** (e.g. OneSearchPro.my/raya) – domains aren't case-sensitive, but the part after the slash can be, so keep it lowercase.
- **Know when to skip it** – if the original link is already short and clear, you don't need a vanity URL.
