## **Creately’s Core Features**

What are Creately’s strongest assets?

After tinkering around with it, we’ve actually found Creately’s core features to be super helpful and it’s clear as day the developers know what users are looking for.

Here are the juicy features you can expect from Creately:

### **State of The Art Diagramming**

![State of the art diagramming | Creately Review | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/image.png)

_A wide variety of templates for users to bring their processes to life._

We always have this problem of having an idea but cannot expand on it. It’s essentially a “we don’t know where to start” problem.

Here’s where Creately’s impressive suite of diagramming tools shines.

With approximately 70 types of diagramming standards and over 1000 specially designed shapes and connectors, it can support rapid, efficient diagramming. The variety here is tremendous.

While we’ve messed around with the software, we do hope that Creately will periodically add more templates because we can see from their mock-ups, that they truly have an innovative bone.

### **Seamless Visual Collaboration**

A collaborative online environment has been on the rise ever since COVID-19.

Through the pandemic, remote teams have spread across different locations and Creately’s accommodative features for remote work are stellar.

We absolutely adore its quality-of-life features like permission settings, a robust commenting system, and real-time activity tracking through visible cursors.

On paper, this all might seem a little “extra” for software, but quality-of-life features like these ensure smooth and effective collaboration.

### **Visual Project Management**

When it comes to visualising complex ideas, Creately also excels.

Intricate concepts are always present in corporate environments. Turning them digestible and allowing users to personalise their projects makes these ideas less of a headache.

This creative flexibility means each project becomes uniquely its own, enhancing both understanding and presentation.

### **Impeccable Task Management**

Managing daily tasks has never been easier. Creately’s “My Tasks” view helps organize tasks with deadlines with clarity and simplicity.

Imagine having a massive number of pending tasks flooded on your screen—that’s a no-no. 

Having the front face of your task management software cluttered with information is nauseating.

We love how users are allowed to immediately get what they want with just a simple search instead of navigating through a large number of clicks in this software.

## **The Good And The Not-So-Good**

![The Pros and Cons of Creately | Creately Review | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/visual-3.jpg)

Earlier, we were extremely objective with the core features of Creately. Now, we want to talk more about how we personally feel using the software.

Do note that this is a little more subjective as this is from our perspective. If you are looking for perfect accuracy, you’ll ultimately have to use the software yourself.

**What we loved:**

1.  **Minimalistic Design and Very Approachable**Creately’s minimalist approach makes planning comfortable despite its complexity. It was pleasing to our eyes. The clean, visual-centric interface allowed us to manage a lot of information without feeling overwhelmed. Its simplicity and seamless usability stand out.  
    
2.  **Multipurpose and Has a Bit of Everything**Maybe having functionalities could be overwhelming for some but we’ve found them to be great to use. Whether for task management, data organization, or campaign planning, the platform proves versatile enough for our scale of operations.  
    
3.  **Professional Templates Are Readily Available**The availability of professional templates is a significant advantage. Sometimes when we’re stuck in a rut, these templates can be sources of inspiration, kickstarting creativity and streamlining the planning process.  
    
4.  **Awesome Compatibility**Creately is compatible with most modern operating systems, including Windows and macOS—our office uses both of them. Plus, their web and mobile options are extremely seamless, ensuring almost no issues with navigation. Having various co-workers trying out the software, they really appreciate this smooth operational experience.  
    

**A Few Minor Setbacks:**

1.  **Cancelling Can Be a Hassle  
    **We did this as an experiment because we have come across many users experiencing this issue, and down the road, we might need to cancel as well. It’s true, cancelling the service can be cumbersome as it requires emailing. This process is quite time-consuming, which might not be convenient for users wanting a quicker cancellation method.  
    
2.  **Requires Paid Version For Full Experience**  
    The first time around, we tried using the unpaid version of the software because we didn’t know what we were jumping into. There, quite a number of features are locked behind a paywall, necessitating a subscription for full access. After using the paid version, we noticed the difference between the two versions is pretty jarring.  
    
3.  **Does Not Have A Dedicated App**  
    Perhaps we’re a little nit-picky here. As tech lovers, we were lowkey bummed out due to the absence of a dedicated app for Creately. Don’t get us wrong, the software works amazingly on browsers, but sometimes, we just have so many browser tabs open, that it gets inconvenient to have an extra one.

## **Security And Privacy**

When it comes to security and privacy, Creately impresses with a robust infrastructure. All user information is encrypted, protecting data from unauthorized access.

This ensures that sensitive information remains secure while using the platform. This is extremely crucial for professionals handling confidential data.

Creately also performs backups every 24 hours. This regular schedule means that even if something goes wrong, user data can be quickly restored.

We cannot stress how important this is. This significantly minimises potential data loss and downtime. Knowing that our work is continually safeguarded gives us peace of mind.

Furthermore, their privacy policies are clear and transparent. Creately does a commendable job of outlining how user data is handled, stored, and protected. 

For businesses and individual users alike, it’s reassuring to know that our information is respected and secure on the platform.
