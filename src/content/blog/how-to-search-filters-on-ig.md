## How To Search for Filter on Instagram

Instagram filters can breathe life into your photos and videos, adding that extra oomph to your content.

Whether you’re looking to use the **best filter in Instagram**, explore new artistic possibilities with popular filters or even just looking at **how to browse effect on Instagram**, finding filters is easier than you might think.

Here’s what you need to do to find and apply filters:

**Step 1: Open Instagram.** Start by launching Instagram on your mobile. Ensure you’re logged into your account to access the full range of features.

**Step 2: Navigate to Your Story.** Tap on your profile picture in the top left corner of the home feed or swipe right from your feed to access the camera. This will allow you to create a new story or post.

![Instagram Stories and Filters | How to Search Filters on IG | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/word-image-43724-2.jpeg)

**Step 3: Browse Filters.** Once you’re in the camera mode, you’ll notice a carousel of filters at the bottom of the screen. Swipe through these filters to preview and select the ones you like. Instagram frequently updates this selection with new options, so explore the variety.

![Browsing Filters on Instagram Stories | How to Search Filters on IG | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/word-image-43724-3.jpeg)

**Step 4: Explore More Filters.** If you can’t find what you’re looking for in the initial **filter Instagram** selection, click on the _“Browse Effects”_ button at the end of the filter carousel. This will take you to the Instagram Effect Gallery, which showcases a wide array of filters and effects created by different artists.

**Step 5: Search for Filters.** At the top of the Effect Gallery, you’ll find a magnifying glass icon. Tap on this to get to the search bar. Here, you can type in the **Instagram filter names** or keywords related to the type of filter you’re interested in. Whether it’s _“vintage”_, _“glitter”_, _“retro,”_ or any other keyword, Instagram will display filters that match your search criteria.

**Step 6: Try Filters.** Once you find a filter that piques your interest, tap on it to preview how it will look on your camera feed. If you like what you see, click the _“Try It”_ button to add the filter to your camera for immediate use.

![Trying Out Filter on Instagram Stories | How to Search Filters on IG | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/word-image-43724-4.jpeg)

**Step 7: Save and Use Filters.** After trying the filter, you’ll have the option to save it to your favorites for easy access in the future. To do this, click the save button (a bookmark icon). Now, the filter will be readily available in your Instagram camera carousel.

## How To Find Instagram Filters by Creator

While Instagram’s selection of filters is impressive, sometimes you may be on the lookout for filters created by specific artists or creators.

These filters can offer a unique and personal touch to your content. Here’s how you can find Instagram filters by a specific creator:

**Step 1: Open Instagram and Access the Camera.** Launch the Instagram app and go to the camera mode by tapping your profile picture in the top left corner or swiping right from your feed.

**Step 2: Choose a Filter.** Swipe through the available filters at the bottom of the screen or click on _“Browse Effects”_ to access the Effect Gallery.

![Manually Searching for an Instagram Filter | How to Search Filters on IG | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/word-image-43724-5.jpeg)

**Step 3: Browse by Creator.** In the Effect Gallery, you’ll see a magnifying glass icon that will lead to the search bar. Enter the creator’s name or username in the search bar. Instagram will display filters created by that specific creator.

![Preview and Use Filter on Stories | How to Search Filters on IG | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/word-image-43724-6.jpeg)

**Step 4: Preview and Use Filters.** Click on the creator’s name or profile picture to see all the filters and the **IG filters name** they’ve designed.

You can preview each filter by tapping on it. If you like a particular filter, tap on it to add it to your camera for immediate use.

**Step 5: Save to Favorites.** To keep a creator’s filter for future use, click the save button (bookmark icon) next to the filter. It will then be stored in your favorites for quick access.

## How to Get Instagram Filters From Friends and Mutuals

One of the charms of Instagram is its sense of community and the opportunity to share creative tools with friends and mutuals.

If you’ve come across a filter that a friend or someone you follow is using and you’d like to try it for yourself, here’s how you can easily get Instagram filters from friends and mutuals.

### Ask Them Directly

The simplest way to obtain filters from friends and mutuals is to ask them directly.

If you see a filter in their story or post that catches your eye, send them a direct message (DM) and inquire about the filter’s name or where they found it.

Most users are happy to share their favorite filters and will often send you a direct link to the filter for easy access.

### Browse Their Effects

If you’re following someone who creates their own Instagram filters, you can explore their effects directly from their profile.

Visit their profile, tap on the smiley face icon (effects tab) under their bio, and you’ll find a collection of filters they’ve designed.

You can preview and add them to your camera for immediate use.

### Try Filters from Shared Stories

![Using "Try It" to Try Filters from Shared Stories | How to Search Filters on IG | One Search Pro Marketing](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/word-image-43724-7.jpeg)

When someone shares a story with a filter, there’s a _“Try It”_ option at the top left of the screen. Click on this button to add the filter to your camera immediately.

### Check Instagram Stories’ “Effect Gallery”

Instagram also curates filters from friends and mutuals in the _“Effect Gallery”_ of your camera. You can find these **Instagram story filters** by tapping on your profile picture or swiping right from your feed, then scrolling through the options.
