## So, How Much Does SEO Cost in Malaysia?

Before we dive into the details of SEO services pricing Malaysia, we have to understand that there are many factors influencing the pricing.

One of the key factors affecting SEO costing is how large and established the consultants or agencies you approach are.

Generally, SEO services in Malaysia cost anywhere from RM 800 to RM 12,000.

Therefore, before you embark on any SEO project, it is important to determine exactly what you need and what you can afford.

## The Types of SEO Pricing in Malaysia

As with many service industries, SEO services Malaysia can be priced using different formats. The types of SEO service cost format you decide on will depend largely on what your brand needs and how many projects you are pursuing.

There are three main types of SEO service providers in Malaysia, namely agencies, consultants , and freelancers.

### By Hourly Rate

The per-hour SEO pricing in Malaysia concept is fairly straightforward. You will be charged by the number of hours it takes to audit your site and to come up with SEO keywords for the various pages on your site or the different content.

Be sure to request an estimate of the hours involved before agreeing to the SEO agency, freelancer or SEO consultant’s hourly rate.

Large agencies charge around RM 440 – RM 540 per hour, professional consultants largely charge RM330 – RM440 per hour SEO pricing whilst freelancers charge RM180 – RM 220 per hour.

Any simple standalone project with the most basic SEO task will typically require at least 2-3 hours of work, and sometimes more.

![Working Hourly Rate for SEO Services | SEO Price Malaysia | One Search Pro](https://onesearchpro.my/wp-content/uploads/2021/12/The-hourly-rate-is-the-amount-of-money-received-for-each-hour-spent-working.jpg)

The hourly rate is the amount of money received for each hour spent working. Source: Upwork

### Monthly Retainer Rates

Retainer fees are paid to SEO service providers like a monthly salary. This will translate into SEO monthly costs for your company.

Just like a full-time employee, you’ll be paying the providers a set amount no matter the amount of work that needs to be done.

This type of SEO pricing system works if you have a lot of continuous work that needs to be done. It will save you a lot in the long term as the [_average SEO cost per month_ will work out to be more affordable than, say, project-based costing.](https://app.ahrefs.com/keywords-explorer/google/us/overview?keyword=average%20seo%20cost%20per%20month)

[

Typically, monthly retainer fees for SEO services in Malaysia range from RM 3000 to RM 10,000 per month, with some freelancers charging as low as RM 2000, especially if they’re new in the industry.

](https://app.ahrefs.com/keywords-explorer/google/us/overview?keyword=average%20seo%20cost%20per%20month)

### [](https://app.ahrefs.com/keywords-explorer/google/us/overview?keyword=average%20seo%20cost%20per%20month)Quotations by Project

Should the previous two SEO management pricing systems not satisfy you, there’s another way services providers charge, which is on a project basis.

The process involves you communicating your needs to the SEO experts, who then provides you a quote for the price of their SEO services. You can then both negotiate the pricing and reach an agreement before the work commences.

This pricing concept is pretty popular because it avoids misunderstandings and lays out the amount of work and its corresponding costs very clearly before the project starts. However, it’s not suitable if you have continuous SEO work coming in and require their long-term SEO services.

SEO project prices typically range from RM 800 by freelancers to RM 2500 if you’re hiring an agency.

### By Packages

These days, to make it easier for their clients, many Search Engine Optimization agencies, consultants, and freelancers offer SEO package deals. These packages are set by the service provider and can be purchased as is or customized according to the client’s needs.

An SEO package deal is perfect if you’re new to the world of SEO and would like to try the services of SEO experts for starters. These packages are very simple and straightforward.

For example, an SEO pricing package may include an SEO audit, optimized targeted keywords for one website, and keywords for an additional 10 pages for RM 500- RM 1000 by a freelancer.

Packages like these typically run up to RM 2500 if you approach a consultant, and can run up to RM 5000 if you’re contracting an agency.

## What Really Affects the Cost of SEO in Malaysia?

If you’re unfamiliar with how much does SEO optimization costs in Malaysia, a good place to start would be to understand what affects the price of search engine optimization for your business.

### Level of Expertise

The more experienced and the longer your search engine optimization service provider has been in the industry, the higher their rates.

Although there is no definitive SEO price guide in the industry, we can safely estimate that a fresh graduate providing SEO services Malaysia will charge a lot less than an established agency that has been around for more than 5 years.

One Search Pro is an SEO service provider with more than 10 years of experience in SEO – including local, on-page, off-page, and technical SEO. Source: One Search Pro

### Their Location

You can expect that seeking SEO services in larger cities like Kuala Lumpur, Georgetown, Ipoh, and Johor Bahru will definitely fetch a higher price than a provider stationed in a smaller town or suburb.

How much is SEO in these different areas boils down to operating costs. Agencies usually have higher running costs in larger cities where rental is higher.

### Your Website’s Condition

SEO price Malaysia is also very heavily affected by how much work needs to be done on your website. If it is a simple case of updating a previously existing template that is complete and just needs a facelift, the costs might be lower.

However, if you need more backend work with various parts that need optimization and re-programming from the ground up, this will take more time and cost more too.

### The Amount of Work

Costs will also be determined by the size of your website of websites that need to be optimized. This means that each page has to be customized to fit Google SEO Malaysia and appear high on Search Engine Results Pages (SERPs).

Additionally, you should also determine if you want to optimize your site for general SEO, [](https://onesearchpro.my/local-seo/)**[local SEO](https://onesearchpro.my/local-seo/)**, technical SEO, [](https://onesearchpro.my/mobile-seo/)**[mobile SEO](https://onesearchpro.my/mobile-seo/)**, or even [](https://onesearchpro.my/youtube-seo/)**[Youtube SEO](https://onesearchpro.my/youtube-seo/)**.

If your website needs to be targeted more specifically, this will require more time and more SEO efforts. Therefore, it usually costs more if you have a very specific demographic you need to target.

## How Can You Compare SEO Prices?

One of the first things you should do is to research or get quotes from the various SEO agencies or freelancers you’re interested in. Then, compare their prices with your budget.

You should also be prepared to negotiate with the agency or freelancer that fits the closest to your needs. Here are some tips when choosing an SEO partner:

*   Cheaper doesn’t always mean better. You get your money’s worth so if you invest more, you’ll probably see more fruits than that in cheap SEO services.
*   Avoid providers who promise too much in a short time. SEO takes time to work and is not guaranteed 100% in terms of immediate results.
*   Make sure your SEO provider uses ethical methods and avoids blackhat SEO which can earn you penalties from Google.
