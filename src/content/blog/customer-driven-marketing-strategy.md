A customer-driven marketing strategy refers to a marketing strategy that keeps your customers squarely in the middle of everything you do.

From the early stages right through the end, it’s the only strategy you implement for your business.

_That means you understand as much as possible who your customers are, what they want, and their preferences._

You know all about the demographics and interests of your target market, and you take the opportunity to understand them.

As reasonable as it may sound, customer-driven marketing is not the same as customer service. Your main objective is to satisfy your customers and provide exactly what they need, being as flexible as you could for them.

_So, in today’s world, which [](/digital-marketing-strategy/)[**digital marketing strategy**](/digital-marketing-strategy/) is more reliable and sufficient?_

The product-centric or customer-driven strategy? Many marketing experts will argue that there are vast distinctions between these two strategies.

In this article, the focus is on the consumer.

So is it all about how you make your customers happy? What can your business do to offer the solution to their problem from arising from a simple customer feedback?

But how do you meet and exceed your customer expectations?

## **What is Customer-Driven Marketing Strategy?**

![Word of Mouth Marketing | Customer-Driven Marketing Strategy | One Search Pro](/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/word-image-10.png)

_A customer-driven marketing strategy means changing focus from the product to the user and basing your marketing strategy and plans on customers’ needs. Source:_ [_Reve Chat_](https://www.revechat.com/)

A customer-focused marketing strategy satisfies customers’ needs uniquely and helps businesses improve **[Return On Investment](https://josephmuciraexclusives.com/how-return-on-investment-roi-is-calculated/)** (ROI). By recognizing and targeting consumers with a higher customer lifetime value, companies can boost relationships by offering solutions to serve their needs.

Customer-centric businesses ensure that the customer is at the center of a business’s philosophy, operations, or ideas.

A defined driven marketing strategy is the activities of the business, which as its key objective is to meet the customer expectations and needs. This strategy should be concise with the exact guidelines, sales and revenue goals of the brand – by implementing educated assumptions.

According to the [](https://www.iedunote.com/marketing-process)[**five-step model of the marketing process**](https://www.iedunote.com/marketing-process), the first step in marketing is understanding the marketplace and customer needs and wants. As it relates to customers, the aim of marketing is to sense and understand the customer so well that the product or service fits them and sells itself.

Being customer-driven means all your sales, marketing, or support strategy must be organized and planned accordingly.

There are times when a process changes from the products to achieving consumer needs, which results in cultivating customer loyalty and helping expand the customer base (namely new customer acquisition).

You prove that you practice what you advocate. If your brand relates to your audience, they will willingly connect with you and share it with others. A value-based strategy can help you understand your [](/social-media-target-audience/)[**target audience**](/social-media-target-audience/) and keep your followers loyal in the long run.

## **How to Successfully Build a Customer-Driven Marketing Strategy?**

The ability to understand your customers is essential to ensure the success of a consumer-based strategy. The customer-first approach exceeds the duties of your marketing team. Both your sales and marketing customer service team need to accommodate outstanding customer service and experiences.

Let us go further and elaborate more on creating a customer-driven marketing strategy for your brand.

### **Step 1: Identify Your Niche** **and Create** **Detailed Buyer Personas**

The first major step of a marketing strategy is to identify your niche. Of course, if you choose the market-driven strategy approach, your marketing strategy starts well before the product is produced.

You should have full information about your customers. This will let you segment and target customers for your marketing plans. This can be for email marketing campaigns or PPC ads, and your success relies on how extensively you manage to segment your customers by related groups and provide them with what they need.

So, what are the four steps to designing a customer-driven marketing strategy (or rather, buyer personas)?

![Market Segmentation | Customer-Driven Marketing Strategy | One Search Pro](/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/word-image-13.png)

_Market segmentation gives better matching of customer needs and enhanced profits for businesses. Source:_ [_Baccus Tech_](https://www.baccus.tech/)

*   Market Segmentation – A plan is used to divide a target audience into smaller and controllable categories. A personalized consumer creates more loyal customer, improve brand recognition and increase company’s profit.
*   Market Targeting – is a process of choosing exactly your target market from the whole market. It consists of a group/groups of buyers to whom the company wants to provide or for whom a product is created, price is set, promotion or marketing efforts are catered to, and distribution network is prepared.
*   Differentiation – this strategy is a business approach created by offering customers something unique and clear from items their competitors may provide in the market. The key objective of implementing a differentiation strategy is to improve competitive advantage.
*   Positioning – this refers to the ability to influence customer’s insight. It lets a company achieve predominant margins regarding a brand or product relative to competitors. The goal of this strategy is to establish the image or identity of a brand based on customer perceptions.

![Market Targeting | Customer-Driven Marketing Strategy | One Search Pro](/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/word-image-8.jpeg)

_With targeting, marketing becomes more affordable, efficient, and effective at generating customer leads. Source:_ [_MediaTel_](https://mediatel.co.uk/)

You can also approach your buyer persona by:

*   Doing market research on your existing customers by organizing surveys and interviews and collecting their personal information like their email address.
*   Study your competitors. Learn about their case studies and find out who’s using their product or services and what is their objective.

### **Step 2: Know What They Want**

Knowing and understanding what your customers need is vital for every business owner. Whether you’re selling directly to individuals or other businesses, once you know their demand, you can use the knowledge to influence new prospects and existing customers that buying from you is in their best interest.

The more you know about your consumers, the more effective your sales and marketing endeavor will be. So it’s well worth finding out the 5 W’s and 1 H:

*   Who they are
*   What they buy
*   Why they buy it
*   When they buy
*   What they think about you
*   How much are they willing to spend

But the question is, how do you get to know all this customer data? Fortunately, there are various easy ways to research and study your customer.

**Speak to your customer.** One of the best and cost-effective ways to gather information about your customer is to ask them. Get a group from your best customers that are willing to provide valuable customer feedback.

Ask things like how they know about your business and what makes them choose you to solve their problem or what is their buying journey. This is one of the most commonly utilized tactics in **[zero-cost marketing](/zero-cost-marketing/)**.

You can use all the answers to create a written or video testimonial and add it to your blog posts, webcasts, whitepapers, or other [](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/developing-a-strategy/)[**content marketing strategies**](https://contentmarketinginstitute.com/developing-a-strategy/).

**Formal Research.** Formal customer insight market research should comprehend the main obstacles and issues encountered by your potential customers. In addition, just like all marketing research, it should address how information about your business and the buying stage is retrieved.

**Keyword Research.** Understanding the keywords used by your customers is crucial as it lets you know what they think at the exact moment they have a need.

### **Step 3: Building Product Strategy to Meet Product-Market Fit**

If you were given a choice, which one would you choose? Create a product and find the audience that suits best, or find your customers and develop a product based on the market data you have gathered?

Opting for the second option is more advantageous as the latter is full of risks. Businesses should design their products and services based on their consumer’s needs instead of creating a product that might not be the best for their audience.

Essential tips for designing a successful customer-based marketing strategy:

![Brand Positioning Map | Customer-Driven Marketing Strategy | One Search Pro](/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/word-image-14.png)

_Example of a brand positioning map._  
_Source:_ [_Trade Marketing Agency_](https://wrightobara.com/)

**Design a positioning map** – create a positioning map according to customer insights of your brand based on your competitors’ products or services.

**Highlight your value proposition** – fully understand customer needs and address how your products or services serve their needs. Pushing your products or services without a value proposition will not produce any significant results.

**Select competitive advantage** – develop a product or service that differentiates your business from your competitors.

### **Step 4: Creating an Omnichannel Customer Engagement Plan**

Being omnichannel helps to gain valuable insights into customer interactions and their behavior during the buying process. As a result, businesses and brands with secure omnichannel customer strategies retain customers more than those who only focus on a single channel.

Omnichannel customer engagement allows your customers to connect with your brand through their selected channels by putting together an easy adjustment in the same interaction. It helps improve fulfillment and boost the lifetime value of your customers.

The right tools that provide real-time assistance, for example, live chat or visual tools like video chat for order inquiries or automate your customer support tasks with chatbots to provide 24×7 support for customers with common inquiries.

### **Step 5: Investing in Quality Customer Service**

Customer service is the support that you provide for your customers before and after they purchase from you. Delivering fantastic customer services is vital if you want to retain customers and grow your business, as customers often remember the kind of services you provided for them.

Conversely, if your business’s customer service is poor, you will jeopardize your business reputation and lose customers no matter how good your product is.

A customer service-oriented culture is all about focusing on customer’s requirements and needs, valuing them over anything else, and responding to them as soon as possible in an efficient manner. This should not involve your support system alone as it should be implemented throughout your business to build a better brand reputation.

One of the critical elements of customer service is always addressing your customer’s concerns no matter what industry you’re in, and it’s essential to handle those concerns appropriately.

Some of the most important [](https://tallyfy.com/customer-value/#:~:text=of%20customer%20value.-,What%20is%20Customer%20Value,services%20over%20what%20was%20paid.)[**customer service values**](https://tallyfy.com/customer-value/#:~:text=of%20customer%20value.-,What%20is%20Customer%20Value,services%20over%20what%20was%20paid.) are optimism, accountability, authenticity, trust, respect, and customer communication. These characteristics will help your business earn the loyalty and trust of your consumers. In addition, people prefer brands that have an image of what they believe. Therefore, having regard to positive values is vital for business as well as good deeds.

## **How to Measure the Success of Your Customer-Driven Marketing Strategy?**

One of the benefits of utilizing a targeted marketing strategy is that a company may be able to have more precise marketing campaigns and have a more accurate understanding of how customers respond to product and service offers.

Below are the five elements of marketing strategies that are customer-driven and how to create customer value:

**_Element #1_**

### **Targeting Your Market**

Use marketing research to recognize typical demographic characteristics within your customer base, such as age, gender, occupation, and income level. The more you know about your target customer, the easier it will be to create a strategy that appeals to these features.

For example, targeting by topic is a good strategy if your client wants to drive sales on their website or reach a specific audience.

_**Element #2**_

### **Meeting Customer Needs**

A value-driven marketing strategy focuses on fulfilling your customers’ needs and studying how your product or services serve their needs. Having good knowledge of customer needs and wants helps add practical value and level up the overall brand recognition.

In addition, customer satisfaction level provides your business a competitive benefit so you can stay a step ahead in the market!

_Customer happiness helps you measure loyalty, reduce churn and increase revenue. Gif Source_ [_Tenor_](https://tenor.com/)

**_Element #3_**

### **Building Customer Loyalty**

Customer-driven marketing helps build your customer loyalty which can result in repeat sales and cross-reference. One technique practiced by many marketers is by implementing a [](https://www.orientation.agency/insights/how-to-generate-brand-loyalty-and-reward-loyal-customers)[**rewards program**](https://www.orientation.agency/insights/how-to-generate-brand-loyalty-and-reward-loyal-customers) that allows customers to receive points each time they make a purchase.

At the end of the day, having and implementing a marketing strategy driven by consumers not only make your consumers but also your brand happy when you gain a solid customer base.

**_Element #4_**

### **Using Customer Feedback**

The success of a brand lies in consistent and positive customer satisfaction and experience. The most successful technique to provide positive customer service is simply by asking for customer feedback from your supporters.

Find out what they like about your products and how you can improve your services through customer feedback – they may end up being the decisive factor in overturning a business!

**_Element #5_**

### **Gaining Customer Referrals** **Through Word of Mouth Virality**

Generating customer referrals is part of a consumer-driven marketing strategy. First, motivate your existing customers to spread the word about your business to get new customers. Then, exercising a referral program where customers are rewarded with benefits like discounts helps gain customer referrals in no time.

So now that you know how to measure your customer-focused strategy, you need to decide which targeting strategy focuses best on common consumer needs, and tailor-make your business approach to cater to those strategies.

Once target segments are identified, your strategies should involve using a single marketing strategy to reach all customers. The benefit you get from a single marketing strategy is that your business is free to allocate all of its resources to attracting a single and defined type of audience with a specific need. This helps you fulfill customer needs better than your competitors.
