Your Instagram grid is the first thing people see when they open your profile. A planned grid makes your brand easy to recognise and gives visitors a reason to follow.

Below are 12 Instagram grid ideas, each with a simple layout diagram, who it suits and how to do it – updated for Instagram's taller 3:4 profile grid.

## What is an Instagram grid layout?

**An Instagram grid layout is the visual pattern your posts create together on your profile page – for example alternating photos and quotes, a colour per row, or one large image split across nine posts.** Instagram shows posts three across, so most layouts are planned in sets of three.

## Why should you plan your Instagram grid?

**A planned grid makes your profile look consistent, shows what your brand offers at a glance, and makes it easier to decide what to post next.**

- **It looks professional:** a curated grid gives a stronger first impression than random posts.
- **It promotes your products clearly:** visitors quickly understand what you sell or do.
- **It makes posting easier:** you know in advance what type of post comes next.
- **It wins follows:** you have a few seconds to convince a profile visitor to follow.

## What changed with Instagram's 3:4 profile grid?

**Since early 2025, Instagram's profile grid shows taller rectangular 3:4 thumbnails instead of squares.** Instagram's head, Adam Mosseri, said the change was made because most photos and videos uploaded today are vertical. ([Inquirer](https://usa.inquirer.net/165044/bye-squares-why-instagram-switched-to-a-taller-grid), [Kapwing](https://www.kapwing.com/resources/instagrams-new-grid-layout-size-and-dimensions-2025/))

| | Old grid | Current grid |
|---|---|---|
| Thumbnail shape | Square (1:1) | Tall rectangle (3:4) |
| Best post size | 1080 × 1080 px | 1080 × 1350 px (4:5) |
| Square posts | Shown in full | Cropped at the sides |
| Puzzle grids | Planned as 1:1 squares | Must be planned as 3:4 tiles |

Instagram has since added an option to reorder posts on your profile grid ([fuzz.my](https://fuzz.my/20260610212041175)), so fixing a broken pattern is easier than before.

## What makes a great Instagram grid layout?

**A great grid uses high-quality images, a consistent editing style and a fixed colour palette, and follows a planned pattern.**

- **High-quality images:** sharp, well-lit photos – natural light works better than indoor lighting.
- **Consistency:** stick to one style; if each post is different, plan the order so the grid still flows.
- **One editing style:** use the same preset or filter on every post.
- **A colour palette:** choose one or two brand colours and repeat them.
- **Simple backgrounds:** remove clutter so the main subject stands out.
- **A content mix:** plan which types of posts (photos, quotes, products) you publish each week.

## 12 Instagram grid ideas with layout examples

### 1. One post at a time (consistent palette)

**Best for:** Beginners and busy brands.

![Instagram grid diagram: every tile is a single photo in the same warm colour palette](/images/blog/instagram-grid/01-one-at-a-time.png)

**How to do it:**

1. Pick 2–3 brand colours and one editing preset.
2. Post one strong photo at a time.
3. Check the profile view before posting so nothing clashes.

![Real example: a minimal, consistent single-post feed](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/03.jpg)

_Square layout is the most easy to maintain. Source:_ [_The Wave Shave Come_](https://www.instagram.com/thewaveshavecome/)

### 2. Checkerboard

**Best for:** Brands that mix photos with quotes, tips or offers.

![Checkerboard Instagram grid diagram alternating photos and quote posts](/images/blog/instagram-grid/02-checkerboard.png)

**How to do it:**

1. Alternate two post types: photo, then text/quote, then photo.
2. Keep the text posts in one background colour.
3. Always post in the same order so the pattern never breaks.

![Real example: a checkerboard of photos and quotes](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/04.jpg)

_Use checkerboard layout by posting a photo, then a quote, followed by a photo and another quote. Source:_ [_Boss Babe_](http://www.instagram.com/bossbabe.inc)

### 3. Centre column (vertical line)

**Best for:** Coaches, educators and quote-led accounts.

![Instagram grid diagram with quote posts down the centre column](/images/blog/instagram-grid/03-centre-column.png)

**How to do it:**

1. Post in sets of three.
2. Make the middle post of every row a quote or text post.
3. Use the same background for every centre post.

![Real example: quotes running down the centre column](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/05.jpg)

_The key element of vertical lines feed is on the center often filled with quotes. Source:_ [_Birch House_](https://www.instagram.com/birch.house.lettering/?hl=en)

### 4. Diagonal grid

**Best for:** Accounts that post one text post for every two photos.

![Instagram grid diagram with quote tiles running diagonally](/images/blog/instagram-grid/04-diagonal.png)

**How to do it:**

1. Post in a repeating 3-post cycle (text, photo, photo).
2. Keep the diagonal tiles in one style.
3. Plan at least nine posts ahead in a grid planner.

![Real example: tips and quotes arranged diagonally](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/06.jpg)

_Diagonal grid often features images with the same visual aesthetics. Source:_ [_Human Doing Life_](https://www.instagram.com/humandoinglife/)

### 5. Puzzle grid

**Best for:** Launches, campaigns and big announcements.

![Puzzle Instagram grid diagram: one large image split across nine 3:4 tiles](/images/blog/instagram-grid/05-puzzle.png)

**How to do it:**

1. Design one large image sized for a 3×3 grid of 3:4 tiles.
2. Split it into nine pieces with a grid planner.
3. Post from the bottom-right piece to the top-left, and make each tile still make sense on its own.

![Real example: one image split across the grid](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/07.jpg)

_Puzzle layout can be quite challenging to maintain, but with proper planning it can turn out to be a nice feed. Source:_ [_The Jana Bishop_](https://www.instagram.com/thejanabishop/)

### 6. Mixed borders

**Best for:** Photographers and lifestyle brands.

![Instagram grid diagram alternating framed photos and full-bleed photos](/images/blog/instagram-grid/06-mixed-borders.png)

**How to do it:**

1. Add a white frame to every second post.
2. Keep the frame width the same each time.
3. Use full-bleed images for your strongest shots.

![Real example: alternating borders](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/08.jpg)

_Mixed feed creates an interesting story and personality. Source:_ [_Forrest Mankins_](https://www.instagram.com/forrestmankins/?hl=en)

### 7. White borders on every post

**Best for:** Product brands with many different colours.

![Instagram grid diagram where every photo has a white border](/images/blog/instagram-grid/07-white-borders.png)

**How to do it:**

1. Add the same white border to every image.
2. Centre the product inside the frame.
3. Use one editing preset so colours stay balanced.

![Real example: white borders tying different products together](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/09.jpg)

_White border layout keeps the feed neat and bright. Source:_ [_Lady Austin_](https://www.instagram.com/lady.austin/?hl=en)

### 8. Full portrait posts (made for the 3:4 grid)

**Best for:** Every brand since the 2025 grid change.

![Instagram grid diagram of tall 3:4 portrait posts filling each tile](/images/blog/instagram-grid/08-portrait-3x4.png)

**How to do it:**

1. Shoot and design in portrait (4:5, 1080×1350 px).
2. Keep key text and faces in the centre 3:4 area.
3. Avoid square designs, which get cropped on the profile grid.

![Real example: tall photos with plenty of white space](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/10.jpg)

Rectangular photos provide large white spaces and make your feed look clean and spacious. Source: [Yuka Studio](https://www.instagram.com/yukastudio/?hl=en)

### 9. Two-colour palette

**Best for:** Brands with strong brand colours.

![Instagram grid diagram alternating two brand colours](/images/blog/instagram-grid/09-colour-palette.png)

**How to do it:**

1. Choose two core brand colours.
2. Make every post feature at least one of them.
3. Use the colours in backgrounds, props or text.

![Real example: a seasonal colour palette](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/11.jpg)

_Color scheme layout is fun when you add vibrant colors that pop-out. Source:_ [_Primark_](https://www.instagram.com/primark/?hl=en)

### 10. Colour blocking by row

**Best for:** Fashion, food and seasonal campaigns.

![Instagram grid diagram with each row in a different solid colour](/images/blog/instagram-grid/10-colour-blocking.png)

**How to do it:**

1. Give each row of three posts one dominant colour.
2. Change colour with each campaign or season.
3. Plan three posts at a time.

![Real example: bright colour blocking](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/12.jpg)

_Filters and color blocking brings your Instagram feed to another level of fun. Source:_ [_Aww Sam_](https://www.instagram.com/aww.sam/)

### 11. White and negative space

**Best for:** Minimal, premium and skincare brands.

![Instagram grid diagram of small subjects on plain white backgrounds](/images/blog/instagram-grid/11-negative-space.png)

**How to do it:**

1. Shoot products on plain white or light backgrounds.
2. Leave lots of empty space around the subject.
3. Keep lighting and angle the same in every post.

![Real example: calm, airy negative space](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/13.jpg)

_White and negative space gives a unique style and calmness to your feed. Source:_ [_The Frank Zoo_](https://www.instagram.com/thefrankzoo/?hl=en)

### 12. Row by row

**Best for:** Brands that post in sets of three (series, carousels, campaigns).

![Instagram grid diagram where each row of three is one theme: photos, a text row, then photos](/images/blog/instagram-grid/12-row-by-row.png)

**How to do it:**

1. Post in sets of three that share one theme.
2. Alternate photo rows with text or announcement rows.
3. Never post a single post on its own, or the rows shift.

## What are the best free tools to plan an Instagram grid?

**Planoly, Preview, UNUM and Plann let you drag posts into order and preview your grid before posting; VSCO is a free photo editor for keeping a consistent look.** Free plans change often, so check each app's current limits.

| Tool | What it helps with | Platforms |
|---|---|---|
| [Planoly](https://www.planoly.com/) | Visual grid planner, scheduling, image splitting | iOS, Android, web |
| [Preview](https://thepreviewapp.com/) | Drag-and-drop feed preview, presets, team planning | iOS, Android |
| [UNUM](https://www.unum.la/) | Grid preview, templates, basic edits | iOS, Android |
| [Plann](https://www.plannthat.com/) | Grid planning, scheduling, colour palettes | iOS, Android, web |
| [VSCO](https://vsco.co/) | Photo editing presets for a consistent look | iOS, Android |

### Planoly

Planoly is a visual planner and scheduler. To split one image into several grid posts: tap **+**, choose the image, tap the grid icon, pick the split format, tap **Split**, check the preview, then upload.

![Planoly Instagram planner app](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/14.jpg)

### Preview

Preview lets you upload upcoming posts, swap their order with a tap, edit with built-in presets, and plan the feed with teammates.

![Preview Instagram planner app](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/15.jpg)

### UNUM

UNUM offers drag-and-drop grid previews, templates and basic editing, so you can see how a new post fits before you publish.

![UNUM Instagram planner app](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/16.jpg)

### Plann

Plann combines grid planning and scheduling with colour-coded placeholders, which helps when you're planning a colour-blocked or row-by-row grid.

![Plann Instagram planner app](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/17.jpg)

### VSCO

VSCO is a photo and video editor with presets and tools such as saturation, grain and contrast – useful for keeping every post in the same style.

![VSCO Instagram planner app](/images/blog/best-instagram-grid-ideas/18.jpg)

## How do you keep your Instagram grid consistent?

**Choose one layout, one colour palette and one editing style, and plan at least nine posts ahead in a grid planner before you publish.** Changing layouts every few weeks is the fastest way to make a feed look messy.
