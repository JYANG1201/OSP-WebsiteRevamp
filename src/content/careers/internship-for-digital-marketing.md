# Internship For Digital Marketing

## | RESPONSIBILITIES

*   Help the team with operational matters.
*   Brainstorm idea and do copywriting for post content.
*   To curate, manage and publish blog posts/content on different platforms.
*   Leverage on Social Media & SEO best practices including content planning, digital marketing strategy, keyword research, page-building, rank tracking and more.
*   Stay up to date on industry trends, including the best practices on multiple channels.
*   Manage & keep the company social account active by posting regularly in various channels (social profile, forums and more).
*   Do research and come out the proposal / ppt for our potential client
*   Accept all other duties as assigned by Superiors/Directors that are related to digital marketing job scope.

## | REQUIREMENTS

*   Candidate must currently pursuing a Diploma or Bachelor’s Degree in any related course such as Marketing, Advertising, Journalism, Mass Communication, etc.
*   Good command of English & Mandarin in both verbal and written.
*   Active in & like to browse social media.
*   Prior experience with blog management especially in WordPress is a plus.
*   Experience in content marketing with basic SEO knowledge will be an added advantage.
*   Strong written and verbal skills with attention to detail and a creative voice.
*   Flexible and effective team player with a positive can-do attitude.
*   Ability to work in a fast-paced and dynamic environment as a team player.
*   Fast learner who is not afraid to change approaches quickly and provide constructive ideas.
*   Able to start immediately would be preferred.

## | BENEFITS

*   Opportunities – above training and guidance, you will have the opportunity to try, to build your confidence and become your best self
*   Fun & dynamic working environment
*   Friendly colleague
*   Near public transport (MRT/LRT)
*   Work life balance (**Friday WFH**)
