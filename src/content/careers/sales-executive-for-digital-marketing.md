# FULL-TIME JOB FOR Sales Executive for Digital Marketing

## | Priority Job Character Requirement:

*   Outgoing and Smart looking people
*   Good listener/ talker
*   Eager to learn about all sort of businesses and keep exploring new knowledge
*   Work under pressure and able to meet timeline

## | RESPONSIBILITIES

*   To carry out daily job duties such as performing telemarketing calls, scheduling online & physical appointments, pitching sales presentations as well as following up with clients and data entry of daily activity updates.
*   Assist in preparing marketing proposals, quotations, and client presentations.
*   Build strong relationships with customers.
*   Collaborate with internal teams to plan and propose digital marketing strategies and recommend suitable online advertising budgets for new leads and customers.
*   Immerse in digital marketing trends and brainstorm creatively for strategies with the team.
*   Measure and report performance of leads/ accounts status.
*   Identify new business opportunities and potential clients through online research or referrals.

## | REQUIREMENTS

*   Candidates must possess at least a bachelor’s degree in marketing-related courses.
*   Good command of **English & Mandarin** in both written and verbal. We are seeking candidates proficient in both English and Mandarin to effectively communicate with our diverse client base and stakeholders.
*   Flexible and effective team player with a positive can-do attitude.
*   Active in & like to browse social media.
*   Ability to work in a fast-paced and dynamic environment as a team player.
*   **Fresh Graduate are welcome to apply**
*   **2 Full-Time (Junior / Senior) Position available**
