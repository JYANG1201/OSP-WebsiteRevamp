# Brand Brief

## Positioning
- **Business:** One Search Pro (OSP) — a full-service digital marketing agency offering SEO, Digital Strategy, and Creative services.
- **Ideal Customer:** Broad — local/SMB businesses without in-house marketing expertise, growing mid-market companies who need more than one-off tactics, and e-commerce/DTC brands focused on organic growth. All share the same core need: SEO-led growth without juggling multiple vendors.
- **Primary CTA:** "Book a Free Consultation" → Contact form page.
- **Secondary CTA:** "Get a Free SEO Audit" (SEO service page) → Contact form page.
- **Differentiator:** Full-service team — SEO, Digital Strategy, and Creative under one roof — backed by an AI-driven SEO methodology. Positioned against agencies that only offer isolated tactics or outsource pieces of the work.
- **Positioning Statement:** One Search Pro is the full-service growth partner for businesses who are tired of stitching together SEO, strategy, and creative from separate vendors. One integrated team, one AI-driven methodology, measurable results.

## Brand Voice & Tone
- **Personality:** Bold, results-driven, confident.
- **Tone:** Direct and energetic — speaks in outcomes, not jargon.
- **We are:** Data-driven, integrated, fast-moving, proof-obsessed.
- **We are NOT:** Corporate/stiff, vague about results, a single-tactic vendor.

## Visual Identity

### Color Constraints
- **Anchor colors:** Black + orange (required across every direction), in the spirit of the reference screenshot at `OSP-WebsiteRevamp/References/Screenshot 2026-08-04 120644.png` — radial orange/red glow on black, chevron/arrow motif. Screenshot's text content is not used, only its color/lighting/motif language.
- **Avoid:** None specified.

### Font Constraints
- **Must-use font:** Poppins, for both headings and body, across every direction.

> Final palette and fonts are selected per concept direction during brainstorm — see `directions.json`.

### Visual Style
- **Keywords:** Dynamic, bold, animation-forward, energetic, premium.
- **Inspiration patterns:** Black background with radial orange/red glow lighting and a chevron/arrow motif (from reference screenshot).
- **Do:** Commit to strong black+orange contrast, use motion/animation as a core part of the design language, use the real client logos on hand for social proof.
- **Don't:** Corporate-flat layouts, soft pastel palettes, static/lifeless sections.
